HARTFORD ATHLETIC CONTINUE BID TO REMAIN KINGS OF THE USL CUP

Hartford Athletic took a 2-1 victory against Colorado Springs Switchbacks FC on Tuesday night in the Semifinals of the Prinx Tires USL Cup to advance to the Final on Sunday, Oct. 4 at 3 p.m. ET on ESPN2, extending the club’s undefeated streak in the competition to 13 games.

Hartford has yet to lose in regulation in the competition across the past two seasons and has won all five of its knockout stage contests outright, posting a 10-0-3 record overall. The streak remains the longest across the USL Cup’s three editions as it prepares to host Louisville City FC, itself on an eight-game undefeated streak dating to last year’s tournament, at Trinity Health Stadium with a chance at a second consecutive crown.

LOUDOUN UNITED’S EDDY DAVIS 10TH-YOUNGEST TO RECORD HAT TRICK IN CHAMPIONSHIP

Loudoun United FC's Eddy Daniels III became the 10th-youngest player to record a hat trick in the USL Championship's history last Friday night at Segra Field. | Photo courtesy Patrick Garber / Loudoun United FC

Loudoun United FC’s Eddy Davis III recorded the second hat trick in club history on Friday night against Colorado Springs Switchbacks FC. In turn, the Philadelphia Union loanee became the 10th-youngest player to record a hat trick in the USL Championship’s regular season aged 20 years, 79 days old.

The youngest player to record a hat trick in the Championship is current Chivas de Guadalajara and Mexico international Efraín Álvarez, who bagged the first three-goal game of his career aged 15 years, 324 days old against Saint Louis FC on May 9, 2018.

LOUISVILLE CITY’S SEAN TOTSCH EQUALS RECORD FOR MOST PENALTIES WITHOUT MISS

Louisville City FC defender Sean Totsch has scored 14 of 14 penalty kicks in the USL Championship regular season over his career. | Photo courtesy Matt Schlotzhauer / Indy Eleven

Louisville City FC defender Sean Totsch recorded his 31st regular season goal in the USL Championship on Saturday night against Rhode Island FC with his 14th goal from the penalty spot. It equaled the mark previously achieved by former Richmond Kickers and Colorado Springs Switchbacks FC midfielder Luke Vercollone, who went 14 of 14 from the spot in the regular season during the USL Championship era, the most makes without a penalty miss in league history.

Totsch and Vercollone are currently the lone players to have recorded double-digit penalty kick goals in the league’s history without a miss. Current Colorado Springs Switchbacks FC defender Talen Maples is 9 of 9 in his career in the regular season as the closest active player to Totsch’s mark.

TAMPA BAY ROWDIES REACH 150 REGULAR SEASON VICTORIES

The Tampa Bay Rowdies became the seventh club to reach 150 regular season wins in the USL Championship's history with its victory against Brooklyn FC on Saturday night. | Photo courtesy Morgan Tencza / Tampa Bay Rowdies

The Tampa Bay Rowdies became the seventh team to reach 150 regular season victories in the USL Championship this past Saturday with their 2-0 victory against Brooklyn FC, also clinching a place in the 2026 USL Championship Playoffs. The Rowdies reached the mark in their 303rd regular season contest, with the side holding a 150-85-68 mark overall since joining the league in the 2017 season.

Since the Rowdies joined the Championship, they rank second in the Championship in regular season wins to Louisville City FC (174). Tampa Bay has averaged 1.71 points per game in the Championship to date.

Quick Hits

New Mexico United forward Greg Hurst is the second player in club history to record 30 regular season goals in the USL Championship. | Photo courtesy Nicholas Head / New Mexico United

- New Mexico United forward Greg Hurst scored his 30th regular season goal for the club in its 2-1 victory against Las Vegas Lights FC on Saturday night. Hurst is the second player to record at least 30 goals for New Mexico in the USL Championship regular season, trailing only Chris Wehan (35).

- Louisville City FC’s Sean Totsch recorded his 40th regular season goal contribution for the club with his goal against Rhode Island FC on Saturday night. Totsch has recorded 29 goals and 11 assists for LouCity and becomes the sixth player to have recorded at least 40 goal regular season contributions for the club.

- Lexington SC’s Marcus Epps recorded his 250th regular season appearance in the USL Championship in his side’s contest against Sporting Club Jacksonville on Saturday night. The veteran winger is the 24th player to reach the landmark in league history.

- Last Friday night’s contest between Loudoun United FC (20) and Colorado Springs Switchbacks FC (23) was the ninth on record in the USL Championship in which both clubs recorded at least 20 shots, and the first time the feat was accomplished since the 2022 season.

- The Tampa Bay Rowdies’ Charlie Ostrem leads the league with eight assists this season. He would become the first player to record double-digit seasons of assists in both the USL Championship and USL League One should he reach 10 this campaign.

- The Charleston Battery’s 24-game home regular season undefeated streak came to an end in a 2-1 loss to Hartford Athletic last Wednesday night at Patriots Point. The Battery’s streak ranked tied for the seventh-longest in the Championship’s history.

- FC Tulsa failed to record a shot on target in its scoreless draw against San Antonio FC on Saturday night, marking the first time the Scissortails hadn’t recorded a shot on target in the USL Championship since August 31, 2025 against Colorado Springs Switchbacks FC.

- Birmingham Legion FC has dropped 22 points from winning positions this season following its 2-2 draw against Indy Eleven on Saturday night. The Championship’s single-season high for most points dropped from a winning position is held by Las Vegas Lights FC with 28 in the 2018 and 2021 seasons.
