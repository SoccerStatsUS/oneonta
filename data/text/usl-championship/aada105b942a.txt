FORT WAYNE, Ind. – Fort Wayne Football Club announced on Wednesday the club had acquired defender Aleksandar Vukovic on loan from Phoenix Rising FC of the USL Championship for the remainder of the 2026 USL League One season.

The transaction was completed prior to the USL League One roster freeze on Monday, Sept. 7 and is pending league and federation approval.

“I’m really excited to join Fort Wayne FC,” said Vukovic. “It’s an amazing project and I’m grateful for the opportunity to be a part of it. The guys play with a lot of passion and sacrifice, and I think my playing style will fit really well there.”

A native of Nova Pazova, Serbia, Vukovic has played 13 matches across all competitions this season with Rising, recording one goal and one assist.

“Vuko is a commanding presence on the field and excels in the penalty box, both as an attacker and as a defender,” said Fort Wayne FC Head Coach Mike Avery. “His ability on the ball fits with our identity, and he’s hungry to come here during a busy stretch of important matches and help us achieve our goals for the season.”
