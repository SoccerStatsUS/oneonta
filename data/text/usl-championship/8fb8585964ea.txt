The USL Championship, USL League One and Prinx Tires USL Cup will feature throughout the 2026 season on CBS Sports, ESPN, TUDN and a wide range of local television networks, all of which will bring you the action from now until the USL Championship Final on the CBS Television Network in November.

As we go through the campaign, we’re here to provide a one-stop shop for finding out where every game from both leagues is available on national platforms and local networks.

Here’s our guide for Week 28 of the USL Championship and League One regular season, which features the Semifinals of the Prinx Tires USL Cup on Tuesday night and a full slate of action this weekend in the USL Championship and League One, including a trio of games streaming on CBS Sports Golazo Network.

The Semifinals of the 2026 Prinx Tires USL Cup arrive on Tuesday night on ESPN+ as defending titleholders Hartford Athletic plays host to Colorado Springs Switchbacks FC at Trinity Health Stadium at 7 p.m. ET before Louisville City FC visits San Antonio FC at Toyota Field at 8:30 p.m. ET for a place in October’s Final.

The action then shifts to USL League One on Friday night when Athletic Club Boise looks to maintain its push for the postseason when it hosts a resurgent New York Cosmos side at the Stadium at Expo Idaho on CBS Sports Golazo Network at 9 p.m. ET.

The week’s action concludes with a USL Championship doubleheader on CBS Sport Golazo Network on Saturday night, kicking off with an Eastern Conference clash that sees Pittsburgh Riverhounds SC host Loudoun United FC at F.N.B. Stadium at 7 p.m. ET. A key game at the Western Conference playoff line completes the slate as Oakland Roots SC hosts Lexington SC at 10 p.m. ET at the Oakland-Alameda County Coliseum.
