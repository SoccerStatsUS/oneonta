On the latest edition of USL All Access, Devon Kerr and Joe Malfa, filling in for Mike Watts, welcomed Hartford Athletic Head Coach and General Manager Brendan Burke and San Antonio FC midfielder Jorge Hernandez to the show ahead of Tuesday night’s Prinx Tires USL Cup Semifinal matches.

Malfa and Burke also spoke about what the USL Cup means to Hartford’s team and the fanbase as a whole, what goes into scouting future key players for his club, and how Antony Siaha has solidified himself as one of the USL Championship’s top goalkeepers. With Hernandez, Malfa, and the midfielder talked about what he learned in his time spent playing in Belgium, how he has become a veteran presence at only 25-years-old, and the importance of home-field advantage at Toyota Field.
