ALBUQUERQUE, N.M. – New Mexico United announced on Tuesday the club had acquired attacking midfielder Charlie Dennis on loan from Phoenix Rising FC for the remainder of the 2026 USL Championship season. The transaction was completed prior to the USL Championship Roster Freeze on Monday, Sept. 7.

Dennis joins New Mexico having recorded one goal and one assist for Rising this season in the USL Championship. The 30-year-old has recorded 25 goals and 10 assists in 130 regular season appearances in the Championship overall, previously competing for Oakland Roots SC and the Tampa Bay Rowdies.

“I’m delighted to be joining this historic city. I can’t wait to meet everyone.” Dennis said, “I’ve felt this atmosphere many times and I'm looking forward to being on the right side of it.”

“Charlie is someone we have known and monitored for years,” said United Sporting Director Itamar Keinan. “I am excited we had the opportunity to welcome a player of his quality into our family to help us in our final push and into the playoffs.”

Dennis will be available for selection when New Mexico plays host to Indy Eleven on Friday night at Isotopes Park (9:30 p.m. ET | ESPN+).
