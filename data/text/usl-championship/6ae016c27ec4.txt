New Mexico United has recalled midfielder Marlon Vargas from his loan stint at Union Omaha, where he recorded two goals and one assist in eight League One appearances. | Photo courtesy Kayla Watanabe / Union Omaha

ALBUQUERQUE, N.M. – New Mexico United announced on Wednesday the club had recalled Marlon Vargas from his loan to Union Omaha of USL League One.

The transaction was completed prior to the USL Championship Roster Freeze on Monday, Sept. 7th and is pending league and federation approval.

Vargas recorded two goals and one assists across eight appearances in League One for Omaha, including finding the net in his final appearance this past weekend as Los Buhos took a 2-0 victory against the Charlotte Independence.

Previously, the 25-year-old had recorded four goals and two assists in 33 appearances for New Mexico, logging 30 chances created in 1,856 minutes of action. Vargas has recorded eight goals and nine assists in the Championship overall in his career, having previously competed for Tacoma Defiance.
