A dramatic week of action that included numerous late game-changing finishes also included some outstanding strikes throughout the USL Championship campaign. Make sure to cast your vote for the USL Championship Fans' Choice Goal of the Week, presented by SELECT, below. Voting runs through Wednesday, September 9, at midnight ET.

Barry Coffey (HFD) – #CHSvHFD, 9/2/26: The Irish midfielder produced a brilliant stoppage-time header that picked out the bottom-left corner to lift Hartford to victory against the Charleston Battery at Patriots Point.

Emil Jääskeläinen (JAX) – #JAXvLEX, 9/5/26: The Finnish striker created space for himself inside the right side of the penalty area and roofed a shot from a narrow angle into the top corner to start Sporting JAX’s late comeback against Lexington.

Daniel Flores (PHX) – #MBvPHX, 9/5/26: The 23-year-old teed off with an outstanding long-range strike that picked out the bottom-right corner from the left channel to put Rising ahead against Monterey Bay FC.

Ryuga Nakamura (MB) – #MBvPHX, 9/5/26: The Japanese midfielder notched his second goal for Monterey Bay with a blistering first-time strike from almost 30 yards out that arrowed into the bottom-right corner at Cardinale Stadium.
