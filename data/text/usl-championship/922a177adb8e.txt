TULSA, Okla. – FC Tulsa announced on Tuesday it had acquired forward Dylan Borczak on loan from USL League One side Union Omaha for the remainder of the 2026 USL Championship season. The transaction was completed prior to the USL Championship roster freeze on Monday, Sept. 7 and is pending league and federation approval.

“We are excited to welcome Dylan to Tulsa,” said Scissortails Sporting Director and General Manager Caleb Sewell. “In Dylan, we are getting a dynamic attacker that likes to get at players and exploit space. We look forward to Dylan having a successful back end of the season with us.”

Borczak arrives in Oklahoma having previously recorded two goals and four assists in 17 appearances across all competitions for Omaha this season. The 27-year-old has four prior seasons of USL Championship experience in stints with Lexington SC, Memphis 901 FC and Rio Grande Valley FC since 2022. In 78 USL Championship appearances, he has recorded seven goals and two assists with a pair of goals and an assist also coming in U.S. Open Cup play.

Born in Detroit, Borczak began his collegiate career at Schoolcraft College, leading them to an NJCAA national championship in 2017 with five goals and 11 assists in 19 appearances. He then transferred to Oakland University, earning Horizon League Offensive Player of the Year honors in the 2020-21 season while finishing his Grizzly career with 16 goals and 15 assists across three seasons.
