TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Transfer

San Diego FC have transferred defender Andrés Reyes to Colombian top-flight side Atlético Nacional and retain a sell-on percentage, the club announced Saturday.

The 26-year-old Colombian international center back began his career with Atlético Nacional, before embarking on a six-plus-year stint with three MLS teams.

Reyes first came to MLS in 2020 for Inter Miami CF's expansion season. Then, he spent 2021-24 with Red Bull New York and helped them reach the 2024 MLS Cup presented by Audi final.

Via trade, Reyes joined San Diego ahead of their 2025 expansion season. He was limited to one appearance and suffered a season-ending knee injury.

Throughout his MLS tenure, Reyes scored six goals in 112 matches across all competitions.
