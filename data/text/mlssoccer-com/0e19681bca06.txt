Evander is on a record-breaking run of form with FC Cincinnati.

The Brazilian No. 10's 2g/3a performance on Saturday powered the Orange & Blue to a 6-2 home rout of Orlando City. In the process, he <a href="https://x.com/FCCincinnatiPR/status/2058360919084724396" target="_blank">set the club record</a> for most goal contributions in a regular-season match.

Additionally, the two-time Landon Donovan MLS MVP finalist became the <a href="https://x.com/MLS_PR/status/2058364735041458418" target="_blank">ninth player in league history</a> with at least five goal contributions in a regular-season match.

“It’s a nice way to go into the break,” head coach Pat Noonan told reporters after the final whistle at TQL Stadium. “We attacked in a really good way and scored a lot of goals against a really good Orlando team.

“\[Evander\] has taken his game to another level,” Noonan added.

Cincy head into the 2026 FIFA World Cup break with their Evander-led attack firing on all cylinders.

The Orange & Blue have scored at least two goals in each of their past eight games, taking part in three straight goal-fests decided by six tallies or more.

Evander's been at the center of it all, delivering 7g/5a in the club's last five matches.

“When he’s on the ball and in space, everyone comes to life,” said Noonan, who <a href="https://x.com/MLS_PR/status/2058363112634409454" target="_blank">made history</a> on Matchday 15 by equalling Seattle Sounders FC's Brian Schmetzer as the fastest MLS coach to reach 75 regular-season wins.

“You can feel the energy in the stadium, the energy that he provided with movement of the ball, with the 1-v-1 moments where he creates separation.

"And then for me, it’s whether guys are ready to run — are they ready to run to goal? Because he will give them the ball.”

After a slow start to the season amid a 2026 Concacaf Champions Cup run, Cincinnati have a 3W-1L-2D record over their last six games and boast a league-best 36 goals.

Evander, who recently broke the MLS record for most goals scored by a Brazilian-born player, has a club joint-best nine goals along with club-record signing Kévin Denkey.

Once league action resumes in July following the World Cup pause, the Orange & Blue will look to rise in the Eastern Conference standings. They're currently seventh with 20 points (5W-5L-5D record).

“It’s hard to win, it’s hard to score six goals,” Noonan said.

“We have the players to make plays.”
