TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Transfer

Atlanta United have transferred defender Pedro Amador to Israeli Premier League side Hapoel Be’er Sheva for an undisclosed fee, the club announced Thursday.

Amador joined Atlanta in July 2024 from Liga Portugal side Moreirense FC.

The Portuguese left back tallied two goals and nine assists in 53 matches (all competitions) with the Five Stripes.
