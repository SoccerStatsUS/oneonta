The top four clubs from each league-specific table advanced to the Leagues Cup 2026 knockout rounds.

During Phase One, all 18 MLS teams and 18 LIGA MX teams played three games against teams from the opposite league.

The knockout rounds begin with the quarterfinals on August 25-27, building towards the final on September 6.
