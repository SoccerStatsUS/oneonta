Carles Gil put on a clinic to lead the New England Revolution to a 3-1 win at the Columbus Crew on Sunday.

In the process, the 2021 Landon Donovan MLS MVP and five-time MLS All-Star placed himself in some rarefied air.

With a 1g/2a performance, the Revs' captain increased his career total to 152 goal contributions (57g/95a), making him the 20th player in MLS history to reach 150-plus goal contributions.

Gil, who joined New England in 2019, picked up an assist on Matt Polster's 32nd-minute equalizer that canceled out Brais Méndez's opening goal for Columbus.

The Spanish playmaker then slipped a ball in for Dor Turgeman to increase the Revs' lead to 2-1 just before halftime.

Gil put the icing on the cake with a clipped golazo in the 79th minute, his eighth goal of the season.

Gil is tied for 17th on the list of players with 150-plus goal contributions, joining Josef Martínez (Columbus Crew) and Albert Rusnák (Seattle Sounders FC).
