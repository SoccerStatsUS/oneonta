Real Salt Lake have loaned forward Victor Olatunji to Belgian Pro League side SK Beveren through their 2026-27 season, the club announced Friday.

The 26-year-old Nigerian joined RSL in August 2025 from Czech top-flight side Sparta Prague.

He's scored five goals in 28 appearances (all competitions) with RSL.
