The New England Revolution have unveiled their primary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the Independence Day Kit by adidas.

Inspired by patriotic bunting and fireworks that light the skies each summer, the design reflects pride, tradition, and celebration.

The jersey honors the region’s role in America’s founding and celebrates a milestone year when the nation and the world unite through soccer.
