Red Bull New York have unveiled their primary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the Rooted Kit by adidas.

The kit reflects the club’s deep roots in the growth of the game and its unrelenting drive to push it forward.

It captures the spirit of a founding MLS club that continues to shape the sport, both in New York and on the world stage.
