TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Transfer

Red Bull New York have transferred midfielder Gustav Berggren to Polish top-flight side Lech Poznań, the club announced Wednesday.

The 28-year-old former Swedish international returns to Poland after spending one season with Red Bull, where he recorded three assists in 19 appearances.

Berggren began his career in his native Sweden with BK Häcken before joining Raków Częstochowa in Poland.

"We want to thank Gustav for his professionalism and commitment during his time with our club," said head of sport Julian de Guzman. "He has been a great teammate and person to have within our organization, and we wish him and his family nothing but success in the next chapter of his career."

Homegrown midfielder Adri Mehmeti has won the starting job in defensive midfield for RBNY this season, limiting Berggren's minutes.

Currently eighth in the Eastern Conference (25 points), RBNY are seeking a return to the Audi MLS Cup Playoffs in their first year under head coach Michael Bradley.
