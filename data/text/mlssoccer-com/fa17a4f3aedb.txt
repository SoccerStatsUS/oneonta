Toronto FC have unveiled their secondary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the Winter Kit by adidas.

The kit celebrates how the love of the beautiful game persists, even as the thermometer dips.

The white base embodies the rain, sleet, and snow Toronto fans battle through each winter, while the six red lines represent how TFC supporters embody the club's "All for One" motto, despite the elements.
