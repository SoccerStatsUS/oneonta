The Philadelphia Union have unveiled their primary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the 1776 Kit by adidas.

Inspired by the city’s architecture, documents, and icons that shaped the United States, the design captures the grit and pride that define both Philadelphia and the Union.

It celebrates the city where independence began, and soccer’s next chapter continues.
