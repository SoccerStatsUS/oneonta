TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Transfer

Atlanta United have transferred defender Stian Gregersen to Swedish Allsvenskan powerhouse Malmö FF, the club announced Thursday.

The 31-year-old Norwegian international joined Atlanta ahead of the 2024 campaign from French side Bordeaux.

With the Five Stripes, Gregersen tallied 3g/1a in 69 appearances across all competitions.

Gregersen departs after Atlanta reinforced their center-back corps this summer. Paraguay international Júnior Alonso and Chilean international Paulo Díaz were signed and are first choice under head coach Tata Martino.
