Need to know

Club América are Mexico's all-time winningest club, lifting a record 16 league titles.

That success has carried over to the continental stage, with a record seven Concacaf Champions Cup titles alongside Cruz Azul.

Now, as head coach Guillermo Almada settles in, can Club América add a Leagues Cup title to their lavish trophy case?

Phase One

Columbus enjoyed an unbeaten Phase One, defeating Atlas (3-1) and Pachuca (2-1) before drawing Pumas UNAM (1-1) and earning the extra point with a penalty shootout victory.

Summer DP signing Brais Méndez made an immediate impact, recording 2g/1a while adding a goal contribution in each match.

Players to watch

Brais Méndez: The Spanish midfielder and LaLiga alum is readily showing his quality on and off the ball.

Daniel Gazdag: The Hungarian international is in his first full season with the Crew after a distinguished run with the Philadelphia Union.

Steven Moreira: The 2024 MLS Defender of the Year starred in Cape Verde's Cinderella run to the 2026 World Cup knockout stages.

Need to know

Columbus are looking to recapture the Leagues Cup magic that carried them to the 2024 title after defeating LAFC in the final.

This year's run comes amid significant turnover. The Crew transferred Diego Rossi to Monterrey this summer, replacing him with Méndez before recently adding MLS legend Josef Martínez to bolster their attack for the stretch run.

All the while, they're led by interim coach Laurent Courtois after parting ways with Henrik Rydström in mid-May.
