The Rapids saw their three-game win streak snapped on Saturday night with a 3-0 loss at San Diego FC.

Perhaps hosting their biggest rival at home is the perfect setting to bounce back.

Players to watch

Rafael Navarro: Colorado's leading goalscorer with 10g/4a, Navarro has hit double-digit goal tallies in each of his three full MLS seasons.

Paxten Aaronson: Aaronson (4g/6a) paces the Rapids in assists and is enjoying the best MLS season of his career after returning from Europe in 2025.

Loïc Williams: A summer arrival from Granada in Spain, Williams has played every minute of his first six MLS games, including scoring on his debut.

Hamzat Ojediran: Simultaneously a ball-winner and metronome in possession for the Rapids, Ojediran has been a key cog in midfield during his first MLS season.

Need to know

Colorado has been on a tear at Dick's Sporting Goods Park since the FIFA World Cup break, recording four consecutive victories without conceding a single goal.

All this while bidding farewell to 18-year-old Australian center back Lucas Herrington in a club-record transfer to English Premier League side Hull City (reportedly up to $23m) – the largest fee ever paid for an outgoing MLS defender.

That form has seen them climb above RSL in the Western Conference table courtesy of the wins tiebreaker, while tied on 28 points apiece. Current holders of the Rocky Mountain Cup, the Rapids will aim to retain their title with a fifth straight home victory on Saturday.

Morgan Guilavogui: A fellow offseason arrival, the striker has shown his ability to be more than just a goalscorer, leading the team in assists with seven assists.

Need to know

Returning to Dick's Sporting Goods Park after their midweek Leagues Cup exit, Real Salt Lake will be aiming to right the ship by leapfrogging their rivals in the Audi MLS Cup Playoffs race.

They'll have to do so without Zavier Gozo, who recently made the move to UEFA Conference League champions Crystal Palace in the English Premier League via a club-record $15 million transfer fee.

Victors in the club's previous meeting this season, a 2-1 home win, RSL also seek to reclaim the Rocky Mountain Cup title for the first time since 2023 with a win on Saturday. The Claret-and-Cobalt hold a 14-7 advantage all-time in the rivalry.

Colorado have 48% of the market, compared to 28% for arch-rivals RSL. Meanwhile, a draw (25%) is also on the table.
