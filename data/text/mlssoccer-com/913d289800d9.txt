TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Transfer

Houston Dynamo FC have transferred midfielder Ondřej Lingr to Polish top-flight side Korona Kielce, the club announced Friday.

The 27-year-old Czechia international joined Houston in March 2025 as a Designated Player signing from Czech First League club Slavia Prague.

During his Dynamo tenure, Lingr produced 5g/8a in 50 games across all competitions.

Lingr departs with Houston sitting second in the Western Conference with 38 points (12W-7L-2D record).
