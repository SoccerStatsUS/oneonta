Major League Soccer released the following statement after the passing of Inter Miami CF forward Lionel Messi's father, Jorge, at age 68 on Saturday in Argentina.

MLS mourns the passing of Jorge Messi and extends its heartfelt condolences to Lionel Messi, the entire Messi family, and all who loved Jorge. His devotion to his family and support throughout Lionel's remarkable journey will always be remembered. May he rest in peace.
