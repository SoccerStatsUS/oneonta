TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Signing

Minnesota United FC have acquired forward Maurice Malone from Austrian Bundesliga side SK Sturm Graz, the club announced Monday.

The 26-year-old former German youth international is under contract through the 2029-30 season with an option for 2030-31.

Since developing at German Bundesliga side FC Augsburg, Malone has tallied 47g/32a in 192 first-team matches. Sturm Graz, Austria Wien, FC Basel and Wolfsberger AC are among his different stops throughout Europe.

Internationally, Malone previously represented Germany from the U-17 to U-21 levels. He made 17 appearances and scored four goals across his youth call-ups.
