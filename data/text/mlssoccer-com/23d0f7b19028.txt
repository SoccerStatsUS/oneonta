The Leagues Cup 2026 quarterfinal field is set after eight teams advanced from Phase One.

The quarterfinals will be played from Aug. 25-26, with four MLS clubs facing four LIGA MX sides for semifinal spots.

Chicago Fire FC (MLS 1) vs CF Monterrey (LMX 4)

Austin FC (MLS 2) vs. Toluca FC (LMX 3)

Club León (LMX 1) vs. Real Salt Lake (MLS 4)

Club América (LMX 2) vs. Columbus Crew (MLS 3)

There's plenty of recent history between Club América and the Columbus Crew, who met in the 2024 Campeones Cup and saw Las Águilas prevail on penalties. Columbus are pursuing a second Leagues Cup title in three years, after winning the tournament in 2024, and have Brais Méndez leading the way against ex-LAFC star Brian Rodríguez.

Phase One: 9 points, 3W-OL-0D, +5 GD

2-0 win vs. Necaxa

3-1 win vs. Santos Laguna

2-1 win vs. Cruz Azul

Chicago cruised to the quarterfinals with a perfect record, continuing an impressive 2026 campaign for Gregg Berhalter's side.

The Fire capped their unbeaten run by scoring a stoppage-time winner to defeat Cruz Azul, 2-1, at SeatGeek Stadium.

With Chicago also enjoying a strong MLS season and recently completing the blockbuster signing of Polish international legend Robert Lewandowski, momentum is building.

Phase One: 8 points, 2W-0L-1D, +5 GD

2-0 win vs. Club Tijuana

3-0 win vs. Club Puebla

1-1 draw vs. Club América, PK win

Austin FC went unbeaten through Phase One to secure their place in the quarterfinals, despite an up-and-down MLS campaign.

Myrto Uzuni has a joint tournament-best four goals, delivering a hat trick during Austin's 3-0 win over Club Puebla, then scoring a crucial equalizer in their 1-1 draw against Club América to secure advancement.

Can Uzuni's fellow DPs, Facundo Torres and Brandon Vazquez, find their stride as well?

Phase One: 8 points, 2W-0L-1D, +3 GD

3-1 win vs. Atlas

2-1 win vs. Pachuca

1-1 draw vs. Pumas UNAM, PK win

Columbus went unbeaten in Phase One behind standout performances from recent DP arrival Brais Méndez, who netted a goal contribution (2g/1a) in each match.

The Crew opened with a victory over Atlas before serving up some Concacaf Champions Cup revenge in their win against Pachuca.

They closed out Phase One with a 1-1 draw against Pumas UNAM, earning the extra point with a 3-2 penalty shootout victory, as goalkeeper Nicholas Hagen made the decisive save.

Phase One: 7 points, 2W-0L-1D, +7 GD

1-1 draw vs. Tigres UANL, PK loss

4-0 win vs. Atlante

3-0 win vs. FC Juárez

After opening Phase One with a 1-1 draw against Tigres UANL, then falling in the ensuing penalty-kick shootout, RSL responded with back-to-back blowout victories to punch their quarterfinal ticket.

Pablo Mastroeni's side cruised past Atlante FC 4-0 before routing FC Juárez, 3-0, leaving them with seven points and a +7 goal differential.

This comes as homegrown winger Zavier Gozo will reportedly join English Premier League side Crystal Palace for around a $15 million transfer fee.

Phase One: 9 points, 3W-0L-OD, +3 GD

1-0 win vs. Nashville SC

2-1 win vs. Orlando City

3-2 win vs. Inter Miami CF

Club León booked their quarterfinal ticket with a perfect run through Phase One, winning all three of their matches and seeing Colombian striker Daniel Arcila score four of their six goals.

León started by downing MLS Supporters' Shield leaders Nashville SC before defeating Antoine Griezmann and Orlando City.

They then earned a dramatic win over Lionel Messi and Inter Miami CF, eliminating the 2023 Leagues Cup champions from the tournament.

Phase One: 7 points, 2W-0L-1D, +4 GD

3-1 win vs. San Diego FC

3-1 win vs. Portland Timbers

1-1 draw vs. Austin FC, PK loss

One of LIGA MX's most storied clubs, Club América advanced to the quarterfinals after going unbeaten in regulation time during Phase One.

Las Águilas opened with back-to-back 3-1 victories over San Diego FC and the Portland Timbers before drawing Austin FC, 1-1, and falling in the ensuing penalty shootout.

Former LAFC star Brian Rodríguez recorded 2g/2a in América's first two matches. Club América also recently acquired midfielder Edwin Cerrillo from the LA Galaxy.

Phase One: 6 points, 2W-1L-0D, +4 GD

3-0 win vs. Seattle Sounders FC

1-0 loss vs. LAFC

3-1 win vs. FC Dallas

The top-ranked team in Phase One, courtesy of their 2025 LIGA MX Clausura, Apertura and Campeón de Campeones titles, Toluca secured their quarterfinal berth via a pair of convincing victories during Phase One.

Los Diablos Rojos opened the tournament with a dominant win over reigning champions Seattle Sounders FC before suffering a late defeat to LAFC.

Toluca bounced back with a 3-1 victory over FC Dallas to clinch their spot, with Uruguay international Federico Viñas finding the net in both victories.

Phase One: 6 points, 2W-1L-0D, +1 GD

2-1 loss vs. Orlando City

2-1 win vs. Inter Miami CF

2-1 win vs. Nashville SC

CF Monterrey overcame an opening defeat to Orlando City to earn their quarterfinal spot, securing consecutive 2-1 victories over two of MLS's top sides in Inter Miami and Nashville.

Monterrey ultimately advanced on the goals-scored tiebreaker after finishing level with Cruz Azul on points, goal differential and regulation wins.

Along the way, former Chicago Fire striker Hugo Cuypers scored three goals and former Columbus Crew star Diego Rossi contributed 1g/1a. Both attackers left MLS in big-money transfers this summer.
