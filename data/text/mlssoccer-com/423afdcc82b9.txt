TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Signing

Chicago Fire FC have acquired midfielder Anzor Mekvabishvili from Romanian top-flight side Universitatea Craiova, the club announced Tuesday.

The 25-year-old Georgian international is under contract through the 2028-29 MLS season with an option for 2029-30.

Mekvabishvili has tallied 15g/20a in 231 matches across Universitatea Craiova and Dinamo Tbilisi in his home country. He's won four club titles, including the Romanian double in 2025-26.

Internationally, Mekvabishvili has earned 33 caps with Georgia. He was a key starter during their Euros 2024 appearance.

“We're excited to add a player of Anzor's pedigree to the club," said Chicago director of football and head coach Gregg Berhalter. "He is a proven competitor whose experience at both the international level and in European competition will be a valuable asset to our group.

"Being recognized as Player of the Year on a championship-winning team speaks to the consistency, leadership and impact he brings. We believe he will play an important role in strengthening our midfield and raising the level of the squad.”

Mekvabishvili is Chicago's second major signing this summer, following iconic Polish striker Robert Lewandowski.

The Fire are third in the Eastern Conference (37 points) and on track for a second-straight Audi MLS Cup Playoffs trip.
