Canada’s 26-player roster for the 2026 FIFA World Cup is set.

Nine players compete in MLS for six different MLS clubs, making up 35% of head coach Jesse Marsch's team.

Of the 26 players named, another 10 (38%) are either MLS homegrown products or developed in the league.

Combined, those 19 players account for 73% of the CanMNT roster.

The 2026 World Cup has expanded from the usual 32 nations to 48 total. The top two teams advance from each group, and the top eight third-place finishers also make the knockout phase (Round of 32).

The tournament, which is co-hosted by the United States, Mexico and Canada, spans from June 11 to July 19.
