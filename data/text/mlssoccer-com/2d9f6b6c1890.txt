The MLS Disciplinary Committee has issued the following rulings after Matchday 12 of the 2026 season.

The MLS Disciplinary Committee has found the Colorado Rapids in violation of the Mass Confrontation Policy in the 50th minute of their match against St. Louis CITY SC on May 9th.

The Colorado Rapids have violated the policy for the first time this season, and fines have been issued to the club and head coach Matt Wells.
