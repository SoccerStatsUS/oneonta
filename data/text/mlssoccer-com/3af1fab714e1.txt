It was a goal-filled and star-studded Matchday 25, offering standout nominees for AT&T Goal of the Matchday.

Vote here for your favorite.

Robert Lewandowski: The Polish icon opened the scoring in front of a club-record crowd, volleying home Andrew Gutman’s curling cross to help Chicago Fire FC earn a 1-1 draw with Inter Miami CF.

Mateusz Bogusz: With a deft first touch and a left-footed curler, Bogusz secured Houston Dynamo FC’s 2-1 win over Real Salt Lake in the 87th minute.

Brian White: Combining a quick throw, a looped pass from Thomas Müller and a two-touch, head-to-foot finish, Brian White’s strike opened Vancouver Whitecaps FC's 3-0 defeat of the LA Galaxy.

Kristoffer Velde: Tucking a long-range free kick into the side netting, the Norwegian’s first of two goals helped the Portland Timbers to a 2-2 draw against St. Louis CITY SC.
