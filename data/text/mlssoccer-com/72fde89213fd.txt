TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Loan

Minnesota United FC have loaned midfielder Dominik Fitz to Austrian Bundesliga side Wolfsberger AC through May 2027 with a purchase option, the club announced Thursday.

Since joining Minnesota in August 2025 from Austrian Bundesliga side FK Austria Wien, Fitz has made nine appearances across all competitions for the Loons.

He has also appeared in two matches for MNUFC2 in MLS NEXT Pro, recording a goal and an assist in 180 minutes.
