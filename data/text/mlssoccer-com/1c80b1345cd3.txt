Seattle Sounders FC have unveiled their primary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the Evergreen State Kit by adidas.

The kit celebrates the spirit, color, and culture that define Washington and the Sounders.

The design embraces simplicity and elegance, pairing Rave Green with subtle blue accents that mirror the state’s forests and waterways.
