Sporting Kansas City have unveiled their secondary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the 18th & Vine Kit by adidas.

The kit pays tribute to Kansas City’s legendary jazz district, where music, culture, and community shaped a nation’s rhythm.

Featuring sheet music from “Kansas City (Here I Come)” inside the collar, this kit celebrates the soul of a city that has always made the world swing.
