TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Loan

RSL receive: Colin Guske

ORL receive: $50k GAM

Real Salt Lake have acquired midfielder Colin Guske on loan from Orlando City through the 2026 season with a purchase option, the clubs announced Friday.

As part of the deal, Orlando receive $50,000 in General Allocation Money (GAM).

The 19-year-old US youth international has made 16 first-team appearances since signing a homegrown deal with Orlando ahead of the 2025 season.

Guske has also played 46 times for Orlando City B in MLS NEXT Pro, tallying one goal and four assists.

Additionally, Guske recently helped the US qualify for the 2027 FIFA U-20 World Cup.

"This is a great opportunity for Colin and an important step in his development,” said Ricardo Moreira, Orlando's general manager and sporting director.

"As a club, we are committed to doing right by our players and creating opportunities that help them continue to grow. Colin has worked hard throughout our development pathway, and this loan gives him the chance to gain valuable experience and consistent minutes at a high level in the short term. It also reflects our continued belief in the pathway from the Academy to the professional game.

"Every player's journey looks different and we remain focused on providing the right opportunities to help our young players reach their potential, whether that path unfolds in Orlando or through experiences like this one."
