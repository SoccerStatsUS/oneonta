The MLS Disciplinary Committee has issued the following rulings after Matchday 20 of the 2026 season.

The MLS Disciplinary Committee has fined Nashville SC forward Sam Surridge for violating the league’s policy regarding simulation/embellishment in the 52nd+ minute of Nashville’s match against Inter Miami CF on August 15.

The MLS Disciplinary Committee has fined Toronto FC midfielder Djordje Mihailovic for failing to leave the field in a timely manner in the 76th minute of Toronto’s match against the New England Revolution on August 15.

The MLS Disciplinary Committee has fined Inter Miami CF defender Maximiliano Falcón for violating the league’s policy regarding simulation/embellishment in the 49th+ minute of Miami’s match against Nashville SC on August 15.

The MLS Disciplinary Committee has fined Sporting Kansas City midfielder Kwaku Agyabeng for failing to leave the field in a timely manner in the 38th minute of Kansas City’s match against the Colorado Rapids on August 15.

The MLS Disciplinary Committee has fined Sporting Kansas City defender Moisés Mosquera for failing to leave the field in a timely manner in the 92nd+ minute of Kansas City’s match against the Colorado Rapids on August 15.
