Houston Dynamo FC have unveiled their secondary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the Mission Control Jersey by adidas.

The kit celebrates Houston’s pioneering spirit and place on the world stage.

Inspired by the city’s legacy in space exploration, the design features a satellite view of Houston layered with a heat map that captures the energy of its fans.
