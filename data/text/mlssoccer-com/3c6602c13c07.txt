The MLS Disciplinary Committee has issued the following ruling after Matchday 1 of the 2026 MLS season.

The MLS Disciplinary Committee has suspended Vancouver Whitecaps FC forward Emmanuel Sabbi for one match and fined Sabbi an undisclosed amount under Disciplinary Committee Parameter 3 for serious foul play in the 50th minute of Vancouver’s match against Real Salt Lake on February 21.

Sabbi will serve his one-match suspension against Toronto FC on February 28.
