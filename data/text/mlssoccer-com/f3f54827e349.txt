Atlanta United have unveiled their secondary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the Spirit of '96 Kit by adidas.

Atlanta’s past and future collide in this kit inspired by the 1996 Centennial Olympic Games.

Marking 30 years since Atlanta welcomed the world, the design channels the Atlanta Olympic motif featuring nostalgic colors and energy, with a gold crest symbolizing victory.
