New York City FC have unveiled their secondary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the All Nations Kit by adidas.

The kit celebrates New York City as the world’s capital, a place where cultures, languages, and traditions converge.

With the 2026 FIFA World Cup arriving in NYC’s backyard, this kit honors the spirit of the five boroughs – a true nation of nations.
