The Colorado Rapids have unveiled their primary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the Colorful Colorado Kit by adidas.

The kit pairs a bold new base with vibrant accents of burgundy, blue, green, and gold, reflecting the state’s skies, forests, sandstone, and aspens.

Celebrating 30 years of club history, the design honors the past while pushing into a new era, a fearless expression of the energy and color that define Colorado.
