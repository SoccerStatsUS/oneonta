TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Signing

San Diego FC have signed midfielder David Mimbang on loan through the 2027 Sprint Season from sister club FC Nordsjælland of the Danish Superliga, the club announced Wednesday.

The 18-year-old Cameroonian youth international made four appearances at the 2025 U-17 Africa Cup of Nations, scoring one goal. He also helped Cameroon lift the 2025 U-17 UNIFFAC title, where he was named tournament MVP.

“David is a player we’ve been tracking closely within Right to Dream, and bringing him to San Diego was a real group effort and a great example of the collaboration across our sporting departments,” said sporting director Tyler Heaps.

“We’re excited to welcome a midfielder with tremendous potential who brings a different profile and skill set to our group. He has the physical tools, technical quality and mentality to continue developing, and we believe San Diego FC is a great environment for him to take that next step.”

Mimbang is San Diego's latest summer signing following Designated Player winger Elias Achouri, defender Dagur Dan Thórhallson and defender Sam Vines.

The Chrome-and-Azul are eighth in the Western Conference (30 points), on track for a second straight Audi MLS Cup Playoffs appearance following their historic 2025 expansion campaign.
