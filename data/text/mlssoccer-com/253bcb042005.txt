The MLS Disciplinary Committee has the following rulings following Matchday 24 of the 2026 season.

The MLS Disciplinary Committee has fined Charlotte FC midfielder Ashley Westwood for failing to leave the field in a timely manner in the 71st minute of Charlotte’s match against Houston Dynamo FC on September 5.

The MLS Disciplinary Committee has fined San Diego FC midfielder Elias Achouri an undisclosed amount for violating the league’s policy regarding hands to the face/head/neck of an opponent in the 94th+ minute of San Diego’s match against Orlando City on September 5.

The MLS Disciplinary Committee has fined Orlando City forward Antoine Griezmann an undisclosed amount for violating the League’s policy regarding throwing/kicking an object toward fans in the 50th+ minute of Orlando’s match against San Diego FC on September 5.
