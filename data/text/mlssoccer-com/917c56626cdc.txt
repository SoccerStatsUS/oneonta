TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Transfer

Nashville SC have transferred striker Woobens Pacius to French third-division side FC Rouen 1899 and retain a sell-on percentage, the club announced Monday.

The 25-year-old Haitian international had two goals in 18 all-competition appearances since joining Nashville in February 2024 from Canadian Premier League side Forge FC.

He also had loan spells with MLS NEXT Pro affiliate Huntsville City and the USL Championship's Tampa Bay Rowdies.

Pacius earned his first senior cap for Haiti in October during 2026 FIFA World Cup qualifiers.

Nashville are first in the Supporters' Shield and Eastern Conference standings with 43 points (13W-2L-4D).
