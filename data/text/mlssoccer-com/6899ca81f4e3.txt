FC Cincinnati have unveiled their secondary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the Seven Hills Kit by adidas.

The kit celebrates a community that continues to climb, together, toward new heights.

Inspired by the city’s namesake, Cincinnatus, and the seven hills that shape its landscape, the design reflects resilience rising from the banks of the Ohio River.
