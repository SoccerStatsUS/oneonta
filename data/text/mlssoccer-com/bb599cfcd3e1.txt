TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Signing

The New England Revolution have signed homegrown midfielder Judah Siqueira through the 2027-28 MLS season with options through the 2030-31 campaign, the club announced Thursday.

The 17-year-old is the 17th player to make New England's MLS roster from the Revolution Academy.

In recent weeks, he has played twice for the first team on short-term loans from MLS NEXT Pro side Revolution II.

"We are thrilled to sign Judah as a Homegrown Player," said Chris Tierney, New England's general manager and chief soccer officer.

"Over the years, we have watched Judah develop into an outstanding young player through our Academy and with Revolution II. Judah is a high-potential player with a bright future ahead of him. We're excited to see his continued development with our club in years to come."

Siqueira has rapidly progressed through the club's pathway, tallying 3g/1a in 23 MLS NEXT Pro appearances. He joined the Revolution Academy in 2022.

"Signing a professional contract with New England is a dream come true," said Siqueira. "I’m incredibly grateful to God, and to all my coaches, teammates, and family members who have supported me and pushed me. Their belief in me has helped make this moment possible.

"I’m excited to join the first team, continue developing as a player, and contribute in any way I can to help the club succeed."
