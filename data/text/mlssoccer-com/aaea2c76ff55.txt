The Columbus Crew have unveiled their primary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the Crafted for Excellence Kit by adidas.

A tribute to the Crew’s legacy as Major League Soccer’s first club, the Crafted for Excellence Kit honors local pride by featuring color accents drawn from the Columbus city flag.
