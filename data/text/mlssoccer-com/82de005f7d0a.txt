Chicago clinched first place in the MLS Leagues Cup table with a dramatic 2-1 victory over reigning LIGA MX champions Cruz Azul, powered by Puso Dithejane's brace and second-half stoppage-time winner.

Players to watch

Robert Lewandowski: The legendary Polish striker has made an immediate impact since his summer arrival, scoring four goals in nine appearances.

Philip Zinckernagel: The two-time MLS All-Star remains one of the Fire's most dangerous attacking pieces, averaging nearly a goal contribution every game.

Chris Brady: The homegrown standout, now in his fourth season as Chicago's starting goalkeeper, served as the USMNT's third backstop at the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

Need to know

Chicago are three wins away from ending a 20-year trophy drought, dating back to their 2006 US Open Cup triumph.

The opportunity arrives in Year 2 under head coach Gregg Berhalter, who led the Fire to the Audi MLS Cup Playoffs last season for the first time since 2017.

Expectations have only risen following Lewandowski's arrival from FC Barcelona, which has fueled a seven-game unbeaten run across all competitions (6W-0L-1D).
