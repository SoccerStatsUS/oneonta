Last match

After beating Necaxa, 2-0, the Fire continued their positive momentum on Sunday by rallying for a 3-1 win over Santos Laguna.

Ezequiel Bullaude opened the scoring for the visitors, who nearly pushed the game to penalty kicks. However, Chicago turned it around by scoring three straight late goals, courtesy of Philip Zinckernagel (72'), Robin Lod (90') and legendary Polish striker Robert Lewandowski (93').

Players to watch

Robert Lewandowski: Chicago's blockbuster summer signing opened his Leagues Cup account on Sunday, building off his two goals in three MLS games.

Philip Zinckernagel: The Danish midfielder has earned All-Star selections in each of his two seasons with the Fire.

Chris Brady: The 22-year homegrown is among MLS's top goalkeepers and made the USMNT's 2026 World Cup squad.

Joel Waterman: The two-time World Cup veteran with Canada has stepped into the defensive spotlight amid a season-ending injury to South African star Mbekezeli Mbokazi.

Need to know

Chicago are dreaming big in their second season under head coach and director of football Gregg Berhalter, who last season guided the Fire to their first Audi MLS Cup Playoffs since 2017.

Now, the stakes and the ambitions grow higher for a club that made a massive statement of intent last month by signing Lewandowski.

Reaching the Leagues Cup quarterfinals, and possibly contending for their first-ever international trophy, would provide immediate proof of concept for Berhalter's project.
