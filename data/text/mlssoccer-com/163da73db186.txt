Julian Hall's breakout 2026 season with Red Bull New York is no overnight success story.

The 18-year-old homegrown forward is in his third full MLS campaign, after becoming one of the league's youngest debutants in 2023 (15 years, 190 days) and the second-youngest goalscorer (<a href="https://www.newyorkredbulls.com/news/julian-hall-scores-first-mls-goal-becomes-second-youngest-mls-goal-scorer" target="_blank">16 years, 87 days</a>) in MLS history the following year.

"I kind of try and make that my personality: being a mature guy and leading by example," Hall told <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7mUMpj4NQA4" target="_blank">Off The Ball</a>.

With 6g/2a so far in 2026, he's done just that. The US youth international recently became the <a href="https://x.com/avrbny/status/2040587311319024032" target="_blank">youngest player in MLS history</a> (18 years, 11 days) to score five or more goals in his first six appearances of an MLS season.

"I didn’t think I would be so blessed to have such a good start to the season," the New York City native said. "But that’s credit to all my teammates for setting me up in the best way possible."
