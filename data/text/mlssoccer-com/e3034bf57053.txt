TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Transfer

FC Dallas have transferred forward Louicius Deedson to French Ligue 2 side FC Sochaux-Montbéliard, the club announced Thursday.

The 25-year-old scored once in nine matches with Dallas after arriving last summer from Danish top-flight side Odense Boldklub.

Deedson represented Haiti at this summer's FIFA World Cup, marking their appearance since 1974. He's scored 10 times in 35 caps with Les Grenadiers.
