Atlanta United's Tristan Muyumba has won AT&T Goal of the Matchday honors for Matchday 20, claiming 39.6% of the fan vote.

The French midfielder curled a strike into the top-right corner to kick off the Five Stripes' 2-1 comeback win against Red Bull New York.

2nd place, Niklas Dorsch (26.1%): The German midfielder netted his first MLS goal in style, firing home a banger from long range, as Toronto FC snapped their 12-match winless streak with a 2-1 victory over the New England Revolution.

3rd place, Sam Surridge (25.9%): The English forward found the back of the net with a lovely backheel finish inside the six-yard box as Nashville SC defeated Inter Miami CF, 4-1.

4th place, Ervin Torres (8.5%): Austin FC's homegrown midfielder unleashed a blast from outside the box for his first MLS goal in a 2-1 setback to Texas rivals FC Dallas.
