Austin FC defender Guilherme Biro has won AT&T Goal of the Matchday honors for Matchday 21, taking home 53.8% of the fan vote.

The Brazilian fullback scored a flying karate-kick volley in Austin's 2-1 win at Seattle Sounders FC, sealing three road points in midweek action.

2nd place, Noah Streit (23.5%): CF Montréal ended their eight-game winless streak with a 2-1 victory at the Columbus Crew, courtesy of Streit's scorching long-range strike.

3rd place, Franco Negri (11.7%): Houston Dynamo FC took the Western Conference lead with a 1-0 win at Vancouver Whitecaps FC, delivered by Negri's left-footed laser from distance.

4th place, Kristoffer Velde (11.1%): The Portland Timbers soared to a 3-1 win over San Diego FC, and Velde got the party started with a right-footed blast.
