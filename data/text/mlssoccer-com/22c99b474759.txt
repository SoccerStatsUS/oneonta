Columbus Crew forward Gonzalo Tapia has won AT&T Goal of the Matchday honors for Matchday 24, earning 49.3% of the fan vote.

The recently signed Chilean netted a trivela for his first MLS goal, leading Columbus to a 3-0 win over the Colorado Rapids.

2nd place, Derrick Etienne Jr (24.3%): The Haitian international's stoppage-time thunder strike secured Toronto FC's 4-4 draw against Chicago Fire FC.

3rd place, Denis Bouanga (18.1%): The LAFC talisman completed a trademarked counterattack, cutting inside and firing a dart inside the far post in a 2-2 draw at Real Salt Lake.

4th place, Kaick (8.3%): The Brazilian midfielder pulled FC Dallas back from a two-goal deficit with a well-placed blast in a wild 4-3 win over Sporting Kansas City.
