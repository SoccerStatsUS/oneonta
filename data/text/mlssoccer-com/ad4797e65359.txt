Minnesota United FC have unveiled their primary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the Decade Kit by adidas.

Celebrating Minnesota's 10 seasons in MLS, the kit revisits the club's inaugural design with a modern edge.

The design honors the natural beauty and resilience of Minnesota while marking a decade of growth, pride, and the unbreakable spirit of the Loons.
