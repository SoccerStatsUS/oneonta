TRANSFER TRACKER STATUS: Mutual contract termination

Real Salt Lake and forward Rwan Cruz have mutually terminated his contract, the club announced Tuesday.

The 24-year-old Brazilian joined RSL last August on loan from Brazilian Série A side Botafogo. He tallied 0g/1a in 12 appearances for the club.

Cruz occupied a Designated Player roster slot.
