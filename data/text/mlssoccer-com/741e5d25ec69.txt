Austin FC have unveiled their secondary jersey ahead of the 2026 MLS season, the Rooted Kit by adidas.

Centered in the city’s natural beauty, the kit celebrates Austin's connection to its land and people.

Like the oak trees that symbolize the city’s strength, the kit stands as a tribute to ATX’s resilient, outdoor spirit.
