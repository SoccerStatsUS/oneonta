Cavan Sullivan: The 16-year-old wunderkind capped a counterattack with a cutback and world-class finish inside the far post, leading the Philadelphia Union to a 3-1 win at Red Bull New York.

Lionel Messi: The GOAT scored four goals in Inter Miami CF's 7-1 home rout of CF Montréal, capped by a curling free kick in second-half stoppage time as he took the Golden Boot presented by Audi lead.

Kelvin Yeboah: The Ghanaian forward had the Wonderwall rocking with a stunning bicycle kick inside the six-yard box during Minnesota United FC's wild 3-3 draw against Orlando City.
