Houston Dynamo FC have acquired left back Marcelo Saracchi from Argentine powerhouse Boca Juniors, the club announced Tuesday.

The 28-year-old Uruguay international defender is under contract through the 2028-29 campaign with options through 2030-31.

Saracchi is vastly experienced, tallying 12 goals and 25 assists in 272 club games. Aside from Boca Juniors, he's featured for the likes of Celtic FC (Scotland), Galatasaray (Turkey), RB Leipzig (Germany), Levante (Spain), River Plate (Argentina) and more.

Saracchi has won six trophies throughout his club career, including Celtic's 56th league title and the 2026 Scottish Cup while on loan last season.

Internationally, Saracchi has earned 11 caps with Uruguay's senior team. He also played at the 2017 FIFA U-20 World Cup.

"Marcelo is a balanced left back, capable of contributing to all phases of play, with considerable winning experience in top flights in Europe and South America," said Dynamo president of soccer Pat Onstad.

"His work ethic, competitiveness and team-first mentality match the DNA of a Dynamo player. We are excited to welcome Marcelo and his family to Houston, as we continue to push for postseason success this year."

As Saracchi joins the Dynamo, they're fourth in the Western Conference (32 points) and on track for a home Audi MLS Cup Playoffs spot.
