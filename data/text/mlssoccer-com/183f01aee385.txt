Due to pitch conditions and resulting player safety concerns created by inclement weather in the Cincinnati area, Saturday's match between FC Cincinnati and D.C. United has been postponed. The match will be played at a later date.

The Matchday 24 clash was scheduled to kick off at 7:30 pm ET (Apple TV).
