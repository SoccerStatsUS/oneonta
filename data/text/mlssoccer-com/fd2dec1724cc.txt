In his final chance to impress before the 2026 FIFA World Cup break, Brian White entered the MLS record books.

Vancouver Whitecaps FC’s star striker produced 2g/2a in Saturday’s 4-2 win at San Diego FC, reaching 10g/2a on the year and becoming the third American player in league history to score at least 10 goals in <a href="https://x.com/MLS_PR/status/2058382848977936626" target="_blank">four-straight seasons</a>.

White joins legendary MLS all-time goals leader Chris Wondolowski (2010-19) and Roy Lassiter (1996-99) in that prestigious club.

White's consistent form has put him in the conversation for a spot on the USMNT’s World Cup squad.

While he hasn’t featured under head coach Mauricio Pochettino since the 2025 Concacaf Gold Cup and will reportedly miss out on the final 26-man roster, White put in a statement performance.

And it came at the perfect time, snapping a four-game scoreless streak before the Whitecaps resume their MLS campaign on July 16 at Chicago Fire FC (8:30 pm ET | <a href="https://tv.apple.com/us/sporting-event/chicago-fire-fc-vs-vancouver-whitecaps-fc/umc.cse.3b8xzt13ii7or5o84lo2p9d79?ctx_brand=tvs.sbd.4000%2Ctvs.sbd.7000" target="_blank">Apple TV</a>).

Thanks in large part to White's display at Snapdragon Stadium, Vancouver head into the pause atop the Western Conference standings (32 points; 10W-2L-2D record).
