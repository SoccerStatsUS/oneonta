Soccer is a global game, and the NWSL continues to prove it’s one of the most globally connected leagues in the world. That’s never been clearer than in this international window, where 130 NWSL players across all 16 clubs have been called up to represent their national teams, the highest single-window total in league history.

Beyond the record, the number highlights just how quickly the league is growing. It was only last year that the NWSL first surpassed 100 call-ups in a single window, and now that mark has been decisively cleared. Expansion has played a role, with two new clubs adding depth across the league, but the surge also reflects a more active U.S. Youth National Team pipeline under Emma Hayes, further cementing the NWSL as a cornerstone of the global game.
