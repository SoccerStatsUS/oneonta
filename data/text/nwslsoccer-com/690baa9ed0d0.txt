The NWSL has long been a hub for world-class talent, and this international window is no exception. As national teams gear up for a packed slate of April friendlies and qualifiers, 101 players across the league, from all 14 teams, have been called up to 31 different national teams camps. From seasoned veterans to rising stars, NWSL rosters are once again being tapped by national team coaches around the globe—a testament to the league’s growing international depth and competitive quality.
