The Orlando Pride extended their record-breaking unbeaten streak to 21 games after a scoreless draw against Kansas City Current. This match marked only the second scoreless result for both teams this season, a testament to the defensive discipline on display during the fierce battle for points. Orlando has been on an incredible run this season, breaking club, franchise, and individual records every weekend.

Orlando Pride goalkeeper Anna Moorhouse continues to make her mark on the league, earning her 11th shutout of the season and tying the league record. Moorhouse's commanding presence in goal has been a key factor in the Pride's unbeaten run this season. Her relentless shot-stopping has transformed Orlando into the toughest defense in the league, conceding just 12 goals—the fewest by any team so far.

The Chicago Red Stars pulled off a huge road victory, winning in Portland for the first time since June 1, 2013. A lone goal from Ally Schlegel in the first half secured the 1-0 win, marking a major moment for the Red Stars as they push toward a playoff spot. This result not only breaks the drought in Portland but also boosts Chicago's confidence in their ability to contend against top-tier teams.

Washington Spirit forward Ashley Hatch made history during her team’s clash against the Houston Dash, becoming the seventh player in NWSL history to score 50 regular-season goals. Hatch netted two in the game, cementing her place among the league’s elite goal scorers.

In front of a roaring crowd of 6,837 fans, the North Carolina Courage extended their impressive home unbeaten streak to 19 games. The turnout marked the second-largest crowd the team has seen this season, as their fans continue to rally behind them at home.

Ashley Sanchez of the North Carolina Courage made her mark once again by scoring a crucial equalizer against Bay FC. This goal placed her in the record books as the first NWSL player to score against all active clubs in the league.

San Diego Wave forward Amirah Ali wasted no time in her match against the Utah Royals, scoring the fastest goal of the 2024 regular season just 27 seconds into the game. This lightning-quick strike is not only the fastest in Wave history but also ranks as the fourth-fastest in NWSL history.

A significant milestone was reached for four players in Week 19, as Kaleigh Kurtz, Tyler Lussi, and Casey Murphy of the North Carolina Courage and Morgan Gautrat of Orlando Pride all made 100th regular-season appearances. Each of these players has been a key contributor for their respective teams, adding experience and leadership on and off the field.

Washington Spirit secured their place in the 2024 playoffs, becoming the second team to clinch a postseason spot. This marks their first trip to the playoffs since they last hoisted the NWSL trophy in 2021. The Spirit’s return to the postseason is a testament to the grit and talent of their players, who have fought hard all season.
