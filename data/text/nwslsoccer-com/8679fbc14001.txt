District vs. Empire: The Battle of the East returns this weekend as Washington Spirit and Gotham FC meet in the second Rivalries presented by Ally clash of the 2025 season. It’s more than just three points on the line—this matchup is one of the longest-running and most tightly contested in NWSL history, with the two teams facing off 40 times in all competitions. From penalty shootouts to packed stadiums, these two legacy clubs always deliver. Here’s why you won’t want to miss this one.

Spirit and Gotham’s last meeting? The unforgettable 2024 NWSL Semifinal—an instant classic. Gotham struck first behind Esther González, but the Spirit forced extra time with a stoppage-time equalizer from Hal Hershfelt, then won it in penalties thanks to three straight saves from Aubrey Kingsbury.

It was the latest chapter in one of the league’s most storied rivalries. Only Seattle vs. Portland has been played more often, with this coming matchup marking their 41st all-time meeting. Both clubs date back to the NWSL’s inaugural season in 2013, and the history between them runs deep—from the Maryland SoccerPlex to packed Audi Field. Saturday’s clash? Just the next page in a storied rivalry.

Washington’s newest forward is finding her rhythm. After scoring her first goal of the season against powerhouse Orlando, the Nigerian striker is showing signs of breaking out. Her combination of speed, off-the-ball movement, and finishing touch makes her a must-watch—especially in a high-profile game like this.

After cruising to a dominant 4-0 win over Angel City, Gotham hit a speed bump midweek in Portland. With little time to dwell, they'll need a bounce-back performance—fast. This rivalry matchup is a chance to regain momentum, grab critical points, and make a statement on the road.

Gotham’s Spanish striker is on fire. With 5 goals in her last 3 games, Esther has cemented herself as one of the league’s most lethal finishers. Whether she’s poaching in the box or rising for a header, all eyes will be on her to see if she can break through Washington’s back line once again, especially considering her first two NWSL goals were against the Spirit last season.

The Spirit’s home field has become a fortress. They’ve set attendance records, delivered late drama, and built one of the loudest fanbases in the league. With District fans ready to rock and traveling Gotham fans in attendance as well, the atmosphere is going to be rowdy. These teams have met twice in knockout play—both times ended in penalties. Don’t be surprised if it’s another classic.

Don’t miss a second of this East Coast rivalry—rivalry weekends just hit different. Tune in Saturday, April 26 at 1:00 PM ET on CBS and Paramount+!
