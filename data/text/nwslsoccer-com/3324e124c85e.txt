Washington Spirit forward Trinity Rodman's dramatic extra-time goal in the USWNT's Olympic quarterfinal match against Japan yesterday secured the team's place in the semifinals.

This goal, coupled with her earlier group stage goals against Zambia and Australia, highlights her pivotal role in Team USA's offensive strategy at the Paris Olympics.

Rodman has been indispensable for the USWNT, starting in fourth consecutive matches and consistently contributing to the team's success. Alongside her three goals this tournament, she provided a crucial assist in the group stage victory over Germany, maintaining a goal contribution in all four matches.

Team USA fans can catch Trinity Rodman's dynamic play in their nearest NWSL market, while those in the DC Metro area can see her in action at Audi Field for the Washington Spirit. Regardless of the outcome for the USWNT in Paris, her return to Audi Field is highly anticipated, especially with the Spirit hosting English clubs Arsenal and Chelsea at the end of August.

Rodman and the USWNT will now travel to Lyon for a semifinal showdown against Germany in a repeat of their group stage encounter. The winner will advance to the Olympic gold medal match, with Team USA hoping to continue their pursuit of their fifth Olympic gold medal.
