NWSL fans will have a new way to keep tabs on the latest news about their favorite teams and players with theSkimm.

In a new partnership, the NWSL and theSkimm are joining forces to tell stories from across the league – both on and off the pitch. As an official media partner, theSkimm will deliver NWSL news to their vast and avid audience.

Like the NWSL, theSkimm is dedicated to championing women. This partnership with theSkimm will only elevate the game by highlighting elite performances and showcasing just how fun, unpredictable, and action-packed each week in the NWSL is.

Fans can find NWSL news across all of theSkimm’s platforms – from newsletters to social media to the Well Played podcast. TheSkimm will provide exclusive storytelling, behind-the-scenes moments, player interviews, and everything in between.

Millions of readers know theSkimm for bringing them the stories that women care about, and now, those readers will learn about the biggest and most special moments in the NWSL. Talk about a win-win.
