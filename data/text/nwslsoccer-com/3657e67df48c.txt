Four standout NWSL players have earned a spot on the CONCACAF W Champions Cup Group Stage Best XI; Izzy D’Aquila and Shelby Hogan of Portland Thorns FC, along with Delanie Sheehan and Jenna Nighswonger of NJ/NY Gotham FC represented their clubs and the NWSL with strong displays in the tournament.

Portland Thorns FC and Gotham FC will continue to represent the NWSL in the semifinals. The knockout rounds are set for May 2025 at a to-be-announced centralized venue. The semifinal matchups will feature Tigres Femenil vs Portland Thorns FC and Club America Femenil vs NJ/NY Gotham FC.

Thursday, May 22, 2025

SF1: Tigres Femenil vs Portland Thorns FC

SF2: Club America Femenil vs NJ/NY Gotham FC

The winners of these two matchups will advance to the Final on Sunday, May 25, 2025, while the runners up will compete for third place.

Sunday, May 25, 2025

3rd Place: Runner Up SF1 vs Runner Up SF2

Final: Winner SF1 vs Winner SF2
