With a 2-1 overtime win over No. 7 Bay FC, the No. 2 Washington Spirit returns to Audi Field on Saturday, November 16, to host No. 3 NJ/NY Gotham FC, who advanced to the second round after taking down No. 6 Portland Thorns FC by a 2-1 result. The semifinal match will air live on the CBS Television Network and stream on Paramount+ and coverage begins at 12 p.m. ET.

On the other side of the bracket, the top-seeded Orlando Pride defeated the No. 8 Chicago Red Stars, 4-1, to secure the club’s first-ever semifinal berth. The Pride will face the No. 4 Kansas City Current at Inter&Co Stadium on Sunday, November 17. The match will air live on ABC at 3 p.m. ET with studio coverage beginning at 2:30 p.m. ET.
