Get you a tunnel fit that can do it all, and Jaelin Howell delivered in Matchweek 5. Howell strutted into Gotham's home match in a strapless mini dress and a ruffled bottom – made from her club's kit.

The dress was made Coco Cultr, a sustainable fashion brand, and takes Gotham's "Lady Liberty" Nike kit and turns it into something fit for the runway. The new kit comes together to form the face of the Statue of Liberty, and as a dress it's a whole vibe.

The dress is just another fun moment in a string of fun moments that started with Howell signing a contract extension through 2028. After signing her new contract, Howell and teammate Ryan Campbell met rapper and women's sports ambassador Flavor Flav. Howell got to rock a clock of her own, and the Gotham teammates taught Flavor Flav what fancy footwork is.

Not a bad week to be Jaelin Howell.
