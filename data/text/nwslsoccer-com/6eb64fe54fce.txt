The National Women's Soccer League returns to ABC, ESPN, and ESPN+ for the 2025 season, featuring some of the league’s biggest stars and marquee matchups. With 20 games on ESPN platforms—including three playoff games—fans can catch the action across ABC, ESPN, ESPN2, ESPN Deportes, and Disney+.

The first NWSL match of the season on an ABC/ESPN platform kicks off with a high-stakes clash at CPKC Stadium on Saturday, March 15, at 12:30 p.m. ET, as the Kansas City Current hosts the Portland Thorns FC on ABC.

ABC will air six matches, including three playoff games.

ESPN will televise 10 games, ESPN2 will broadcast four, and ESPN Deportes will provide live Spanish-language coverage for 10 matches.

ESPN+ will stream all 20 matches live in English and Spanish.

Disney+ will feature select matches monthly.
