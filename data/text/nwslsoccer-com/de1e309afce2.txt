Hosting Pride Nights has become a beloved tradition across sports and the NWSL is no exception. Every year, each club celebrates its local LGBTQ+ community with a Pride Night match: a joyous evening with special giveaways, meaningful community partnerships, fan activations, music, and more. From rainbow-filled stadiums and Pride-themed merch to inclusive programming and player-led initiatives, each celebration reflects the NWSL community's enduring commitment to visibility, allyship, and love in all its forms.

Make sure to shop the <a href="https://nwslshop.com/collections/2025-pride-collection" target="_blank">NWSL's Pride collection</a> so you are Pride match ready.

Note: While many teams will host their official Pride Nights during the month of June, others will celebrate later in the year. Regardless of timing, all teams will wear Pride-themed numbers during a designated match in June to honor and uplift the LGBTQ+ community together.
