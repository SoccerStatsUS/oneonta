It’s been a long three years since we last saw the characters of “Ted Lasso,” but the wait is over.

For the first time, we see Rebecca Welton (Hannah Waddingham), Keeley Jones (Juno Temple), and Leslie Higgins (Jeremy Swift) venture to Ted’s (Jason Sudeikis) side of the pond, hoping to persuade him to take the job of head coach for AFC Richmond’s new women’s club.

One by one, they fall in love with Kansas City (both in Missouri and Kansas), much like we saw visitors from all over the world embrace American cities during the World Cup. Their tour culminated with a visit to CPKC Stadium to see their facilities and watch the Kansas City Current take on Gotham FC.

It wouldn’t be a true Kansas City Current experience without a Temwa Chawinga goal. The back-to-back NWSL MVP and Golden Boot winner gets her flowers when her very real goal against Gotham last season is highlighted as part of the Richmond crew’s game day.

It’s a familiar sight for NWSL fans – and a dreaded one if your team isn’t the Current. CPKC Stadium is one of the league’s fortresses with the Current going unbeaten in 26 consecutive regular season home matches, an active league record.

For Keeley, the moment sparked something like hope about what the women’s club could be with real investment. Keeley’s optimism and sense of purpose is met with pragmatic concerns from Rebecca.

Between the energy from the match and the guaranteed Lasso lessons to come, it’s the perfect pairing for your NWSL matchweek. New episodes of “Ted Lasso” are available on Wednesdays on Apple TV.
