NEW YORK (APRIL 2, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today that San Diego Wave FC midfielder Lia Godfrey has been named NWSL Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, for the month of March by the NWSL Media Association and the league’s on-air broadcast talent.

The four-time All-American out of the University of Virginia has started her professional career out on the West coast in fine form, scoring three goals in the month, including the match winner against Utah in the 87th minute to secure the team their first win of the season.

Ally, a financial services company with the nation's largest all-digital bank and a leading brand in women's sports, has been a primary sponsor of the NWSL since 2021 and serves as the official retail banking partner of the NWSLPA as well. Through its longstanding partnership, Ally has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to elevating women's soccer and enhancing the experience for athletes and fans alike.

The NWSL Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, is selected by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league consistently, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. Awards for the month of March are based on performances during regular season matches.
