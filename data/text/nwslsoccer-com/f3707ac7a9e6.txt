NEW YORK (August 6, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League (NWSL) announced today that Adrián González has been named Coach of the Month for the month of July by the NWSL Media Association and the league’s on-air broadcast talent.

The Spirit head man had his team firing on all cylinders in July, especially on defense as the Spirit allowed just three goals in their six matches. Out of a possible 18 points in the, the Spirit earned 15, leaving them just one point off the top spot.

The NWSL Coach of the Month is selected by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league consistently, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. Awards for the month of July are based on performances during regular season matches.
