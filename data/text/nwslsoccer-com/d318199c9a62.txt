We’re headed into Week 21 of the 2025 NWSL season (Sept. 18–21) and seven playoff spots are still up for grabs. Kansas City Current have already punched their ticket and secured home-field advantage through the semifinals. While no teams can clinch or be eliminated this weekend, the coveted NWSL Shield could be claimed by a new winner.

No team can be eliminated.

Kansas City has already claimed a home playoff game. No one else this weekend can claim another one.

After Week 20, only Kansas City and Washington – both of whom have never won an NWSL Shield before – remain in contention.

Kansas City can clinch the club’s first-ever Shield in Week 21 with:

A win vs. Seattle AND a Washington draw/loss vs. Angel City

If the Current secure the Shield this weekend, they would become the fastest team to ever clinch with five games still to play.
