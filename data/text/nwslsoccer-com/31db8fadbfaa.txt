DENVER, CO (March 28, 2026) \- For the second time in under a year, an NWSL match as broken the attendance record for a professional women’s sporting event in the U.S., with 63,004 in attendance today for Denver Summit FC’s inaugural home match against the Washington Spirit.

This game broke the NWSL and U.S. professional women’s sports league record set by Bay FC last season when they hosted the Washington Spirit at Oracle Park in San Francisco, which drew a crowd of over 40,000 fans.
