On Friday, Orlando Pride defeated Houston Dash on the road, logging their 18th consecutive unbeaten regular season match. This prolific run extends to 2023 and started, actually, in a 1-0 victory against the Dash.

The Pride initially tied the league record on July 6th when they handed the then-undefeated Kansas City Current their first loss, marking their 17th consecutive unbeaten regular season match—a league best.

Now, with 18 consecutive unbeaten regular season matches, the Pride continue to extend their record and march toward the postseason.
