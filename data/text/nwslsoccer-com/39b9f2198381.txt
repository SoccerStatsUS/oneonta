It’s hard enough to be a professional athlete in the NWSL, especially when you’re competing for both club and country, but Washington Spirit defender Gabby Carle somehow made time to add another achievement to her resume.

Carle passed her MCAT exam.

To quote the great Elle Woods: “What? Like it’s hard?”

After returning from her duties with Team Canada, her Washington Spirit teammates surprised her with a celebration for passing the infamously difficult test to qualify for medical school.

“I’m bad at speeches, guys. You know this, but I love you all. It was a tough year, but thank you for bearing with me and all my studying. I know I was boring. Now I’m not anymore.”

Earlier in the week, Carle shared her own personal celebration after passing the exam. In a video she posted on TikTok, she unboxed a pair of secondhand Christian Louboutin shoes.

“As a little treat, I was like I want red bottom heels. A little treat,” she said.

Rocking gray sweatpants, Carle tried the shoes on for the camera.

“I don’t think I can walk in them,” she said, but managed to give a short runway walk in her living room. “It’s gonna take some practice, but they fit.”

There’s nothing that can stop future Dr. Carle – even a precarious heel.
