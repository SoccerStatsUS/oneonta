How do I watch live games from the 2024 NWSL Regular Season?

Friday night matches airing on Prime Video are available to US fans here.

Saturday night double-headers airing at 7:30 and 10 p.m. ET on Scripps-owned ION network are available to US fans here.

For games airing on CBS, US fans can watch here. US Fans can also stream on Paramount+ here.

For games airing on ESPN, US fans can watch here. Matches on ESPN and ESPN2 will also stream on ESPN+.

For games airing on NWSL+, US fans can watch here.

Fans outside of the US can watch on NWSL+.

Fans in Australia can watch on Optus.

Matchup: North Carolina Courage vs. Bay FC - 7:00 PM EST

How to watch: NWSL+

North Carolina Courage will host Bay FC for the first time in club history this Friday, bringing together two teams riding high on recent success. The Courage are coming off a dominant 4-1 win over San Diego, highlighted by Malia Berkely’s historic three-assist performance in the first half, a display of their potent attacking prowess. Bay FC, fresh from a home victory, also has reasons for optimism, with recent signing Penelope Hocking notching her first assist for the club in their win against Racing. Will the Courage continue their dominance or will Bay FC end their 18-game home unbeaten streak?

Matchup: Orlando Pride vs. Kansas City Current - 8:00 PM EST

How to watch: Prime Video

Friday’s matchup between Orlando Pride and Kansas City Current is set to be a thrilling contest between two of the top teams in the NWSL. Orlando, with a league-best 47 points and an unmatched seven-game home winning streak, boasts a balanced attack powered by Marta, Barbra Banda, and Adriana. Kansas City, riding on Temwa Chawinga’s record-breaking scoring streak, is in third place with 38 points and looking to disrupt Orlando’s dominance. This high-stakes match has major playoff race implications and is surely going to be a must-watch for any soccer fan.

Matchup: Portland Thorns vs. Chicago Red Stars - 10:00 PM EST

How to watch: Prime Video

The Portland Thorns and Chicago Red Stars are set for a much-anticipated showdown, bringing together two of the NWSL’s oldest franchises. Adding to the excitement is the reunion of two-thirds of the USWNT’s iconic "Triple Espresso" trio—Sophia Smith and Mallory Swanson–along with Team USA’s beloved goalkeeper, Alyssa Naeher. Swanson has been a highlight this season for Chicago, with an impressive 69.2% of her shots on target, and is fifth in the Golden Boot race. Despite back-to-back losses in their last two regular season games, Portland remains a formidable force, especially at home with Smith continuing her fine goal-scoring form.

Matchup: Racing Louisville FC vs. Angel City FC - 7:30 PM EST

How to watch: ION

Angel City FC and Racing Louisville are gearing up for another thrilling showdown, with both sides loaded with attacking threats. Leading Angel City’s offensive charge is the electric Alyssa Thompson, who’s been on a tear with four goals in her last three outings, will be joined by Sydney Leroux, Claire Emslie, Messiah Bright, and Christen Press. Racing Louisville, meanwhile, will rely on Bethany Balcer to spearhead their attack. Although she didn’t capitalize on any chances last week, she fired off four shots and netted an assist in the match prior, making her a constant danger to defenders.

Matchup: Utah Royals vs. San Diego Wave FC - 10:00 PM EST

How to watch: ION

San Diego Wave and Utah Royals are set for a crucial rematch after San Diego’s 2-0 win in their first meeting back in May. Since then, the Wave have struggled, going winless in their last 12 regular-season matches, and recently suffering a 4-1 defeat to North Carolina where they allowed three first-half goals. On the other side, Utah comes into this clash with momentum, having won their last three home matches, scoring 10 goals across those victories. María Sánchez will be a key player for San Diego, as she recently became the all-time leading Mexican international in NWSL goal contributions with 28. Utah’s home form has been bolstered by their ability to score from distance, leading the league with six goals from outside the box at home this season. Both teams are hungry for points and looking to fight for a playoff spot, making this a match to watch.

Matchup: Washington Spirit vs. Houston Dash - 1:00 PM ET

How to watch: ESPN2, ESPN+, ESPN Deportes

Washington Spirit and Houston Dash are set for a key matchup, with both teams at pivotal points in their seasons. The Spirit are riding high after a stoppage-time winner against Portland last weekend, where Trinity Rodman recorded her 20th career assist, making her the youngest player in NWSL history to reach that milestone. Meanwhile, Houston is struggling, having lost five straight regular-season matches, though Diana Ordóñez continues to shine, netting her eighth career header—the most by any player since she entered the league in 2022. Washington defeated Houston 3-1 in their first meeting this season, and they’ll look to win consecutive games against the Dash for the first time since 2016.

Matchup: Seattle Reign FC vs. NJ/NY Gotham FC - 10:00 PM ET

How to watch: CBSSN

Gotham FC and Seattle Reign are set to square off in the first Monday night showdown of the season. Gotham enters the match fresh off a dramatic 2-1 victory over Houston where a stoppage time game winner courtesy of Esther guaranteed them 3 points. The team has been in blistering form, winning 14 of their last 19 matches—ideal conditions for the defending champions. Reign, meanwhile, is also headed into the match on a high after beating Angel City at home last weekend. They struggled to find points the first half of the season but now have momentum on their side.
