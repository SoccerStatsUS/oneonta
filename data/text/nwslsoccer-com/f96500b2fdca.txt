The inaugural NWSL x LIGA MX Femenil Summer Cup is a tournament featuring a fusion of the two top leagues in North America. Kicking off in July, the 33-match competition featured 14 NWSL clubs and the top 6 LIGA MX Femenil teams, showcasing the talent and excitement of women’s soccer on a continental stage. The tournament progressed through an intense group stage and now, with the semifinals behind us, only two teams will face off in the championship on October 25 in San Antonio, Texas – Kansas City Current and Gotham FC.

Match: Kansas City Current vs. Gotham FC - Friday, October 25th at 8:00 PM ET

How to watch: CBSSN | Paramount+

In the first semifinal, Gotham FC edged Angel City FC 1-0 with a decisive goal from Delanie Sheehan early in the second half. Despite Angel City's dominance in possession and earning 14 corner kicks, they couldn't find the back of the net, thanks in part to Gotham’s organized defense and solid goalkeeping from Cassie Miller. Gotham’s defensive prowess, which has been a cornerstone of their campaign, once again proved to be the difference.

In the second semifinal, Kansas City Current continued their unbeaten run with a commanding 2-0 victory over the North Carolina Courage. Temwa Chawinga opened the scoring within two minutes, and Debinha sealed the win with a second goal in the 77th minute. Kansas City’s defense, anchored by key performances from Alana Cook and Ellie Wheeler, held strong, while goalkeeper AD Franch preserved her clean sheet with a standout save late in the match.

The championship match will feature two of the most formidable teams in the NWSL—Kansas City Current and Gotham FC. Kansas City, powered by the attacking might of Chawinga, brings a break away, fast-paced style of play. Their relentless attack and solid back line make them a tough opponent to break down. Meanwhile, Gotham FC’s defensive discipline, combined with timely attacking bursts, has helped them thrive, particularly in tight quarters in front of the box.

The stage is set: Kansas City’s offensive firepower versus Gotham’s defensive resilience, with the coveted inaugural Summer Cup trophy on the line.
