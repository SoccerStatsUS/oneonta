The NWSL is back for its 2025 season, kicking off on Friday, March 14th, and promising another electrifying year of unforgettable moments. As teams battle for supremacy and players chase their legacies, every match will be a new chapter in a story of grit, passion, and triumph. Get ready—the road to NWSL glory begins now.

Kansas City Current is riding a wave of momentum after a stellar 2024 campaign, finishing fourth and setting a league record with 57 goals. Leading the charge is captain Lo’Eau LaBonta, the team’s heartbeat and playmaker, alongside Temwa Chawinga, the 2024 MVP and Golden Boot winner, who netted 20 goals last season. They’re joined by dynamic attackers Michelle Cooper and Claire Hutton, while Brazilian goalkeeper Lorena strengthens the backline. New signings Mary Long and Clare Gagne bring youthful energy and depth, making an already potent lineup even more dangerous. With firepower across the pitch, Kansas City has its sights firmly set on championship glory.
