How to watch: CBSSN

Matchweek 7 gets underway a little early, with a single Wednesday night matchup in Utah.

The Utah Royals enter the midweek clash as one of the hottest teams in the league, riding a four-match win streak into their home showdown against the Houston Dash. While Utah’s attack slowed slightly in last weekend’s narrow win over Angel City, the Royals continue finding ways to grind out results. Angel City may have provided plenty of footage for the Dash on how to lock down Mina Tanaka and Paige Cronin, Utah still has plenty of goal scorers for their opponents to contend with. Cloé Lacasse, in particular, has been electric lately with goals in back-to-back matches.

Houston, meanwhile, battled through a rainy 0-0 draw at home last weekend, but one positive takeaway may be the club’s growing trust in its youth. Four rookies — Kate Faasse, Leah Klenke, Linda Ullmark, and Kat Rader — started for the Dash with all but Faasse playing the full 90 minutes. Rader continues to play an important role on set pieces. In short, the experience these players are gaining week after week is quickly becoming one of the team’s biggest strengths and storylines.

Of course, the Dash also have proven difference-makers capable of changing a game in an instant, led by April Amazon Prime Best XI selection Kiki Van Zanten. The forward sits on four goals this season and, despite a brief scoring drought, remains one of Houston’s most dangerous attacking threats. If the Dash can reconnect with her movement in and around the box, they could be the team that finally snaps Utah’s streak, carrying some much needed momentum into the weekend.
