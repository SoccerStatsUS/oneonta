The National Women’s Soccer League (NWSL) today announced international distribution plans for the 2025 NWSL Playoffs, presented by Google Pixel, ensuring fans around the world can experience every match live as the league crowns its next champion.

Coverage will be available across more than 20 territories with an international household reach of nearly 400 million, with exclusive partners including TNT Sports (UK & Ireland), TSN (Canada), Stan (Australia), Sport en France (France), and Dubai TV (MENA).

Additional carriage will be provided through platforms such as Disney+ (Nordics), Sportsdigital (DACH), Canal Goat and XSports (Brazil), ESPN (Sub-Saharan Africa, Netherlands), and Canal 11 (Portugal).

Full coverage and platform details for viewers tuning in outside the U.S. are available here.

The eight-team quarterfinal round of the 2025 NWSL Playoffs, presented by Google Pixel, begins Friday, November 7, on Prime Video at 8 p.m. ET as the reigning NWSL Champion and fourth-seeded Orlando Pride hosts No. 5 Seattle Reign FC at Inter&Co Stadium.
