NEW YORK (March 13, 2024) - Building on a year of unprecedented growth in 2023, the National Women’s Soccer League proudly opens its 2024 season this Friday, March 15, kicking off a historic campaign that will feature more players, more matches and more opportunities to watch the action than ever before.

WE PLAY HERE

In addition to the start of the season, the NWSL announced the return of its heralded<u> </u><u>WE PLAY HERE</u> marketing campaign with the call-to-action encouraging fans to Keep Up!** Leaning into the one-of-a-kind athleticism, competition and emotion of professional women’s soccer, it also captures the momentum of the league’s 12th campaign with four new broadcast partners, two new teams, and the unveiling of the first-ever stadium built specifically for women’s professional soccer.

WHERE TO WATCH

The 2024 NWSL season marks the first year of the league’s landmark media rights deal. Fans will now be able to watch 121 matches across Prime Video, Scripps-owned ION network, CBS and ESPN. The remaining 69 matches will be available for free on NWSL+, the league’s first-ever domestic direct to consumer streaming platform.
