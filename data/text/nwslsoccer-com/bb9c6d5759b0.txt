When Jordyn learned that Dare to Care and Feeding America had evolved the traditional food pantry stereotype of canned or boxed foods to providing meals and groceries with fresh produce and more nutrient dense options, her entire perception changed. As a foodie and an athlete, Jordyn always enjoyed cooking and has known about the importance of food on a healthy lifestyle. When she learned that Dare to Care was not only providing groceries but actual meals created in their Community Kitchen to people in need around the city, and in 13 total counties, she knew she wanted to get involved. Jordyn is grateful to volunteer her time to help this organization continue to push their mission forward to serve the local community.
