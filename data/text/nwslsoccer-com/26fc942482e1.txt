Halftime performances aren’t about waiting for the storm to pass. Sometimes, they’re about dancing in the rain – or something like that. It may have been a rainy night in Columbus, Ohio, for the NWSL Challenge Cup presented by e.l.f., but Amber Mark had it covered.

The NWSL kicked off the Summer of Soccer in epic fashion at Challenge Cup presented by e.l.f. It’s where Gotham took home the first hardware of the 2026 season, the NWSL on Tour road trip began, and Mark brought down the house with a halftime show on the Main Stage presented by e.l.f.

On the heels of her 2025 album, “Pretty Idea,” Mark’s unique genre-bending blend of soul, funk, and dream-pop has made her a singular voice.

Performing hit songs like “Doin’ Me,” “Let Me Love You,” and “Sweet Serotonin,” Mark brought down the house at ScottsMiracle-Gro Field. Columbus will soon be home to an NWSL team, and Mark helped make sure the new era started off right.

The halftime show was an NWSL first brought to life in partnership with e.l.f. As the league grows, including an expansion team coming to Columbus in 2028, the moments only get bigger and more unmissable.
