NEW YORK (October 30, 2023) –

The National Women’s Soccer League today announced the return of the league’s third annual Fan Fest on November 11 in San Diego, California. The festival is set to take place at Snapdragon Stadium ahead of the 2023 NWSL Championship, presented by Bud Light. As part of the celebration, the NWSL and Adobe are teaming up to host a free live concert featuring singer-songwriter, Bishop Briggs. <br>“I’m really excited to team up with Adobe to celebrate the Championship and bring together music and sports in a unique way for one night at the NWSL annual Fan Fest,” said Bishop Briggs, Adobe Stage headliner. “Like Adobe, I believe creativity is everywhere and I want to be able to inspire fans, players and artists alike to be empowered to express their passions no matter where they are.” <br>The free concert is part of Adobe’s continuing partnership with the NWSL to provide fans and players with a greater platform to showcase their creativity on and off the field. NWSL and Adobe this year launched the inaugural ‘Creator Class’, giving four emerging creators the opportunity to participate in through a professional creativity program and develop projects inspired by the NWSL and their favorite clubs. Adobe is also supporting NWSL players on their creative journey, providing the tools and guidance to build their own brands and develop stand-out content. <br>“Adobe’s innovative tools have empowered me to express myself on and off the field and I’m proud to be part of a community that celebrates creativity, champions self-expression and supports emerging women creators,” said Trinity Rodman, Adobe Creative Ambassador and Forward for Washington Spirit, NWSL. “Working with Adobe has allowed me to bring together my passion for both soccer and creativity and given me the opportunity to use my platform to further elevate women’s stories and achievements” <br>Starting at 2 p.m. PT on November 11, Championship matchday, Fan Fest will be free and open to the public and will feature a variety of fan-focused activities to celebrate the culmination of the 2023 NWSL season. <br>For more information on the NWSL and Adobe partnership, visit here . <br>ABOUT THE NATIONAL WOMEN’S SOCCER LEAGUE <br>The National Women’s Soccer League is the premier women’s professional soccer league in the world featuring national team players from around the globe.
