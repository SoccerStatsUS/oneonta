July 8, 2025 (NEW YORK) - The National Women’s Soccer League (NWSL) today issued a summary of findings and recommendations following the conclusion of an independent investigation into reports raised about Bay FC Head Coach Albertin Montoya.

The investigation concluded that Coach Montoya did not violate the NWSL Policy to Prevent and Eliminate Workplace Discrimination, Harassment, and Bullying.

The investigation also found that there were shortcomings in Coach Montoya’s communication style. The investigators made a series of recommendations to both Bay FC and the NWSL to facilitate more effective communication and enhance support structures, which the NWSL, Bay FC and Coach Montoya have taken, and are continuing to take, to address such concerns.

All parties fully cooperated with the investigation and are actively working to implement these improvements and ensure a supportive and high-performing culture.

The investigation was conducted by Debevoise & Plimpton LLP. Current and former players and staff were interviewed as part of a comprehensive review.
