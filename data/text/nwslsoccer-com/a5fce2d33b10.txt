The Women's African Cup of Nations ended in mid-August, but the NWSL players who competed for the trophy and a place in the World Cup next summer are still trickling back to league action.

Matchweek 19 saw even more players welcomed back into the fold with moments including celebrations, debuts, returns, and golazos.​

Before the Washington Spirit's match against Bay FC, the club honored both team's WAFCON competitors, incluing Deborah Abiodun, Gift Monday, Rosemonde Kouassi, Monique Ngock, Mélissa Bethi, and Bay's Racheal Kundananji.

For Ngock and Bethi, it was their Rowdy Audi debut. Ngock signed with the Spirit just before the tournament started and joins her new club a champion. Cameroon defeated Malawi 3-0 in the final game of the tournament, and Ngock earned Women of the Competition honors.

Bethi made NWSL history when she subbed on in the second half of the match, becoming the first Algerian to play in league history.

Orlando's Barbra Banda got her first goal contribution since returning from the tournament in Matchweek 18, and against the Utah Royals, Banda got her first NWSL goal since July 10. She now has 13 on the season, and while competing in WAFCON, Banda put up five goals for Zambia.

Finally, the WAFCON Golden Boot winner and the pride of Malawi returned to Kansas City. Temwa Chawinga made her first appearance on the pitch at CPKC Stadium since the tournament ended and was given a hero's welcome by the Current faithful.

Temwa and her sister, Tabitha Chawinga, led the historic run for Malawai in their first ever appearance in the tournament. Temwa's five goals and two assists earned her the tournament's Golden Boot and a place in the tournament's final, where Malawi lost to Cameroon.

The Chawinga sisters punched Malawi's ticket to the World Cup for the first time, and if you need any proof of what that means for the country, just see watch Temwa's personal cheering section.

How can you not be romantic about soccer?
