NEW YORK (May 31, 2023) –

Pro Sports Assembly (PRO), the association for professional sports business leaders, is proud to announce the National Women’s Soccer League has joined PRO as its latest member. <br>PRO is a member-led association with a core purpose of advancing diverse and inclusive leadership. By developing cultural and gender intelligence, as well as establishing best practices for advocacy and allyship, professional sports properties can drive innovation, bolster financial performance, capture new markets and increase brand loyalty. <br>Teams, leagues, unions and venues that are members of PRO set standards for leadership, while supporting the development of talent who are most knowledgeable and accountable for delivering a fair and profitable future in professional sports organizations. <br>“I’m excited to see the NWSL, a powerhouse in sports, join PRO.” said Ann Seeney, Governing Board Chair of Pro Sports Assembly and Chief People Officer of the US Soccer Federation. “We look forward to building this partnership and working side by side to continue creating a more equitable industry, provide more opportunities for professional development, and support the next generation of inclusive leaders.” <br>“We are honored to join forces with this incredible organization and look forward to working with other trailblazers in the field to develop best practices, promote an inclusive work environment and empower individuals from all backgrounds to become exceptional leaders,” said Chief People and Culture Officer Lauren Lopez. “This partnership aligns perfectly with the NWSL’s core values and mission and will be instrumental in supporting our efforts to create meaningful change across the professional sports landscape.”
