The NWSL is back for its 2025 season, kicking off on Friday, March 14th, and promising another electrifying year of unforgettable moments. With fresh faces eager to make their mark, record-breaking feats on the horizon, and rivalries heating up, the 2025 season is set to raise the bar. As teams battle for supremacy and players chase their legacies, every match will be a new chapter in a story of grit, passion, and triumph. Get ready—the road to NWSL glory begins now.

Following a dominant 2024 season that saw Gotham claim third place with 56 points, the team is ready for another deep run. Returning stars include top scorer Esther, playmaker Rose Lavelle, and the clinical Ella Stevens. Defensive stalwarts Tierna Davidson and Emily Sonnett protect the backline, while reigning NWSL Goalkeeper of the Year Ann-Katrin Berger locks down the net. New additions like Jaelin Howell, Brazilian forward Gabi Portilho, and two-time All-American and three-time conference Defender of the Year Lilly Reale from UCLA bring depth to a squad looking to lift silverware.

Final Standing: 3rd place
