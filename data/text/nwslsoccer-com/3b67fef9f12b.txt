NEW YORK (September 4, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today the 11 players named to the Best XI of the Month, presented by Prime, for the month of August by the NWSL Media Association and broadcast talent. Headlined by Player of the Month Ashley Sanchez, 8 teams are represented in the league’s Top 11, including three clubs who have two players represented.

GK – Ann-Katrin Berger (GFC): Recorded two clean sheets and had 12 saves in four matches

D – Janine Sonis (DEN): Tallied five goal contributions (3G, 2A) in six matches from fullback

D – Tierna Davidson (GFC): Anchored a Gotham back line that conceded just twice in August

D – Sarah Gorden (LA): Had nine tackles and five interceptions across six matches in the month

D – Lauren Milliet (LOU): Totalled six goal contributions (1G, 5A) in six matches in August

M – Croix Bethune (KC): Had two assists and scored a goal in five matches

M – Mina Tanaka (UTA): Found the back of the net three times in August, including a brace against San Diego

M – Kat Rader (HOU): Had three goals in the month, including scoring two against Chicago

F – Ashley Sanchez (NC): Scored five goals and had two assists in seven matches in the month

F – Evelyn Ijeh (NC): Had four goals in the month, including a brace against Washington

F – Emma Sears (LOU): Tallied seven goal contributions (5G, 2A) in six matches, including two braces
