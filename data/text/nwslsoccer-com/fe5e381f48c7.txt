NEW YORK (June 30, 2023) -

The National Women’s Soccer League announced today it has issued a fine to Chicago Red Stars head coach Chris Petrucelli for comments detrimental to the league made on social media on June 26, 2023, in violation of Section 12.2.5 (e) “Prejudicial Statements and Public Criticism” of the League Operations Manual.
