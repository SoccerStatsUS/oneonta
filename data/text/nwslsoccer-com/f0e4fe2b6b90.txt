It was a long 654 days without Sydney Leroux on the pitch, but on August 16, she made the biggest comeback of her career. After nearly two years and a season away from soccer, she subbed on during the second half Angel City FC's match against the Washington Spirit.

Leroux stepped away from soccer ahead of the 2025 season for her mental health saying at the time:

"Anyone who knows me knows I'm a fighter – I always have been. I take pride in showing up, in giving everything I have, but right now I owe it to myself and my children to take a step back and take care of me."

When she ran onto the field at BMO Stadium, the ACFC faithful gave Leroux the warm welcome she deserved, and after the game, she took to social media to show what just meant it.

"I don’t know how to explain 654 days away from the game. No broken bone. No torn ligament. No scar to point to and say, this is why. Just 654 days of questions. Of almost. Of not yet. Of watching the game move on while I stood still. Somewhere along the way, 'when I come back' quietly became 'if.' But I stepped onto that field again." ​

Leroux is one of three original NWSL players still rostered in the league (Jess Fishlock, Alyssa Naeher). She's seen and done it all throughout her soccer career, but her journey back to the pitch is one that will standout.

"The most important comeback in my life. 654 days tried to make me a memory and I came back as proof," she wrote on Threads. "Thank you for never giving up on me."

Welcome back, Syd.
