Sorry, Trinity Rodman can't come to the phone right now. She's busy hitting the celly.

Friday's Rowdy Audi crowd couldn't have been rewarded with a more electric game back for the Washington Spirit. After two weeks off for the international window, the Washington Spirit got their first W at home in an epic 4-0 win over the Kansas City Current that included two goal contributions from Rodman.

She assisted the first of Leicy Santos's goals, but before the end of the first half, Rodman got her first goal of the season to double the Spirit's lead. When Current keeper Lorena mishandled a shot, Rodman was in the right place at the right time making it look easy.

With her tongue out and a little cha-cha move, Rodman's teammates surrounded her in celebration. Then she walked to the sideline, holding her hand to ear like a phone and hung it up. It's a simple but iconic celly and an homage to her boyfriend, tennis player Ben Shelton.

It was already peak matchday atmosphere with former Spirit star Croix Bethune returning to D.C. with her new club. But add a Trin goal stoppage time goal? Forget about it.
