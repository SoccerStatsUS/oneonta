NEW YORK (August 6, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today that Washington Spirit Forward Trinity Rodman has been named the Player of the Month, for the month of July by the NWSL Media Association.

Rodman came out of the break having not missed a beat, scoring a brace in Washington’s first game back, including the match winner in the 94th minute. In the month’s six matches, Rodman had six goal contributions as Washington won five of those six games.

The NWSL Player of the Month is selected each month of the regular season by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league on a consistent basis, and broadcast talent. Awards for the month of July are based off performances during the regular season matches.
