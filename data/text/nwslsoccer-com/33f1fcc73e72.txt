The National Women’s Soccer League is back on CBS, CBS Sports Network, and Paramount+ for the 2025 season, bringing fans more live coverage than ever before. CBS will air 14 matches, including 11 regular-season games and three playoff matchups, culminating in the NWSL Championship on Nov. 22. Additional games will be available on CBS Sports Network, Paramount+, and CBS Sports Golazo Network, ensuring fans can follow the action all season long.

The CBS coverage kicks off on Saturday, April 26, at 1 p.m. ET when the Washington Spirit hosts Gotham FC at Audi Field on CBS.
