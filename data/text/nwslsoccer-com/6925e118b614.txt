“NWSL Talking Pride” presented by Google Pixel, a panel featuring former NWSL athlete Ali Krieger and other leaders in the LGBTQIA+ community will stream live Thursday, June 13 at 3 pm ET from the Stonewall National Monument Visitors Center in New York City- the first event at the center ahead of its grand opening on June 28. In honor of Pride month, this panel will feature prominent members of the LGBTQIA+ community, taking part in a moderated conversation on harnessing the power of sports as a catalyst for change.

The panel will be moderated by Forbes Under 30 Honoree and ESPN Women’s Sports Commentator, Arielle Chambers with participants including:

2x World Cup Champion, NWSL Champion and ESPN Analyst, Ali Krieger

Olympic Gold Medalist, 8x World Champion and ESPN Analyst, Hilary Knight

Pride Live Founder/CEO, Diana Rodriguez

Athlete and Activist, Chris Mosier

The event will stream live on <a href="https://plus.nwslsoccer.com/" target="_blank">NWSL+</a> and the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@NWSLsoccer" target="_blank">NWSL's YouTube channel</a>.
