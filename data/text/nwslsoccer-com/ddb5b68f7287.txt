When: Saturday, March 22nd - 7:30 pm ET

How to watch: ION

READ: How to Watch the NWSL on ION

Saturday night’s showdown between the Washington Spirit and Kansas City Current is one you won’t want to miss. Two of the NWSL’s most electric teams are squaring off under the lights at Audi Field, and the vibes are already immaculate. Whether you're a die-hard fan or a casual viewer looking for some late-night chaos, here’s why this game needs to be on your watchlist.

There’s nothing quite like a night at Audi Field—just ask anyone who’s been. Last season, the Spirit turned D.C. into one of the rowdiest home environments in the league, regularly packing the stadium and selling out playoff matches. That energy is expected to carry into 2025, with fans bringing the heat from whistle to whistle. Watching from home? Turn up the volume and soak it in and make sure to join early for ION’s pregame show, where Lisa Carlin and the team will be on-site to get you closer to the action.

This matchup is as even as it gets. In 2024, the two sides split their series—Washington claimed a dominant 4-1 win in D.C., while KC answered back with a 3-0 shutout at home. Both clubs kicked off their 2025 campaigns with strong wins last weekend, signaling another season of high-level competition. Expect fireworks in this one.

Star power? Check. Two of the league’s most electrifying talents will share the pitch Saturday night. Temwa Chawinga can score from anywhere and does it with style, while Trinity Rodman brings unmatched swagger and highlight-reel footwork. They’re both fan favorites for a reason—and you’ll see exactly why when they go head-to-head.

It’s finally here. After years of fan (and player) anticipation, the Current are taking the field in a proper teal kit for the first time ever. Nike delivered, and now it’s time for the team to debut their fresh threads under the lights.

If you're tuning in for Washington Spirit vs. Kansas City Current, keep your eyes on Rebeca Bernal. The Mexican international and longtime C.F. Monterrey captain is already showing why she was one of the biggest offseason internationa signings. Fresh off helping the Spirit lift the 2025 Challenge Cup, Bernal made her NWSL regular-season debut last week, anchoring the backline with poise and precision in a hard-fought win. With over 250 appearances for Monterrey, four Liga MX Femenil titles, and 65+ goals from the back line, Bernal brings a rare blend of experience, leadership, and attacking threat. As Washington looks to build on last season’s record-setting run, Bernal will be a key factor in containing KC’s high-powered attack—and a must-watch for fans across the world.

With fans ready for NWSL chaos after a long offseason, this will surely be the perfect game to light up your Saturday night. Don’t miss the action this Saturday at 7:30 PM ET on ION!
