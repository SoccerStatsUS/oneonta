SAN DIEGO, Calif.

– San Diego Wave FC defeated Portland Thorns FC, 1-0, at home on Wednesday night to earn a big time victory in their first Challenge Cup match of this year’s competition. In the end, it was an own goal from Portland’s Meaghan Nally that separated the two sides, but a standup performance by San Diego goalkeeper Kailen Sheridan saw her make an incredible nine saves to help her side earn the shutout victory in front of an excited nearly 10,000 fans in attendance. <br>Portland were the more attacking of the two sides from the outset, but it was San Diego who first had a shot on goal saved as forward Sofia Jakobsson tested Portland goalkeeper Shelby Hogan with an effort from just inside the box in the 18 <sup>th</sup> minute. Splitting a pair of defender, Jakobsson worked her way down the left side of the field and into the penalty area where she opened her right foot and blasted a shot toward the near post. However, Hogan was able to leap and collect the effort, ending San Diego’s attempt. <br>One minute later, the first of many impressive saves from Sheridan came as she was required to produce a strong palm on Portland forward Sophia Smith’s effort in the 19 <sup>th</sup> minute. Smith had broken into the box and created just enough space to have a look on goal, and she rocketed a powerful drive toward the near post that Sheridan was ultimately equal to to keep the game level. <br>After a brief back-and-forth in possession, San Diego’s next best attempt came in the 39 <sup>th</sup> minute as forward Rachel Hill looked as if she had beaten the offside trap as she attempted to latch onto a through ball from teammate Kelsey Turnbow. Hill then got a shot away that Hogan was able to keep out, only for the offside flag to be raised and end the move anyhow. At the end of the half, San Diego then got one more crack through Jakobsson in a similar position as her 18 <sup>th</sup> minute strike, but this effort sailed wide of the far post and out as the pair went into the halftime break all square. <br>The second half saw far more of Portland’s attack as defender Natalie Kuikka was first to test Sheridan with a strong left-footed shot just outside the box in the 51 <sup>st</sup> minute, though the chance was collected. <br>The match’s momentum then took a huge swing in San Diego’s favor as forward Jaedyn Shaw received a pass from Jakobsson and lined up a shot from just inside the box to the right. However, Shaw’s shot did not have a clear path toward goal and took a gnarly deflection off the Thorns defender Nally before hitting the back of the net. <br>Portland then gave their best efforts in trying to find an equalizer, with one of those attempts coming in the 86 <sup>th</sup> minute as forward Morgan Weaver fired a shot on goal that Sheridan was equal to in the end. Then, into the last thirty seconds of stoppage time, Portland had one more attempt to bring things level as Olivia Moultrie’s cross landed on the head of Rocky Rodriguez, but the midfielder’s effort did not have near the power it needed to to get past Sheridan and in. <br>In the end, San Diego withstood the barrage of efforts from Portland and Nally’s own goal in the 65 <sup>th</sup> minute decided the match in San Diego’s favor. The win was San Diego’s second-ever in Challenge Cup competition (4-2 vs. Angel City last year). <br>
