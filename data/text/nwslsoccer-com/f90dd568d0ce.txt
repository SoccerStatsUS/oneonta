BRIDGEVIEW, Ill.

– The OL Reign capped off their regular season by defeating the Chicago Red Stars, 3-0, at SeatGeek Stadium on Sunday night to earn a playoff berth as Megan Rapinoe’s career extends to postseason play. Rapinoe, who played her final regular season home match last weekend, scored both of Reign’s first two goals before teammate Jess Fishlock added the third and final with the Reign now facing Angel City next weekend. <br>The Reign nearly added their first goal of the night through Rapinoe in the 18 <sup>th</sup> minute when some calamitous defending by the Red Stars opened up a chance for the forward in the box. However, great recovering defending resulted in Rapinoe’s effort being blocked, and a follow up chance just a minute later by Fishlock was also repelled away. <br>The two teams would remain scoreless through the halftime break, but not for long into the second half as the Reign opened the scoring just 75 seconds into the restart. Coming out of the half and looking to attack immediately, Rapinoe was fed on the left side of the box where she was able to cut in to a central position and fire on goal. Taking aim from 19 yards out, Rapinoe curled her effort around Chicago goalkeeper Alyssa Naeher and in to take a 1-0 lead. <br>Two minutes later, Rapinoe was up to her old tricks again, but this time from a few yards closer in. She received a pass inside the box on the left side where she worked the ball to her right foot and dinked a bending effort toward the far post, off the inside of the post, and into the back of the net as the visitors saw their lead extended to two goals in the span of three minutes. <br>Finally, just after the hour mark, the Reign added their third goal after VAR review as Fishlock latched onto a through ball in behind the defense and rolled a low effort past Naeher, who was left defenseless in goal. From the early looks, Fishlock’s run was deceptive in making her look offside. However, after review, the front leg of a defender on the far side of the field kept her on and she was able to tuck home the team’s third. <br>That three-goal lead would be good enough to roll through fulltime as the Reign return to the postseason again this year, now facing a first round matchup against Angel City at home next week. <br>
