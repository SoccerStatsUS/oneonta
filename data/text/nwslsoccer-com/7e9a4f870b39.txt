To round out their roster over the offseason, the Stars added Spanish midfielder Maitane and Japan U-20 captain Manaka Hayashi, who brings international pedigree after leading Japan to two runners-up finishes in 2024. With fresh talent and a strengthened foundation, Chicago is primed to shine under its new banner.
