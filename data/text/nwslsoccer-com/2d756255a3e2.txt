The National Women’s Soccer League welcomed 56 new players to the league at the 2024 NWSL Draft, presented by Ally, last Friday.Below is a collection of notables to know about the draftees and a recap of the 11 picks that were traded on draft day, separated by round. More information about the 2024 NWSL Draft, presented by Ally, can be found here.

First Round Recap

First Round Draft Day Trades:

Washington Spirit acquired the No. 3 overall pick from the Chicago Red Stars in exchange for defender Sam Staab.

Washington Spirit acquired the No. 5 overall pick and $250,000 in Allocation Money from the North Carolina Courage in exchange for Midfielder Ashley Sanchez.

Chicago Red Stars acquired the No. 10 overall pick from the North Carolina Courage in exchange for $175,000 in Allocation Money.

Second Round Recap

Second Round Draft Day Trades:

Chicago Red Stars acquired the No. 15 overall pick and $125,000 in Allocation Money from Racing Louisville FC in exchange for defender Arin Wright.

Seattle Reign FC acquired the No. 17 overall pick from the Chicago Red Stars in exchange for $125,000 in Allocation Money.

Houston Dash acquired the No. 21 overall pick from Utah Royals FC in exchange for $120,000 in Allocation Money.

Third Round Recap

Third Round Draft Day Trades:

Houston Dash acquired the No. 38 overall pick from the North Carolina Courage in exchange for the No. 40 overall pick and $10,000 in Allocation Money.

San Diego Wave FC acquired the No. 42 overall pick from Racing Louisville FC in exchange for the No. 54 overall pick and $40,000 in Allocation Money.

Fourth Round Recap

Fourth Round Draft Day Trades:

Angel City FC acquired the No. 44 overall pick from Utah Royals FC in exchange for $15,000 in Allocation Money.

Racing Louisville FC acquired the No. 54 overall pick and $40,000 in Allocation Money from San Diego Wave FC in exchange for the No. 42 overall pick.
