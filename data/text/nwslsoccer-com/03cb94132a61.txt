NEW YORK (August 18, 2023) -

The National Women's Soccer League today announced that San Diego Wave FC's home match against NJ/NY Gotham FC, originally scheduled for a 10 p.m. ET start this Saturday at Snapdragon Stadium, will now kick off at 8 p.m. ET to avoid adverse weather conditions due to Hurricane Hilary. <br>The match remains available to stream on Paramount+ and across the NWSL’s international platforms. <br>
