NEW YORK (June 4, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today that Kansas City Current Forward Temwa Chawinga has been named the Player of the Month for the month of May by the NWSL Media Association.

The back-to-back MVP and Golden Boot winner was back to her goalscoring ways in her first full month of action after missing the beginning of the season due to injury. Highlighted by a hat trick against Chicago and backed up by a brace against Houston six days later, the Malawian tallied seven goals in six matches across the month.

The NWSL Player of the Month is selected each month of the regular season by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league on a consistent basis, and broadcast talent. Awards for the month of May are based on performances during the regular season matches.
