NEW YORK (May 5, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today the 11 players named to the NWSL Best XI of the Month, presented by Amazon Prime, for the month of April, headlined by NWSL Player of the Month April Ashley Sanchez of the North Carolina Courage and players from seven NWSL clubs.

GK - Sandy MacIver (WAS): Earned three clean sheets in three games in the month

D - Sam Hiatt (POR): An integral part of a Portland backline that helped the team go unbeaten in April with four tackles and three interceptions

D - Esme Morgan (WAS): Played every minute for a Washington team that did not concede a single goal in the month

D - Tara Rudd (WAS): Paired with Morgan, the Spirit captain helped lead the team to three clean sheets in three games

D - Kennedy Wesley (SD): Anchoring the San Diego defense, Wesley played every minute and scored her first goal of the year in April

M - Ashley Sanchez (NC): The April Player of the Month scored a goal in each of the Courage’s three games in the month, including two equalizers and a game winner

M - Leicy Santos (WAS): The Colombian playmaker tallied two goals in April, a brace against Kansas City

M - Rose Lavelle (GFC): Lavelle scored in both of Gotham’s wins in the month, her first two tallies of the season

F - Trinity Rodman (WAS): Rodman scored two goals and added an assist in April, totalling three goal contributions in three games for the winger

F - Barbra Banda (ORL): Tied for the lead in the Golden Boot race at the end of the month, Banda scored twice in the month

F - Kiki van Zanten (HOU): The Jamaica international found the back of the net twice for Houston against Louisville, marking her second brace of the season

As the presenting sponsor of the NWSL Best XI, Amazon Prime is bringing fans closer to the players they love by placing the spotlight on the excellence of the athletes selected for this honor. Prime encourages members to explore their passions and interests through the entertainment they watch, the music they listen to, and the things they like to buy. You could say: Whatever you’re into, It’s on Prime. Prime Video’s NWSL coverage continues every Friday night throughout the regular season.

The NWSL Best XI of the Month is selected each month of the regular season by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league on a consistent basis, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent.
