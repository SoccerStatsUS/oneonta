It’s time for the Summer Cup semifinals. Four of the best teams from the tournament are set to face off, with the winners facing off in the final on October 25th in San Antonio, TX. Here’s what fans have to look forward to!

How do I watch live games from the NWSL x LIGA MX Femenil Summer Cup?

CBS Sports will be the exclusive English-language distributor of all 33 Summer Cup games, with every match set to stream live on Paramount+, 11 matches simulcasting on CBS Sports Golazo Network and 12 matches simulcasting on CBS Sports Network, including the semifinals and championship match.

TUDN, the sports programming branch of Spanish-language network Univision, will air three group stage matches while NWSL+ will serve as the Spanish-language home for the remaining group stage matches in the U.S.

NJ/NY Gotham FC vs. Angel City FC - 5:00 PM ET

Where to watch: CBSSN, Paramount+

Despite missing their USWNT players, who are away at the Olympics, Gotham’s squad has excelled defensively, allowing zero goals, and their offensive play has improved as the tournament progressed. However, scoring remains a concern with only four goals in three matches.

Angel City FC has also defended admirably, achieving clean sheets in two of their three group stage matches and conceding only one goal in their first game. Like Gotham, they have scored just four goals, but the return of star forward Christen Press from injury could provide a significant boost to their attack.

Kansas City Current vs. North Carolina Courage - 9:00 PM ET

Where to watch: CBSSN, Paramount+

The Kansas City Current have been prolific in the Summer Cup, scoring 10 goals in just 3 matches. This continues their regular season trend of consistently finding the back of the net, with contributions from a variety of players.

Meanwhile, the North Carolina Courage struggled to secure victories in regular time for two of their matches, relying on penalty shootouts to advance. This may have hinted at potential scoring issues, but their recent performance, featuring 3 quality goals in their last match, suggests they may have regained their offensive rhythm, making this semifinal matchup one to look forward to.
