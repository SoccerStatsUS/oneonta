…but first: one last weekend of NWSL chaos before summer break. Matchweek 10 is the final chance for clubs to grab three points and head into July feeling good about where they stand. Some teams enter the weekend fresh off statement wins, gritty results, and the return of goal-scoring juggernauts, while others are still looking for answers in front of goal and on the backline. With momentum and valuable points still on the line, here’s what to expect in Matchweek 10 before the league presses pause until July.

Orlando Pride vs. Bay FC \- 7 p.m. ET

How to watch: NWSL+

A morale-boosting win against highly-ranked San Diego last week gives the Orlando Pride some forward momentum going into the last match before the summer break. In a truly chaotic game against the Chicago Stars last week, Bay FC went down to nine players and lost 1-0. That means they will be without starting goalkeeper Jordan Silkowitz and defender Aldana Cometti against Orlando, who have an opportunity to unleash Golden Boot leader Barbra Banda against a backup keeper Emmie Allen. But all hope is not lost. Bay has a deep roster of attacking talent: Racheal Kundananji, Hannah Bebar, Claire Hutton, Cristiana Girelli, Dorian Bailey… the list goes on. Yes, things may be a little shaky in the back missing a defender and a starting goalkeeper and goals have been far and few between this season, but if Bay can sustain a strong attack, it may not matter.

Racing Louisville FC vs. Denver Summit FC \- 8 p.m. ET

How to watch: Prime Video

Racing Louisville enters this weekend in last place after a narrow 2-1 loss to the North Carolina Courage. The Denver Summit also sustained a close loss to the Utah Royals, 2-1. Louisville needs to deploy Katie O’Kane against Denver. She’s a professional at finding and exploiting space, and the numbers show it: she’s tied for second in most goals scored and second in assists for the club this season. The gaps in Denver’s defense are ripe for a player like O’Kane to succeed in.

Speaking of Denver’s defense, their key to the match will be defensive determination. The Palacios goal scored against the Summit last week was a result of the defense not fully recovering after getting the ball stolen from them. Denver’s defense can’t get caught ball-watching again if they want to take home the last three points before summer break.

Kansas City Current vs. Boston Legacy FC \- 1:30 p.m. ET

How to watch: ION

After their dominant 3-1 win over the Portland Thorns last week, the Kansas City Current is hoping to keep rolling. On the other side, the Boston Legacy’s five-game unbeaten streak came to an end last weekend when they lost 2-1 to Seattle. It’s crucial for Kansas City to not get too comfortable against a team like Boston, who are incredibly unpredictable and excel during moments of chaos. Boston’s key to the match is simple: defend against Kansas City’s dynamic striker Temwa Chawinga and keep her contained in the midfield. They also lead the league in conceding penalties, something they need to be smart about this weekend. If they handle business defensively, the team is free to put its energy into scoring.

Portland Thorns FC vs. Utah Royals FC \- 4 p.m. ET

How to watch: ION

This top of the table matchup is one every NWSL fan will be watching with interest. At their best, both teams have high-flying offensive lines that don’t take no for an answer. Portland has the ever-dangerous Sophia Wilson and Pietra Tordin, while Utah has the firepower of Mina Tanaka and Cloé Lacasse. Each pair sits at seven goals scored this season. In net is an equally exciting matchup: Mandy McGlynn for Utah against Mackenzie Arnold for Portland. Both are in the top five for fewest average goals against. This match will be a war of attrition for both teams - the key will be using substitutes as game changers. Whatever side can use their substitutes effectively will triumph.

Washington Spirit vs. Seattle Reign \- 6:30 p.m. ET

How to watch: ION

Washington and Seattle last met May 10, where the Spirit got the win in a gritty 1-0 match. What will this rematch look like? The Spirit are coming off a hectic defeat in the Concacaf W Champions Cup final. The Reign won their first match since March 28 last week against Boston Legacy, and they looked clinical every step of the way. It’s essential for the Spirit to stay disciplined defensively against the Reign, who are surely soaring after that win. But one thing is for sure: the Spirit can score. Their offense is top five in the league for both goals and shots on goal. If they can focus defensively, it will stop Seattle in its tracks.

Seattle needs to get Maddie Dahlien on the ball as much as possible. She contributed to both goals last weekend, scoring one and dribbling into the box to gain a penalty. Her tenacity is exactly what a team gaining momentum like Seattle needs.

Chicago Stars vs. San Diego Wave \- 1 p.m. ET

How to watch: CBS Sports

The Chicago Stars got their first win since April 25 this past weekend against a nine-player Bay FC. Meanwhile, San Diego were upset by Orlando 1-0. Chicago’s success in the Bay game came chiefly from the second-half inclusion of forward Mallory Swanson, who scored the only goal of the match. While Swanson is still coming back from maternity leave and can’t play 90 minutes, every minute she’s on the pitch Chicago becomes a more assured team. Her workrate is undeniable; in only 44 minutes on the pitch this game, she scored a goal. Chicago needs to utilize Swanson as much as possible to give them every chance to succeed against a robust San Diego Wave.

It’s crucial for San Diego to tighten up defensively. Recently many of the goals scored against them were the result of too much space between the defenders. San Diego’s offense is fantastic - they lead the league in both goals and shots on target. If their defense can stay compact, they’ll be unstoppable.

Gotham FC vs. Houston Dash \- 3 p.m. ET

How to watch: ESPN App

Fresh off competing in the Concacaf W Champions Cup, Gotham will be eager to get a win in domestic play. Meanwhile, Houston is looking to continue to build positive momentum after a 2-1 win over Angel City this past weekend. Gotham will likely be without star goalkeeper Ann-Katrin Berger after she sustained an injury in the Champions Cup. Because of this, Gotham’s defense will need to be even more vigilant against this energetic Houston attack. They currently lead the league in clean sheets and goals conceded, so they just need to stay consistent and not let Houston rattle them.

Houston needs to keep building confidence in their young attacking players like Kat Rader, Kate Faasse, and Maggie Graham. Especially with key attacking piece Kiki Van Zanten still out, it’s imperative that the Dash give as much time as possible to the other offensive players. Both Rader and Graham have come into their own as the season has progressed, rounding out the top three goalscorers for the Dash this season alongside Van Zanten. Whenever she comes back, the Dash will have a much-more well-rounded attack.

Angel City FC vs. North Carolina Courage \- 7 p.m. ET

How to watch: Victory+

Last week Angel City suffered a chippy 2-1 defeat to Houston, and North Carolina won 2-1 over Racing Louisville. Both teams have the same number of goals scored, goals conceded, and wins. The key to the match for Angel City is simple: exploit as many gaps as possible. Many of Angel City’s best chances come from running players like Gisele Thompson or Kennedy Fuller through the gaps in a team’s back line. If they can find and manipulate that space, they can cause chaos in the box and easily score on the Courage.

On the other side of the field, North Carolina’s offense is, to put it mildly, doing well.. Ashley Sanchez is currently second in the Golden Boot race and Manaka Matsukubo is in the top five for assists and shots on goal for the entire league. But there’s one player who’s quietly been on the rise for the Courage: Evelyn Ijeh. The Swedish striker scored in both of her last two matches, and her combination play with Sanchez and Manaka keeps getting better. The Courage needs to keep using Ijeh as their secret weapon if they want to get the win against Angel City and head into the summer break on a high note.
