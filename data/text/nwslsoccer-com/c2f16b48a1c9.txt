Get your biscuits and your “Believe” signs: Ted Lasso is back.

This time everyone’s favorite fictional coach is leading AFC Richmond’s new women’s club – at the behest of some old friends. The first episode of the fourth season dropped on August 5, and NWSL fans will see some familiar faces.

The season starts off in Kansas City, the hometown of both Lasso and the actor who plays him, Jason Sudeikis. As any NWSL fan knows, a trip to Kansas City isn't complete without a trip to CPKC Stadium.

As it turns out, the NWSL looms large in the first episode, and when the show premiered in Los Angeles and New York City, players from Angel City FC, Gotham FC, and the Boston Legacy walked the red carpet, met the stars of the show, and celebrated the moment for women’s soccer.

Gotham players like Jordynn Dudley, Mandy Freeman, Teagan Wy, Sophia Cook, and Andrea Kitahata, as well as Carolyn Tisch Blodgett, walked the red carpet ahead of the premiere event in New York on August 4.

Chloe Ricketts, Laís Araújo, Andressa Karolaine and General Manager Domènec Guasch were among those who made the trip down from Boston to represent the Legacy.

Before the premiere in Brooklyn on August 4, the cast and crew celebrated the new season with a premiere in Los Angeles, and Angel City FC players came out in full force for the moment.

The show celebrated its Los Angeles premiere on July 27, and Angel City players Riley Tiernan, Hannah Seabert, Karsyn Cherry, and Carina Lageyre were on hand for the party.

The night before, the newest cast members returned the favor by showing up to support Angel City at their match against Racing Louisville.

New episodes of “Ted Lasso” are available to stream on Apple TV every Wednesday.
