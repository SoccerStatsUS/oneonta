The NWSL is back for its 2025 season, kicking off on Friday, March 14th, and promising another electrifying year of unforgettable moments. With fresh faces eager to make their mark, record-breaking feats on the horizon, and rivalries heating up, the 2025 season is set to raise the bar. As teams battle for supremacy and players chase their legacies, every match will be a new chapter in a story of grit, passion, and triumph. Get ready—the road to NWSL glory begins now.

Known for their possession-heavy, fluid style, North Carolina Courage heads into 2025 with exciting attacking options. Ashley Sanchez, the team’s assist leader last year, returns alongside young international talent Aline Gomes and Manaka Matsukubo. Dynamic new signings Jaedyn Shaw and Japanese internationals Shinomi Toyama might very well be the difference maker this season, with Kaleigh Kurtz, the team’s 2024 Iron Woman, anchoring the defense alongside promising collegiate addition Brooklyn Courtnall. With their balanced mix of experience and youth, the Courage is aiming for another playoff run.
