NEW YORK (June 6, 2023) -

The National Women’s Soccer League announced today that NJ/NY Gotham FC defender/midfielder Jenna Nighswonger has been named Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, for the month of May by the NWSL Media Association and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. <br>Appearing both on the back line and in the midfield during the month, the No. 4 overall pick from the 2023 NWSL Draft, presented by Ally scored her first career goal on May 7, notching the game-opening tally in Gotham FC’s 2-1 win against the Chicago Red Stars. <br>The following match, the Huntington Beach, California native scored her second goal in league play on May 21, opening scoring for Gotham FC once again in the club’s 4-1 victory over OL Reign. <br>Nighswonger’s offensive production also included an assist on teammate Lynn Williams’ game-tying goal against the North Carolina Courage, helping secure a draw on the road for Gotham FC. <br>Nighswonger appeared in 283 minutes of game action throughout the month, starting in four matches, registering 64 successful passes and winning 16 duels. <br>The NWSL Rookie of the Month presented by Ally is selected each month of the regular season by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league on a consistent basis, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent.
