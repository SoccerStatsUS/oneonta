The NWSL called, and Columbus answered.

The league awarded the city the 18th expansion club bid. The club will kick off its inaugural season in 2028 along with the 17th expansion team in Atlanta.

NWSL Commissioner Jessica Berman was on hand at ScottsMiracle-Gro Field to announce the league’s decision. The ownership group comprises Haslam Sports Group, Nationwide, and Drs. Christine and Pete Edwards

“We are setting a new standard for what's possible in women's soccer, with a powerful signal of where women's sports is headed. Columbus is that next chapter in the story,” Berman said. “This is a city that has long been at the heart of American soccer, a community that understands the power of sport and the power of community.”

Columbus has a long history of celebrating the beautiful game beyond the months-long campaign to bring the NWSL to the city. From the coordinated effort to “Save the Crew” in 2017 to a rich history with both the men’s and women’s U.S. national teams, the heart of soccer in America might just run through Ohio.

“We believe sports are one of the greatest unifiers of today's world and tremendous for the cities that they are a part of,” Haslam Sports Group Managing Partner Whitney Haslam Johnson said. “We believe in the power of women's sports and are honored to be part of the No. 1 women's soccer league in the world.”

“At the NWSL, we often say, ‘This is not a moment, it's a movement,’” Berman said. “But it is moments like today that ensure that this movement continues.”
