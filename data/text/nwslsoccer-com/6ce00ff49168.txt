A San Diego Wave offense that’s scored five goals in three games is about to get reinforcements. The club announced the signing of former Chelsea striker Catarina Macario on Friday morning.

Macario joins the Wave with a contract that runs through 2030.

Signing with San Diego is a return home for Macario, who spent time in the city after moving from Brazil. She played for local youth clubs near the very pitches the Wave train on.

“It’s amazing to see all of \[my family’s\] sacrifices paying off. San Diego means so much to me. It’s where I grew up,” Macario said in an emotional video released by the Wave. “It’s where I became a young woman, where I learned to speak English and made lifelong friends. To be back here and actually represent the city and play for San Diego, it means a lot to me.”

She became eligible for the U.S. Women’s National Team after becoming a U.S. citizen in 2020, making her first appearance in 2021 and winning a bronze medal at the Tokyo Olympics. Macario has since scored 16 goals and earned five assists in 29 appearances. Half of those goals came in 2025 alone.

Macario signed with Chelsea in 2023, scoring just six minutes into her debut and then again off the bench in her second appearance. Before Chelsea, Macario spent two and a half years in Lyon, where she scored in the club’s UEFA Women’s Champions League final win against FC Barcelona.

At Stanford, Macario earned the MAC Hermann Trophy in back-to-back seasons, becoming just the sixth woman to win multiple times.
