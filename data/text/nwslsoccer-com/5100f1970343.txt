NEW YORK (November 6, 2025) – Today, the National Women’s Soccer League (NWSL) announced the formation of the NWSL Advisory Board, a first-of-its-kind initiative with a collective of investors, cultural leaders, and soccer legends united by a shared commitment to accelerate the league’s growth, deepen its cultural impact, and shape the future of the women’s game.

Conceptualized and led by Commissioner Jessica Berman, NWSL clubs are represented by up to two delegates. The Advisory Board brings together some of the most influential voices in sports, entertainment, and business—all of whom are current investors in NWSL clubs. Together, they’ll help the league unlock new opportunities for partnership, expand fan reach, and strengthen its position as both a premier professional league and a powerful cultural platform.

“When we looked across our Clubs’ investor base, we realized how fortunate we are to have such an extraordinary group of cultural icons, athletes, and leaders who believe – and have invested – in the power and potential of this league,” said NWSL Commissioner Jessica Berman. “Their experience and influence will be instrumental as we continue building not just a league, but a movement—one that redefines entertainment and what’s possible in sports.”

“Women’s sports are rewriting the rules of what’s possible — and the NWSL is leading that charge,” said Elizabeth Banks, Board Member and Investor, Boston Legacy FC. “This league has incredible athletes, incredible stories, and an incredible fan base. I’m proud to help amplify that energy and make sure the next generation sees women’s sports not as the exception, but as the standard.”

“The NWSL is one of the most exciting leagues in sports today, with growth that rivals anything we’ve seen before,” said Eli Manning, Board Member and Investor, Gotham FC. “I’m honored to be part of this Advisory Board and to help continue building a league that inspires communities, empowers women, and sets the standard for excellence on and off the field.”

“As players, we’ve seen firsthand how much the NWSL has grown and how much potential still lies ahead,” said Alex Morgan, Board Member and Investor, San Diego Wave FC. “What makes this Advisory Board so special is that it brings together people who share our belief in what women’s soccer can be — not just as a sport, but as a movement. I’m proud to be part of helping shape the next chapter for the league, the players, and the fans who make it all possible.”

Beginning in 2026, the Advisory Board will convene bi-annually to identify bold opportunities for growth, storytelling, and innovation. Working alongside existing ownership and leadership groups, the Board will help fuel collaboration and creativity across the NWSL ecosystem. The NWSL will be working together with Rich Kleiman and his team at Boardroom to program the inaugural summit in Spring 2026.

The creation of the Advisory Board marks another milestone in the NWSL’s continued evolution as the world’s premier women’s soccer league—and reinforces the league’s commitment to ensuring that the women’s game leads the future of sport.
