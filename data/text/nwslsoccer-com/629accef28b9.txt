We’re headed into Week 23 of the NWSL season and after the North Carolina Courage grabbed a playoff spot last weekend, the race for the final 3 spots is more intense than ever. This weekend, four teams could very well clinch the final three top-4 seeds, which would mean quarterfinal hosting. Meanwhile, four others are on the verge of elimination. Every match carries playoff implications, and the stakes couldn't be higher.

Washington Spirit can clinch a top-4 seed and a quarterfinal home match with:

A win or draw OR

A North Carolina draw or loss

NJ/NY Gotham FC secures a top-4 seed and a quarterfinal home match with:

A win or draw OR

A North Carolina draw or loss

Kansas City Current clinches a top-4 seed and a quarterfinal home match with:

A win OR

A North Carolina draw or loss

Chicago Red Stars can clinch a playoff spot with:

A win OR

Racing Louisville loss OR

A draw AND Portland draw or loss OR

A draw AND Louisville draw OR

Portland loss AND Louisville draw

Several teams are fighting for their playoff lives, with four facing possible elimination this weekend.

Seattle Reign FC will be eliminated with:

A loss or draw OR

A Portland win AND Bay FC win OR

A Portland draw AND Bay FC win AND Louisville win

Utah Royals will be eliminated with:

A loss OR

A Portland win AND Bay FC win OR

A draw AND Portland win or draw AND Bay FC win/draw OR

A draw AND Louisville win or draw AND Bay FC win/draw

Angel City FC will be eliminated with:

A loss AND Portland win or draw AND Bay FC win or draw OR

A loss AND Louisville win or draw AND Bay FC win or draw OR

A draw AND Portland win AND Bay FC win OR

A draw AND Portland draw AND Bay FC win AND Louisville win

San Diego Wave will be eliminated with:

A loss AND Portland win or draw AND Bay FC win or draw OR

A loss AND Louisville win or draw AND Bay FC win or draw OR

A draw AND Portland win AND Bay FC win OR

A draw AND Portland draw AND Bay FC win AND Louisville win

Orlando Pride have already secured the NWSL Shield and the top playoff seed, locking in home-field advantage throughout the playoffs.

Washington Spirit, NJ/NY Gotham FC, Kansas City Current, and North Carolina Courage have all clinched playoff spots but are still vying for a top-4 finish to secure a home quarterfinal match.
