NEW YORK (APRIL 2, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today that Angel City FC forwardSveindís Jónsdóttir has been named the NWSL Player of the Month for the month of March by the NWSL Media Association and the league’s on-air broadcast talent.

Jónsdóttir has started the year firing on all cylinders, having at least one goal contribution in each of Angel City's first three games, totalling three goals and two assists in the month.

Her trio of goals tie her for the league lead despite playing one match fewer than each of the other players who have scored three tallies this season.

The NWSL Player of the Month i**s selected by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league consistently, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. Awards for the month of March are based on performances during regular season matches.
