NEW YORK (September 3, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today that Houston Dash midfielder Kat Rader has been named Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, for the month of August by the NWSL Media Association and the league’s on-air broadcast talent.

The Duke product scored three goals in August, including a brace in the team’s 5-0 win against Chicago. Rader is now up to nine goals on the season, two shy of the league rookie record and one away from becoming the first Dash player to have double-digit goals in a season since Rachel Daly in 2018.

Ally, a financial services company with the nation's largest all-digital bank and a leading brand in women's sports, has been a primary sponsor of the NWSL since 2021 and serves as the official retail banking partner of the NWSLPA as well. Through its longstanding partnership, Ally has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to elevating women's soccer and enhancing the experience for athletes and fans alike.

The NWSL Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, is selected by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league consistently, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. Awards for the month of August are based on performances during the regular season matches.
