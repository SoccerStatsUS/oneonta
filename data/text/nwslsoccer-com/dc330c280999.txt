NEW YORK (August 6, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today that Houston Dash midfielder Linda Ullmark has been named Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, for the month of July by the NWSL Media Association and the league’s on-air broadcast talent.

Ullmark had two goals and two assists across four matches in the month, highlighted by a goal and an assist in Denver to help her team earn a point on the road, and the game winner in Houston’s 1-0 win against Bay FC.

Ally, a financial services company with the nation's largest all-digital bank and a leading brand in women's sports, has been a primary sponsor of the NWSL since 2021 and serves as the official retail banking partner of the NWSLPA as well. Through its longstanding partnership, Ally has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to elevating women's soccer and enhancing the experience for athletes and fans alike.

The NWSL Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, is selected by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league consistently, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. Awards for the month of July are based off performances during the regular season matches.
