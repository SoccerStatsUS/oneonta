Sunday’s showdown between Gotham FC and the Chicago Stars FC is shaping up to be a must-watch clash. Gotham returns home after a tough midweek loss in Portland followed up by a huge result against the Washington Spirit on the road. With international stars who thrive in clutch moments and two youthful squads full of players eager to prove themselves, this one has all the ingredients for a classic. Tune in on Sunday, May 4 at 1:00 PM ET on CBS and Paramount+ for all the action.

1\. Esther González Is on a Record-Breaking Tear

Gotham FC’s Golden Boot leader is making history in real time. Esther González has scored seven goals in her last four matches, including three braces, only ever done by three other NWSL players before—Christen Press (who did it twice), Sophia Smith, and Diana Ordóñez. She scored in the last matchup against Chicago and shows no signs of slowing down.

2\. Milestone Moments on the Horizon

Sunday could see history made on both sides. Gotham’s Ann-Katrin Berger is just one win away from tying the club record for goalkeeper victories—remarkably, in just 30 appearances compared to Kailen Sheridan’s 80. Midge Purce also sits one goal away from passing Carli Lloyd for third on Gotham’s all-time scoring list, trailing Esther González who sits in second at 18 goals. On the other side, Chicago’s Alyssa Naeher is chasing her 60th club win and 450th save—both league firsts—and could become just the second goalkeeper in NWSL history to reach 50 career shutouts. If you love watching greatness unfold, this is your match.

3\. Brazilian Face-Off

This match brings flair, footwork, and fire thanks to a dazzling group of Brazilian internationals. Gotham’s trio of Bruninha, Geyse, and Gabi Portilho offers a dynamic blend of creativity, technical skill, and pace. Portilho scored in her NWSL debut, while Geyse has added attacking bite since arriving from Manchester United on loan. On the other side, Chicago’s Ludmila is electric, blazing fast and with an arsenal of fun goal celebrations when she scores. With all four players capable of producing highlight-reel moments, this Brazilian battle is one you won’t want to miss.

4\. Ludmila + Jameese Joseph: Chicago’s Dynamic Duo

Chicago’s offense was firing on all cylinders in Week 3 thanks to the chemistry between Ludmila and rookie Jameese Joseph. Ludmila delivered a stunning brace—including a fun backflip celebration—while Joseph became the first player this season to notch multiple assists in one game. The pair is bringing energy, unpredictability, and serious upside to the Stars’ front line. If their connection can click this weekend, Gotham’s back line will have their hands full.

5\. Rookie Watch: New Faces Already Making a Big Impact

This rookie class is not messing around. Gotham’s Sarah Schupansky opened her NWSL account with a goal and an assist last week against the Spirit, continuing her run as one of just four rookies in the league to start every game this season. Fellow Gotham first-year Lilly Reale—who scored her first pro goal in Week 4—was also named to the NWSL’s Best XI of the Month for April, leading all rookies in interceptions and open play crosses. With so many rising talents all across the league, and with each match week seeing a stunning rookie moment, don’t be surprised if Sunday’s match produces another breakout performance.

Kickoff is set for Sunday, May 4 on CBS at 1:00 PM ET. Don’t miss it!
