NEW YORK (May 7, 2026) \- Following a review by the NWSL Disciplinary Committee, the National Women’s Soccer League has issued an additional one-match suspension to North Carolina Courage defender Feli Rauch for an incident that occurred in second half stoppage time of the club’s match against the Kansas City Current on May 2.

Following an in-game review of the incident, Rauch received a red card resulting in an automatic one-match suspension. Upon further review, the Disciplinary Committee determined that the incident rose to the level of a violation of Section 12.4.11, “Major Game Misconduct,” of the League Operations Manual.

As a result, Rauch has been issued an additional one-match suspension, increasing her total suspension to two matches.

Rauch will serve her suspension during the club’s May 8 match against Orlando Pride and May 16 match against Chicago Stars FC.
