Dates, times and host venues have been set for the quarterfinal round of the 2024 NWSL Playoffs, presented by Google Pixel, kicking off the weekend of November 8-10.

The expanded eight-team quarterfinal round begins Friday, November 8, on Prime Video at 8 p.m. ET as the No. 1 seed Orlando Pride, who clinched the NWSL Shield, presented by CarMax, at the beginning of the month, hosts the to-be-determined No. 8 seed at Inter&Co Stadium.

The Kansas City Current hosts the lone quarterfinal match on Saturday, November 9, at CPKC Stadium, kicking off at 12 p.m. ET. The match will air live on the CBS Television Network and stream on Paramount+.

The Washington Spirit opens ABC’s doubleheader coverage on Sunday, November 10, with a 12:30 p.m. ET match at Audi Field in Washington, D.C.

ABC’s Sunday doubleheader coverage continues at 3 p.m. ET as NJ/NY Gotham FC, the defending NWSL Champion, rounds out quarterfinal round action at Red Bull Arena in Harrison, New Jersey.

The 2024 NWSL Playoffs, presented by Google Pixel, will air on a variety of platforms for viewers tuning in outside the United States, including NWSL+. Further information regarding international distribution will be available in the coming weeks.

The 2024 NWSL Championship will air on Saturday, November 23 at 8 p.m. ET live on CBS and streaming on Paramount+. Tickets for the match remain available by visiting the link below.
