April 19: Angel City FC (A) – Challenge Cup Opener <br><u>What to Know</u> : <br>It’s a cast of familiar faces for OL Reign heading into the 2023 season, and after finishing first in the league last year it would be hard to find many who find that to be a problem. As of Monday, March 20, Laura Harvey and her staff have retained 24 of the 27 players rostered from the 2022 season. <br>The club’s acquisitions will all surely contribute, with the likes of Elyse Bennett and Emily Sonnett both regular features for Kansas City and Washington, respectively. Bennett scored three goals in the regular season last year while Sonnett, regarded as one of the best defenders in the league, will help shore up a back line that conceded the fewest goals in league play last season (19). <br>Without the additions, though, the Reign would still be making their case as one of the most complete squads around with U.S. Women’s National Team talent sprinkled throughout via Sofia Huerta, Rose Lavelle, and Megan Rapinoe. It’s expected that standout Phallon Tullis-Joyce will be in goal again with regular starters Huerta, Alana Cook, Sam Hiatt, and Lu Barnes in front of her. The midfield is once again full with Jess Fishlock, Quinn, Nikki Stanton, and others competing with Lavelle as new signing Luany helps provide even further depth. <br>Up top, the side is bolstered by the return of all of forwards Bethany Balcer, Jordyn Huitema, Tziarra King and Veronica Latsko, all of whom will run alongside Rapinoe and Bennett as the Reign look to establish their place at the top again. <br>World Cup year or not, OL Reign appear to have all the ingredients necessary for another run… the question comes down to whether or not they are able to replicate their successes of last year or if familiarity will breed complacency, something Harvey and her staff can only plan against. <br>
