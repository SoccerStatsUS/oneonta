It was a long 540 days between Sophia WIlson's last two NWSL goals, but it was worth the wait. Wilson scored the game-winning goal in Portland's match 2-1 victory over Angel City FC for her first since returning from maternity leave this season.

With the Thorns up 1-0 in stoppage time, Wilson bagged an insurance goal that ultimately made the difference in the game. Wilson made a run down the left hand side of the field and teed herself up for a beautiful left-footed banger that even a diving Claudia Dickey couldn't defend.

Wilson was swarmed by her teammates and was seen getting visibly emotional as she blew kisses to the crowd and held up a heart. She playfully denied getting teary when asked about it during her postgame interview, but with her husband, Michael Wilson, and daughter, Gigi, in the stands, it was a moment she said that this couldn't have come at a better time.

"It was definitely a long time coming, I think. I tried not to put too much pressure on myself, just coming back and playing at this level, but I really needed a goal with them being completely honest," Wilson said. "I think once I get one, hopefully more will come, but I'm really proud of our team."

Angel City FC is a favorite for Wilson, who last scored in the NWSL on Nov. 2, 2024 against L.A. Wilson's U.S. Women's National Team teammate Trinity Rodman also scored her first goal of the season in the Washington Spirit's win over the 2025 NWSL Shield winning Kansas City Current.
