NEW YORK (March 8, 2023) –

The National Women’s Soccer League today announced a partnership with Canadian sports broadcaster TSN to distribute NWSL matches, including the 2023 NWSL Championship, across its network in Canada this season. Live match coverage will air [in English] across TSN and TSN+. <br>“The NWSL is the proud home to a number of Canadian players and providing fans in Canada with as many opportunities as possible to watch those players compete was a priority for us this offseason,” said NWSL Commissioner Jessica Berman. “We’re proud to partner with TSN to make that goal a reality as we continue expanding coverage opportunities for our growing audiences around the world.” <br>The NWSL features Canadian players on 10 of its 12 teams, highlighted by reigning NWSL champion and Portland Thorns FC mainstays Christine Sinclair and Janine Beckie. Sinclair, who captured her third championship with Portland in 2022, has 322 international appearances and is the most-capped active international player among men and women, and leads all men’s and women’s players with 190 international goals. <br>The 2023 NWSL season begins Saturday, March 25. A full broadcast schedule including platform-specific details will be released in the coming days. All matches will be available internationally on NWSLSoccer.com. <br>Highlights and news updates throughout the NWSL season are available across SPORTSCENTRE, TSN.ca , TSN’s official social media accounts, and DIGITAL SPORTSCENTRE.
