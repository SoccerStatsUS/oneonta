Spanish forward Esther delivered a game-changing performance off the bench, setting an NWSL record with the fastest three-goal contributions in league history during Gotham FC’s 5-1 victory against Bay FC at home.

In the 70th minute, Esther assisted Rose Lavelle’s go-ahead goal to give Gotham FC the lead.

She then scored two quick goals of her own in quick succession to put the match out of reach.

Her two goals and one assist all came within a span of four minutes and 20 seconds, sealing a dominant win for Gotham.
