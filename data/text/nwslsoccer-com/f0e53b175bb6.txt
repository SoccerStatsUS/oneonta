NEW YORK (September 3, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League (NWSL) announced today that North Carolina Courage Head Coach Mak Lind has been named Coach of the Month for the month of August by the NWSL Media Association and the league’s on-air broadcast talent.

The first-year head coach in North Carolina led his team to four wins in six matches behind a high-powered offense that scored two or more goals in each match in August, including putting four past the Washington Spirit at Audi Field in statement victory. The Courage have now scored 40 goals so far this season, the most in the league.

The NWSL Coach of the Month is selected by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league consistently, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. Awards for the month of August are based on performances during regular season matches.
