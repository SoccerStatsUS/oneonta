ORLANDO, Fla.

– NJ/NY Gotham FC stunned the Orlando Pride as a late Midge Purce penalty kick and late Lynn Williams finish lifted the visitors over the Pride in over 107 minutes of action with stoppage time included. The 2-0 result means that Gotham FC is now unbeaten in its last six visits to Orlando in all competitions (W5 D1), including winning each of its last four matches at Exploria Stadium. Those four wins mark the first time Gotham has won three or more consecutive road matches against a single opponent in the club’s NWSL history.

ORLANDO, Fla.

– NJ/NY Gotham FC stunned the Orlando Pride as a late Midge Purce penalty kick and late Lynn Williams finish lifted the visitors over the Pride in over 107 minutes of action with stoppage time included. The 2-0 result means that Gotham FC is now unbeaten in its last six visits to Orlando in all competitions (W5 D1), including winning each of its last four matches at Exploria Stadium. Those four wins mark the first time Gotham has won three or more consecutive road matches against a single opponent in the club’s NWSL history. <br>The first half, though goalless, was marked by end-to-end energy that saw chances created for both sides throughout. For Gotham, forward Lynn Williams was ever active and tested Pride goalkeeper Kaylie Collins from close range though Collins was ultimately able to keep the shot out. On the other end, Orlando midfielder Kerry Abello had the pair of best looks for the hosts in the first half. Abello had chances from both close in and a long range effort denied by Gotham goalkeeper Abby Smith as the hosts pressed for the game’s first goal. <br>Despite all of that, however, neither team would find the back of the net and the pair went scoreless into the halftime break. <br>Immediately into the second half though, Orlando forced Gotham FC to put their guards back up with a cleverly timed effort in the 47 <sup>th</sup> minute. Taking the ball into the box on the left side, the Brazilian pushed a low shot toward goal that Smith was able to push back away and out for a corner kick. Though Orlando did not create anything from that set piece, it was enough to kick start a back-and-forth second half. <br>In the 50 <sup>th</sup> minute, Gotham FC had won a corner as Kristie Mewis’s shot was parried out of play by the goalkeeper Collins. As defender Kelley O’Hara swung the ball in, teammate Jenna Nighswonger had a close range effort blocked and multiple Gotham teammates suffered the same fate in the immediate after match as the ball pinballed around the penalty area before eventually being cleared. <br>Back the same way in the 65 <sup>th</sup> minute, Gotham recovered possession right off the feet of Collins via a Lynn Williams challenge and as Williams barreled toward the endline, her teammates got in position to receive a cutback pass. First from Williams was to Sinead Farrelly, who had her shot blocked away from near the penalty spot. Then, a follow up chance from the right foot of Yazmeen Ryan fell just wide of the far post as Orlando escaped being punished. <br>Twelve minutes later, Orlando was back on the ball and looking to threaten the Gotham goal through Abello once more. As the Pride moved forward, a ball into the penalty area was partially cleared by the Gotham defense only to fall at Abello’s feet about 20 yards from goal. Swinging a strong left foot at the ball, her shot landed just over the crossbar and out of play as the search for an opening goal continued. <br>Then, right as the match approached the third of nine minutes of added time, Gotham was awarded a penalty kick as Allie Long’s header from close in was judged to have been handled by Pride defender Caitlin Cosme resulting in the decision. After VAR review, the decision was upheld and Gotham forward Midge Purce was given the opportunity to step up and convert in the 100 <sup>th</sup> minute of the match. Sending her right-footed effort toward the top right side of goal, Purce converted and broke the hosts’ hearts late on with little time to recover. <br>Finally, approaching the 107 <sup>th</sup> minute through the 17 <sup>th</sup> minute of added time, Gotham’s long ball technique of relieving pressure would pay off as a lumped pass allowed Williams space on the left side. With the Orlando defense tired, Williams took the ball all the way down toward goal where she curled a low effort into the far side netting and in to double her side’s lead and end the match. <br>The loss is Orlando’s third straight to start their 2023 campaign, while Gotham FC bounces back from its first defeat of the year to earn its second win after defeating Angel City in the opening weekend. Though Orlando’s last win came against Gotham, a 2-1 away victory last August, the Pride have now collected just one point from their last nine matches (D1 L8). <br>
