Athletes are known to be superstitious. The Washington Spirit aren't immune to the whims of superstitions, but they also aren't taking it too seriously either.

There's a new good luck charm in town, and after the Spirit's dominant 4-0 win over the Kansas City Current, he's going to be here to stay.

Meet José, the Washington Spirit's lucky fake coyote.

The plastic four legged friend is designed to deter geese and other animals from messing with the pitch. While training in San Jose, Calif., the Spirit became enamored with one such coyote on the practice fields. It was an uncharacteristically slow start for Washington, but they left Northern California with their first three points of the season with a win over Bay FC.

To recreate the successful environment that led to the win, the Washington Spirit President of Soccer Operations Haley Carter spared no expense. She shipped a replica of the fake coyotes to the club's facilities, Hal Hershfelt explained in a TikTok video of her assembling the team's new mascot. The team named him José in honor of the Bay Area city.

The Spirit came back after the international break with something to prove. Leicy Santos scored her first career brace. Trinity Rodman earned a goal and assist and showed love to her boyfriend's celebration. Claudia Martinez became the first Paraguayan player to score a goal in the NWSL.

It was an instant Rowdy Audi classic, and Spirit fans might just have a silly plastic coyote to thank.

"We're so excited. Maybe it's because of our good luck charm, but we're pumped," Hershfelt said after the game.

Before the night was done, the team led the crowd in a chant of Jose to the tune of "Olé, Olé, Olé." It's safe to say the coyote is here to stay.
