The Washington Spirit has announced the club’s featured highlights for Saturday night’s match against northeast rival NJ/NY Gotham FC. Hispanic Heritage tributes include honorary captains Daniela R. Fernandez from the Congressional Hispanic Caucus Institute, David Diaz from the Greater Washington Hispanic Chamber of Commerce and Under Secretary of the Air Force Gina Ortiz Jones.
