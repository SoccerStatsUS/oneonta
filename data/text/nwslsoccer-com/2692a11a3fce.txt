The NWSL continues to solidify its place as a powerhouse in women's football, with several players and a coach earning nominations for the prestigious 2024 FIFA Best Awards. From standout performances on the pitch to leadership off it, these nominations highlight the league’s growing influence in shaping the global game. Additionally, this will be the first year the FIFA Marta Award is given to the best goal scorer of the year.

TO CAST YOUR FAN VOTE: https://www.fifa.com/en/the-best-fifa-football-awards/2024

Four NWSL stars are among the nominees for the Best FIFA Women’s Player, celebrating their outstanding contributions to club and country over the past year:

Barbra Banda (Orlando Pride/Zambia)

Mallory Swanson (Chicago Red Stars/USA)

Naomi Girma (San Diego Wave/USA)

Sophia Smith (Portland Thorns/USA)

Trinity Rodman (Washington Spirit/USA)

Joining the ranks of elite tacticians, Jonatan Giráldez earned a nomination for Best FIFA Women’s Coach. After guiding Barcelona to another treble-winning season, Giráldez took the reins at the Washington Spirit, where his innovative strategies and experienced staff helped drive the team to their third NWSL Championship appearance in club history.

The NWSL boasts two nominees in the Best FIFA Women’s Goalkeeper category:

Alyssa Naeher (Chicago Red Stars/USA)

Ann-Katrin Berger (NJ/NY Gotham FC/Germany)

The inaugural FIFA Marta Award, named in honor of the Brazilian legend who capped her illustrious career with an NWSL Championship win alongside Orlando Pride in 2024, celebrates the best goal scored in women’s football worldwide. This year, three NWSL players represent the league among the nominees, with one scoring their nominated goal before joining their NWSL team:

Trinity Rodman (USA/Washington Spirit) USA v Japan | 3 August 2024

Asisat Oshoala (Nigeria/Barcelona/Bay FC) Barcelona v Benfica | 14 November 2023

Marta (Brazil/Orlando Pride) Brazil v Jamaica | 1 June 2024
