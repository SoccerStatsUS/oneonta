The NWSL is back for its 2025 season, kicking off on Friday, March 14th, and promising another electrifying year of unforgettable moments. With fresh faces eager to make their mark, record-breaking feats on the horizon, and rivalries heating up, the 2025 season is set to raise the bar. As teams battle for supremacy and players chase their legacies, every match will be a new chapter in a story of grit, passion, and triumph. Get ready—the road to NWSL glory begins now.

Seattle Reign FC is determined to rebound from a disappointing 2024 campaign, and they’ve made the moves to do it. Lynn Biyendolo (formerly Lynn Williams), the NWSL’s all-time leading scorer, headlines the new-look offense, supported by Jess Fishlock, Lu Barnes, and another key newcomer, standout defender Madi Curry. New talent like Maddie Dahlien, a UCLA graduate, and Emily Mason, a Rutgers standout, adds depth to a team that was just missing a few pieces last season to reach a better ending. With a mix of seasoned veterans and fresh faces, the Reign is ready to silence their doubters—and maybe finally claim their highly coveted title.
