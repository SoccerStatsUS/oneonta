The National Women’s Soccer League kicks off its historic 12th campaign this Friday, March 15 with the 2024 UKG NWSL Challenge Cup. 2023 NWSL Champion NJ/NY Gotham FC host San Diego Wave FC, winner of the 2023 NWSL Shield, presented by CarMax, at Red Bull Arena in Harrison, New Jersey in the newly reformatted single-match contest.
