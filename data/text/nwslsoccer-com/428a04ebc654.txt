A record-breaking 56 players from the NWSL will represent 12 out of 13 countries competing in the 2024 Olympic Games starting July 25th, the highest representation from any league worldwide. This remarkable achievement highlights the exceptional skill and talent showcased in every NWSL match, offering soccer fans who enjoy the Olympics a chance to see their favorite stars in action during the regular season.
