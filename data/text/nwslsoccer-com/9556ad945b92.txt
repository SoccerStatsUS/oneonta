New York became even more of a soccer city Tuesday morning.

Gotham FC, welcome to Queens. The reigning NWSL Champions will call Etihad Park home in 2028, and the move was made official with an announcement at City Hall.

Gotham owner Carolyn Tisch Blodgett, GM Yael Averbuch West, and former player Ali Krieger were onsite for the announcement alongside Mayor Zohran Mamdani, Governor Kathy Hochul and Queens Borough President Donovan Richards Jr. The move will bring the world's game to the world's borough, Mayor Mamdani said, especially for girls playing soccer all over the city.

"It is an investment in our young female athletes, who are too often overlooked," he said. "Instead of begging their parents to have to take them to New Jersey, girls growing up in Jackson Heights, Sunnyside, and Flushing will now be able to get on the 7 train after school and watch some of the greatest soccer players in the world go to work."

Etihad Park is New York's first soccer specific stadium set to open next spring and will host both Gotham FC and New York City FC. The all-electric sports stadium will be the first of its kind in the U.S.

For Gotham, this next chapter will help redefine what excellence can look like.

"We've never mistaken success for the finish line," Tisch Blodgett said. "Every milestone has challenged us to think even bigger, and that's what today represents."

Gotham FC will preview what it will be like to call Queens home on July 15. In a 2025 NWSL Championship rematch, Gotham and the Washington Spirit face off under the lights at Citi Field in a history-making Rivalry Week presented by Ally match.
