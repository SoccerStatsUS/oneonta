Fandom knows no bounds. It doesn’t matter where you live, you love the team that you love, and there’s data to prove it.

Visions Insights data shows that it’s not as clear cut as one might expect. Vermont’s allegiance is to Gotham FC, not switching over to majority for Boston Legacy quite yet. North Dakota, on the other hand, may be inducted as an informal member of New England.

The San Diego Wave dominate the California fanbase, and the Denver Summit have already laid a claim to Colorado, New Mexico, and (checks notes) Delaware.
