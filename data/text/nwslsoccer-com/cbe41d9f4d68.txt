NEW YORK (May 4, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today that North Carolina Courage midfielder Ashley Sanchez has been named the NWSL Player of the Month for the month of April by the NWSL Media Association and the league’s on-air broadcast talent.

Sanchez scored three goals in three matches, and each one contributed in a major way to the Courage going unbeaten in the month. She scored the equalizer matches against Boston and Portland and scored the match winner in North Carolina’s 1-0 win in Houston.

The NWSL Player of the Month i**s selected by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league consistently, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. Awards for the month of April are based on performances during regular season matches.
