While the defenses of the NWSL have a break from the likes of Barbra Banda and Temwa Chawinga wreaking havoc, the teams competing at the Women's African Cup of Nations (WAFCON) are not as fortunate.

The stakes could not be higher, with the best four teams qualifying for the 2027 World Cup, and it should come as no surprise that the NWSL stars stepped up for their countries.

Chawinga, playing alongside her sister, Tabitha Chawinga, led Malawi to a stunning upset of the reigning WAFCON Champions, defeating Nigeria, 3-2.

In their tournament debut, Temwa Chawinga scored Malawi's first WAFCON goal. Just minutes later, Tabitha would score its second. In second half stoppage time, Temwa scored an insurance goal that proved to be the game-winner.

While Malawi's win came down to the final minutes, Zambia cruised to a 6-0 win over Egypt, and Banda did what she does best: score. The NWSL Golden Boot leader scored four goals (yes, four) as she continues to be in impeccable form. ​

As part of Group C, Malawi and Zambia will face off in the final round of group stages on August 5. The last time we saw Chawinga and Banda face off was earlier this summer, when the Orlando Pride defeated the Kansas City Current, 3-0. Banda scored, which could be a sign of things to come or a little extra motivation for Chawinga to return the favor.

Across the tournament, 11 NWSL players represent eight countries, and it’s not just Banda and Chawinga who’ve already made an impact for their teams.

Rosemounde Kouassi earned an assist in Ivory Coast’s 4-1 win over Burkina Faso, and her newly-signed Washington Spirit teammate Mélissa Bethi scored in Algeria’s 2-0 win over Senegal.

Boston Legacy's Aïssata Traoré converted a penalty kick for her native Mali's lone goal against Cameroon on July 29.

The group stage runs until August 6 with quarterfinals starting August 8.
