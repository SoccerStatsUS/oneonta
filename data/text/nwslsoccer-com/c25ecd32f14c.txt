NEW YORK (May 4, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today that Gotham FC forward Jordynn Dudley has been named NWSL Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, for the month of April by the NWSL Media Association and the league’s on-air broadcast talent.

The 21-year old made the most of her second and third career starts at the end of the month, contributing an assist in Gotham’s 3-0 win over Bay FC, and backing it up with her first career goal five days later in Chicago.

Ally, a financial services company with the nation's largest all-digital bank and a leading brand in women's sports, has been a primary sponsor of the NWSL since 2021 and serves as the official retail banking partner of the NWSLPA as well. Through its longstanding partnership, Ally has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to elevating women's soccer and enhancing the experience for athletes and fans alike.

The NWSL Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, is selected by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league consistently, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. Awards for the month of April are based on performances during regular season matches.
