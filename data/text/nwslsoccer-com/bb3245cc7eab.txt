NEW YORK (March 9, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League (NWSL) today announced the promotion of Sarah Gregorius to Vice President of Sporting. In her new role, Gregorius will serve as the league’s senior sporting leader, responsible for shaping and overseeing the NWSL’s long-term sporting strategy in alignment with the league’s competitive priorities and continued growth. She will report to Commissioner Jessica Berman.

“Sarah has already made a meaningful impact on the league in her time here,” said Berman. “Her experience across the global women’s game, combined with her perspective as a former player and respected leader, makes her uniquely suited to guide our sporting strategy. We’re excited for her to lead this next phase of work with our clubs to continue strengthening the competitive foundation of the NWSL.”

Gregorius will lead the league’s sporting function across key areas including competition, player development pathways, international relations, data analytics, medical and high performance, research and innovation, and the continued advancement of the player experience across the NWSL ecosystem. Working closely with clubs, technical leaders and stakeholders across the global women’s football landscape, she will help guide the evolution of the NWSL’s sporting infrastructure to support world-class performance environments and sustain the league’s position as the most competitive women’s league in the world.

“I’m honored to step into this role at such a pivotal moment for the NWSL,” said Gregorius. “We have an incredible opportunity at the league not only to continue setting the global standard for women’s professional soccer, but to lead the way in how the women’s game evolves. I look forward to working alongside our clubs, players and the broader football community to build systems that support elite performance, player wellbeing and long-term growth in the NWSL and across women’s football.”

Gregorius brings years of senior leadership experience across elite sport and the women’s game, with a proven ability to operate at both strategic and execution levels in complex, high-growth environments. Prior to joining the NWSL in the fall of 2024, she served in global strategy and policy leadership roles at FIFPRO, representing players’ interests and contributing to the advancement of women’s football worldwide.

A former professional player and international for the New Zealand Women’s National Team, Gregorius brings a rare combination of firsthand playing experience and executive expertise that will continue to strengthen the league’s sporting leadership as it enters its next phase of growth.
