NEW YORK (May 7, 2026) \- Following a review by the NWSL Disciplinary Committee, the National Women’s Soccer League has issued an additional one-match suspension to Angel City FC midfielder Maiara Niehues for an incident that occurred in first half stoppage time of the club’s match against Utah Royals FC on May 2.

Niehues was issued a red card at the time of the incident, resulting in an automatic one-match suspension. Upon further review, the Disciplinary Committee determined that the incident rose to the level of a violation of Section 12.4.11, “Major Game Misconduct,” of the League Operations Manual.

As a result, Niehues has been issued an additional one-match suspension, increasing her total suspension to two matches.

Niehues will serve her suspension during the club’s May 9 match against San Diego Wave FC and May 17 match against Portland Thorns FC.
