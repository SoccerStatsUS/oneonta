When your team is in the championship, you can't take any chances. One Gotham fan decided she wasn't leaving it up to fate and brought out the big guns: an Etsy witch.

Laura Brown posted on X (formerly Twitter) that she paid an Etsy witch for Gotham to win the Championship. She got her money's worth.

In a detailed post, Brown shared the details of the "Victory Spell" the witch, who goes by Desert Rose Psychic. Highlights include: energizing the team, calling in competitive energy, and affirmations for fluid and sharp teamwork.

Desert Rose Psychic signed the note, "Wishing you a thrilling, powerful, and victorious championship night."

Whether it was thanks to divine help or not, Gotham beat the Spirit, 1-0. A five star review is in order.
