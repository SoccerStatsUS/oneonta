The Bay FC midfielder started every game this season

NEW YORK (November 6, 2025) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today that Bay FC midfielder Taylor Huff has been named Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, for the month of October/November by the NWSL Media Association and the league’s on-air broadcast talent.

Huff not only started every game in October for Bay, but she was one of two rookies to start every game in 2025. She was the lone Bay rookie to appear in every game.

With four goals on the year, Huff is tied for second most on the team. She also proved to be a crucial part of Bay’s set pieces, taking 48 corner kicks, the most of any rookie this season.

Ally, a financial services company with the nation’s largest all-digital bank and a leading brand in women’s sports, is also a primary sponsor of the NWSL and the official retail banking partner of the NWSLPA.

The NWSL Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, is selected by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league consistently, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. Awards for the month of October/November are based off performances during the regular season matches.
