NEW YORK (May 4, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League (NWSL) announced today that Washington Spirit head coach Adrián González has been named NWSL Coach of the Month for the month of April by the NWSL Media Association and the league’s on-air broadcast talent.

González had his team firing on all cylinders during the second month of the season. The Spirit won all three matches in April, scoring seven goals across the three games and conceding none, leading to the team skyrocketing up the table, landing in third place before the end of the month.

The NWSL Coach of the Month is selected by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league consistently, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. Awards for the month of April are based on performances during regular season matches.
