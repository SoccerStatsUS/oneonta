Due to poor air quality conditions in the New York metropolitan area, tonight’s match between NJ/NY Gotham FC and the Orlando Pride at Red Bull Arena has been postponed. The rescheduled match is set for August 9 at 7:30 p.m. ET and will be available to stream on Paramount+.

Due to poor air quality conditions in the New York metropolitan area, tonight’s match between NJ/NY Gotham FC and the Orlando Pride at Red Bull Arena has been postponed. The rescheduled match is set for August 9 at 7:30 p.m. ET and will be available to stream on Paramount+.<br>The safety of our players, officials and fans is our top priority. Following consultation with the NWSL Medical and Operations staff, it was determined that the match could not be safely conducted based on the projected air quality index.
