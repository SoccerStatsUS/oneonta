It’s never too early to start the MVP debate, right?

Just a third of a way through the NWSL season, and there are already players who are pulling ahead of the pack. NWSL experts and analysts weigh in with their way-too-early MVP predictions.

In just 11 matches, Banda leads the Golden Boot race right now with nine goals. To be in the Golden Boot race every year (or win it) is incredible. Every single game she is a goal threat – regardless of the opponent or her team’s form. This year I feel her game has evolved even more. She will drift into wide areas more often to give herself different angles, to either run at opponents or get shots off. She constantly gives her team confidence. Even if Banda is not scoring, she's getting shots off with two or three defenders around her. She’s one of the best in the world.

An honorable mention goes to Denver Summit’s Janine Sonis. The Colorado native scored her first goals for the expansion side in epic fashion – braces in back-to-back matches.

Banda’s attacking output as a striker has been second to none this year in the league – potentially even globally. Game after game this season it has appeared that there is no structure, gameplan, or individual that can stop her from continuing her scoring barrage. Banda’s well rounded skillset provides the Pride multiple ways to get to goal. She can be destructive in behind backlines, use her strength to hold off defenders and create a more comfortable finish, and scores from nearly any height or angle. I am not sure what the Orlando Pride would be without her impact. Banda has been the driving force to keep the Pride in play contention this season.

As much as attackers shine in this league, flowers need to be given to a defensive player that has stood out, as well. Historically speaking, the best defensive teams have always made a run at the Shield or championship, and the Utah Royals are one of the best defenses in the league. Kate Del Fava played an integral role within the surprise success of the Utah Royals this season. The Royals have relied on their press to help create chances and disrupt opposition’s rhythm. A huge part of that falls on the shoulders of Del Fava. She reinforces the press from behind, wins aerial duels from opponents trying to play over the press, and also has put out countless emergency situations in one-vs-one defensive moments. Her leadership, defensive reliability, and constant availability for selection makes her an extremely valuable asset to the Royals’ 2026 campaign.
