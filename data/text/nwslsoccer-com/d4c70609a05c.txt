Rebeca Bernal wasted no time making an impact in her NWSL regular-season debut on Friday night. The Mexico international, who signed a three-year deal with Washington Spirit after a stellar career in Liga MX Femenil, started in the club’s season opener, adding another milestone to her journey. Fresh off helping the Spirit lift the 2025 NWSL Challenge Cup, Bernal anchored the backline as they kicked off their 2025 campaign with a win.

Bernal’s presence was felt in the second half as the Houston Dash pushed to make a comeback The 26-year-old showcased the defensive leadership that made her a multi-time champion with Monterrey, helping Washington maintain its shape and composure under pressure and return home with all three points.

Her debut came just days after lifting the NWSL Challenge Cup, where she played a key role in the Spirit’s penalty shootout victory. Now, with league play underway, Bernal looks set to be a crucial piece of Washington’s defensive core this season.

The Spirit will continue their campaign next Friday night, facing the Houston Dash on the road at 8 p.m. EDT.
