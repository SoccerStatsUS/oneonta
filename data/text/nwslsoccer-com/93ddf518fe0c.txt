Third time's a charm. After falling in the deciding match in 2021 and 2024, Gotham FC are bringing the Challenge Cup, presented by e.l.f., home.

The reigning NWSL Champions defeated the Kansas City Current, 2-0, on a rainy Friday night in Columbus, Ohio.

It was a scoreless effort through much of the first half until a Gotham corner kick got chippy in the box. Jaelin Howell drew a foul, earning a penalty kick, and Esther González drove it home from the spot in the 37th minute.

Breakout rookie Jordynn Dudley doubled the score in the second half, forcing a turnover near midfield and taking care of business herself. Dudley wove through the Current defense and ripped a shot to the lower left corner of the net past Kansas City keeper Marisa Jordan.

As has been the story for the first part of the season, Gotham's stalwart defense, led by Tierna Davidson, Jess Carter, and Emily Sonnett, kept the Current's attacking threats like Temwa Chawinga and Croix Bethune contained and unable to get on the board.

And we can't forget keeper Ann-Katrin Berger's filthy footwork.

When the final whistle blew, confetti joined the mix falling from the sky as Gotham lifted their first Challenge Cup. With the NWSL returning to action this weekend, some new hardware in the trophy case goes a long way.
