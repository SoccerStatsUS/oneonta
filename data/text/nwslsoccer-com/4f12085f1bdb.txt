NEW YORK (September 3, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today that North Carolina Courage forward Ashley Sanchez has been named the Player of the Month, for the month of August by the NWSL Media Association.

Sanchez continued her best season to date in August, including back-to-back braces against Boston and Angel City, bringing her total goals scored for the month to five, and added two assists. Her 15 goals this season is a new Courage single-season record, and ties Alex Morgan and Crystal Dunn for the most goals scored in a season by an American with eight matches still to play.

The NWSL Player of the Month is selected each month of the regular season by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league on a consistent basis, and broadcast talent. Awards for the month of August are based on performances during the regular season matches.
