SAN DIEGO, Calif.

– San Diego Wave FC secured a first-ever NWSL Shield on Sunday night after a regular season finale victory over Racing Louisville FC on the back of goals from forwards Jaedyn Shaw and Alex Morgan helped the home side to a huge win at Snapdragon Stadium. It had already been guaranteed that the Wave would have a first round playoffs bye, but the 1 <sup>st</sup> place finish is all the more sweet as the club earns its first-ever piece of silverware in dramatic fashion. <br>The Wave would open the scoring in 26 <sup>th</sup> minute as Shaw produced an incredible solo run and snuck a bouncing effort past Louisville goalkeeper Katie Lund and in as the home crowd erupted. Shaw, who with the goal became the highest-scoring teenager in NWSL history, had been played in by Morgan before a clever touch released her in behind where she fired past Lund and in. Though Lund had gotten a piece of the effort, it was still powerful enough to roll across the line and in. <br>The hosts would hold onto that one-goal lead all the way through the first half with Louisville struggling to produce a meaningful effort and test the Wave defense early on. <br>Unfortunately for the visitors, though, the night went from bad to worse as San Diego’s Alex Morgan scored her goal just 80 seconds after the restart in the second half to all but put the match to bed. Morgan had received a pass just inside the penalty area, and pulling the ball back in close to her, she then unleashed a curling left foot effort that slammed into the far side netting and in for her team’s second on the night. <br>That effort would be the only meaningful one in the second half despite good efforts from both sides, but as the fulltime whistle sounded, San Diego’s quest for silverware was complete and the home crowd gave them plenty of support to culminate a fantastic regular season. <br>
