What Summer Cup is:

The NWSL x LIGA MX Femenil Summer Cup, a first-of-its-kind collaboration between the two leagues, started over the weekend. This groundbreaking tournament features all 14 NWSL clubs and the top six teams from the LIGA MX Femenil: Tigres UANL, Club América, Chivas de Guadalajara, Rayadas de Monterrey, Pachuca, and Tijuana.

The 33-match tournament begins with 30 group stage matches, followed by a four-team semifinal round on August 6. The semifinal doubleheader will take place at CPKC Stadium, home of the Kansas City Current, in Kansas City, Missouri. The championship match is scheduled for Friday, October 25, at Toyota Field in San Antonio, Texas.

Group stage games take place through Friday, August 2nd.

The inaugural Summer Cup’s semi final will take place at CPKC Stadium in Kansas City, Missouri. Matches will take place on Tuesday, August 6th back-to-back. Following 30 matches of group play, the 4 clubs out of the 5 group winners will face off at the home of the Kansas City Current. Both semi-final matches will be on CBS Sports Network and Paramount+
