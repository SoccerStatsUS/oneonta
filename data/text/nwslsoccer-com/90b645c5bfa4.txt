LEICY SANTOS STUNNER

The Washington Spirit earned a clean sheet against the Courage, but it was Leicy Santos who stole the show. Kate Wiesner sent a beautiful ball upfield to the top of the box, where Santos trapped the ball midair with her right foot before driving it home with her left. The ball never touched the ground, and it was enough to get her the No. 1 play on Sportscenter's Top 10.
