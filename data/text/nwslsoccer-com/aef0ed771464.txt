The push for the NWSL playoffs is underway as we head into Week 19. The Orlando Pride are the only team that have clinched a spot in the playoffs with a Week 18 victory over the Chicago Red Stars.

Three teams can lock in a postseason spot this weekend. Check out the following scenarios below.

Washington Spirit – 2 scenarios to clinch playoffs in Week 19

The Washington Spirit need a win/draw against the Houston Dash OR

North Carolina or Gotham to win/draw AND Chicago or Louisville to win/draw

Kansas City Current - 1 scenario to clinch playoffs in Week 19

The Kansas City Current need to win against the Orlando Pride AND North Carolina Courage OR Gotham FC win/draw AND Chicago or Louisville win/draw

Gotham FC - 1 scenario to clinch playoffs in Week 19

Gotham FC needs to win against the Seattle Reign AND Chicago Red Stars win AND a Louisville draw

The Orlando Pride can clinch a Top 4 seed/spot with a win over the Kansas City Current and a North Carolina Courage loss.
