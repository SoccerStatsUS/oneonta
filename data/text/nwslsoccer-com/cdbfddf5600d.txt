NEW YORK (January 9, 2024) – The National Women’s Soccer League has shared the final list of over 200 players who have registered for the 2024 NWSL Draft, presented by Ally. The draft will be hosted at the Anaheim Convention Center on Friday, January 12 at 5 p.m. PT/8 p.m. ET in Anaheim, California.

Live coverage of the 2024 NWSL Draft, presented by Ally, will be televised on ION from 8 to 10 p.m. ET. All rounds of the draft will be streamed on ION Plus. Viewers can find access to ION in their area by visiting https://iontelevision.com/find-us.

Fans tuning in to the NWSL Draft outside the U.S. can watch the entirety of the event on Watch.NWSLSoccer.com.

The final list of players registered for the 2024 NWSL Draft, presented by Ally, can be seen below or here. More information about this year’s draft, including draft rules and the latest draft order, can be viewed here.

The 2024 NWSL Draft, presented by Ally, is free and open to the public.
