On Monday, the NWSL was highlighted globally when the 2024 Ballon d’Or rankings revealed three of its standout stars – Sophia Smith, Mallory Swanson, and Trinity Rodman – in the top 10. Famously known as "Triple Espresso," the attacking trio had strong seasons with their NWSL clubs before departing for the Olympics during the summer to power the USWNT to Olympic glory.

Leading the NWSL contingent, Sophia Smith of the Portland Thorns ranked 4th, just behind three FC Barcelona Femení players–Aitana Bonmatí, Caroline Graham Hansen, and Selma Paralluelo–who led their club to win the Champions League. Smith is one of soccer’s rising superstars and most talented goal scorers, and the ranking further cements that growing reputation. Chicago Red Stars’ Mallory Swanson followed closely in 6th, showcasing remarkable resilience and skill after returning from a devastating injury in 2023. Washington Spirit’s Trinity Rodman clinched 9th, her quick footwork and creativity with a ball quickly establishing her as a fan favorite for soccer fans across the world.

Orlando Pride’s Barbra Banda, currently second in the NWSL Golden Boot race and a clinical goal scorer for Zambia as well, was ranked 12th. Chicago Red Stars’ Alyssa Naeher and Houston Dash’s Tarciane rounded out the NWSL’s presence, securing 17th and 23rd respectively, with exceptional performances in goal and defense for their national teams and club teams.
