NEW YORK (April 3, 2026) - The National Women’s Soccer League announced today the 11 players named to the NWSL Best XI of the Month, presented by Amazon Prime, for the month of March. Headlined by NWSL Player of the Month Sveindís Jónsdóttir, seven teams are represented in the league’s Top 11, including four from San Diego Wave FC.

GK – Abby Smith (DEN): Smith made 20 saves across four games and kept two clean sheets, including against 2025 champions Gotham FC to secure Denver their first ever win

D – Gisele Thomspon (LA): Thompson scored her first goal since May of last year and was a crucial part of an Angel City defense that allowed just two goals in the month, making seven tackles in three games

D – Kennedy Wesley (SD): Wesley anchored a SD defense that allowed only three goals over four games

D – Jess Carter (GFC): Carter made 10 tackles and had six interceptions for a Gotham defense that kept three clean sheets in four games in March

D – Sofia Huerta (SEA): Huerta was a huge influence on both ends of the pitch in March, tallying two assists and 15 tackles

M – Kenza Dali (SD): Dali was at the center of a San Diego midfield doing a little bit of everything and leads the league in chances created

M – Olivia Moultrie (POR): Moultrie tallied two goals and one assist, with both of her goals being game-winners.

M – Lia Godfrey (SD): Godfrey started her rookie campaign scoring three goals in the month, all of which were game-winners

F – Dudinha (SD): Dudinha had a say in five of the seven goals the Wave scored in March, assisting three and scoring two

F – Barbra Banda (ORL): Banda returned to action in fine form in the opening month, scoring three goals in her first three games back from injury

F – Sveindís Jónsdóttir (LA): Jónsdóttir had a goal contribution in each match of the month, totalling three goals and two assists and leads the golden boot race

As the presenting sponsor of the NWSL Best XI, Amazon Prime is bringing fans closer to the players they love by placing the spotlight on the excellence of the athletes selected for this honor. Prime encourages members to explore their passions and interests through the entertainment they watch, the music they listen to, and the things they like to buy. You could say: Whatever you’re into, It’s on Prime. Prime Video’s NWSL coverage continues every Friday night throughout the regular season.

The NWSL Best XI of the Month is selected each month of the regular season by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league on a consistent basis, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent.
