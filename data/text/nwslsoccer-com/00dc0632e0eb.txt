What a homecoming for the Denver Summit. With a record-breaking attendance of 63,004 people, the Summit were welcomed back home to Denver after three games on the road.

The Denver Summit and Washington Spirit settled for a scoreless draw in front of the largest crowd in NWSL history. The previous record was set in 2025 when Bay FC hosted the Washington Spirit at Oracle Park.

Boston Legacy set a new record for inaugural club home opener with a crowd of 30,207 at Gillette Stadium on March 14. The Summit nearly doubled that number for an inaugural home opener.

Jordan Angeli, Summit part owner and an NWSL original, announced the crowd size and record just before the second half kicked off.

In addition to Angeli, Brandi Chastain, Julie Foudy, Olympic gymnast Aly Raisman, Bryce Young, Mellody Hobson, and Nobel Peace Prize laureate and author Malala Yousafzai all attended the game, making it a real moment for the NWSL.

Leading into the game at Mile High, the Summit saw the city rise up to the moment to welcome them. They collaborated with local coffee shops for Summit specialty brews, hosted a pop up street soccer event, and even Bruce Brown rocked a Summit kit arriving to Friday night's Denver Nuggets game.

It was already a big week for the Summit, who earned their first win in club history with a 2-0 win over the reigning NWSL Champions, Gotham FC. With the team’s against the Orlando Pride last weekend, that’s five points for the Summit against the last two reigning champions and the 2024 and 2025 runners up.
