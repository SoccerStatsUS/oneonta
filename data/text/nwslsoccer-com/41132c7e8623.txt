NJ/NY Gotham FC and Red Bull Arena have reached an agreement for Gotham FC to play home matches at the arena with this multiyear deal.“Gotham FC has been a tremendous partner and we are thrilled that they will continue to call Red Bull Arena their home,” said Marc de Grandpré, General Manager, New York Red Bulls. “They continue to make an impact on the growth of soccer in the Tri-State area and we are proud to support them.”
