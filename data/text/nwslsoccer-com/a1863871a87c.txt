The highly anticipated docuseries The Offseason premiered on Friday, October 18th, exclusively on X (formerly Twitter), drawing over 2.5 million views within its first 24 hours.

The reality-style series follows 11 NWSL players who spent the 2024 offseason training together in Miami. Viewers are treated to an intimate look at NWSL players' lives, both on and off the field. The Offseason is co-created by NJ/NY Gotham FC forward Midge Purce and Angel City FC co-owner Alexis Ohanian. It offers fans a glimpse into the personal and professional journeys of some of the NWSL's top talent. The show features an all-star cast of NWSL players:

Midge Purce – NJ/NY Gotham FC forward

Michelle Alozie – Houston Dash forward

Kelli Hubly – Portland Thorns FC defender

Lo’eau LaBonta – Kansas City Current midfielder

Paige Nielsen – Houston Dash defender

Ify Onumonu – Former NJ/NY Gotham FC and Utah Royals FC forward

María Sánchez – San Diego Wave forward

Taylor Smith – Former NJ/NY Gotham FC forward

Nikki Stanton – Seattle Reign FC midfielder

Taryn Torres – NJ/NY Gotham FC defender

Ally Watt – Orlando Pride forward
