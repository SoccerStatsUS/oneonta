Angel City may have had their first loss of the year, but there was one big W for Kennedy Fuller At just 19-year-old Kennedy Fuller is in her third NWSL season, but before she ever played professionally, Fuller was a fan. And based on a viral moment from this weekend, she’s still just a fan at heart.

Fuller went viral for her awestruck reaction to the GOAT – Marta.

As fans waited around to get autographs and photos from the Orlando Pride players, they saw Fuller and teammate Taylor Suarez anxiously waiting for one of the very players they just competed against.

“Me again,” Fuller said, then screamed when Marta pulled her and then Suarez in for a hug.

As Marta takes photos with fans, Fuller and Suarez jump up and down and share “Can you believe that just happened?” looks.

They say, “Never meet your heroes,” but that doesn’t apply if your hero is Marta.
