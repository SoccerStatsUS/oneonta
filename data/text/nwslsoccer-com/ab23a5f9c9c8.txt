Is there anything better than a good rivalry? No, there isn't.

NWSL Rivarly Week presented by Ally is coming up, and it's a three match slate that will set the tone for the rest of the season.

The first place San Diego Wave host Angel City FC for the next SoCal Derby It's always fun when San Diego and Angel City face off, but with both coming off shutout wins, neither one will want to give up an inch of momentum.

The Cascadia Rivalry returns to Seattle. The Thorns are coming off a stunning 4-0 win that got everyone involved while the Reign are had a tough go of it in North Carolina. It would be quite the statement to win at home against a bitter foe.

Finally, Gotham FC and the Washington Spirit are set to make history at Citi Field on July 15 in a 2025 NWSL Championship rematch. With Gotham set to make the move to Queens in 2028, it's a great way to kick off a new era.
