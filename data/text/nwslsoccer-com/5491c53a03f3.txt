It was the substitution soccer fans longed to see. Sophia Wilson made her first appearance in an NWSL game since November 2024.

In the very first game of the 2026 NWSL season, the Portland Thorns beat the Washington Spirit at Audi Field, 1-0. If that was a statement from the visiting team, Wilson seeing her first regular season game minutes since coming back from maternity leave is an exclamation point.

Wilson came into the game 76 minutes in, and even the Spirit faithful at Rowdy Audi gave her a warm welcome back to the pitch.

“Even a road crowd can appreciate Sophia Wilson’s return,” Amazon Prime play-by-play announcer Mike Watts said.

The 2024 NWSL MVP made her 2026 debut against her fellow USWNT teammate, Trinity Rodman, making it a “Double Espresso” reunion.

Yeah, we are so back.
