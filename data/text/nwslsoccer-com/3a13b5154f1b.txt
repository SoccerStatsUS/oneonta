NEW YORK (November 19, 2024) - The National Women’s Soccer League today announced Washington Spirit midfielder Croix Bethune as the 2024 NWSL Rookie of the Year, presented by Ally.

Bethune led the league in assists during the regular season, notching her 10th assist during Week 17, and tying the single-season record for assists set by Tobin Heath with the Portland Thorns in 2016. Bethune’s performance on May 1 in a 4-2 road victory over the Chicago Red Stars marked the first time in league history for a rookie to record three assists in one game.

The former Georgia Bulldog led all rookies this regular season with five goals and 10 assists through 17 matches played. The 2024 No. 3 overall draft pick was named the 2024 NWSL Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, a record-breaking four times, notably registering one goal and seven assists throughout May.

In addition to the award, Croix Bethune will also receive a cash bonus from the NWSL and Ally to celebrate her accomplishments from her first season in the NWSL.

Ally, a financial services company with the nation’s largest all-digital bank and a leading brand in women’s sports, is also a primary sponsor of the NWSL and the official retail banking partner of the NWSLPA.
