NEW YORK (July 14, 2023) -

The National Women’s Soccer League announced today it has issued a fine to San Diego Wave FC head coach Casey Stoney for comments detrimental to the league made on social media on July 9, 2023, in violation of Section 12.2.5 (e) “Prejudicial Statements and Public Criticism” of the League Operations Manual.
