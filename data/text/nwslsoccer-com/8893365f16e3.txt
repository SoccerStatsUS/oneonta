The stage is set for a high-energy clash in the nation’s capital as Washington Spirit host Bay FC this weekend. Whether you're tuning in for the drama, the talent, or the storylines that keep the NWSL electric, this matchup has it all. Here are five reasons to keep your eyes on this one:

Bay FC's inaugural season has been bolstered by standout performances from their rookies. Midfielder Taylor Huff, a recent U-23 USYNT call-up, has showcased her attacking prowess with multiple near-goals. During her two years at Florida State University, Huff tallied 17 goals and 16 assists, playing a pivotal role in their 2023 National Championship run. Her competitive spirit and knack for goal-scoring make her a player to watch for the Bay.​

Defender Karlie Lema, a UC Berkeley standout, scored a breathtaking goal in Bay FC's home opener—a long-distance strike following an end-to-end run. At Cal, Lema scored 31 goals in 76 appearances, ranking sixth on the Bears' all-time scoring list. Her speed and finishing ability add a dynamic edge to Bay FC's attack.​

Additionally, midfielder Hannah Bebar, who debuted last weekend, is another rookie who will surely make significant contributions for the team. However, Bay FC head coach Albertin Montoya has recently said Bebar is headed back to finish her semester at Duke so don't expect her until school is out.

Audi Field has quickly become one of the loudest venues in the league, with Spirit fans showing up strong week after week, selling out their last three home games and making it a spirited environment. That home field advantage has made D.C. a tough place for visiting teams, and Bay FC will have to stay composed under pressure if they want to flip the script.

This game serves as a rematch of last season's NWSL quarterfinal, where the Spirit narrowly defeated Bay FC in a tense contest, ending the expansion club's inaugural playoff run. Bay FC will be eager to avenge that loss, while the Spirit aim to get a win at home. This matchup offers both teams a chance to showcase their growth since that 2024 playoff encounter.​

In its inaugural season, Bay FC struggled against the Spirit, failing to secure a point in both regular-season meetings. However, with an improved roster and the infusion of dynamic rookies like Huff and Lema, Bay FC is well-positioned to challenge this trend.

Washington Spirit forward Trinity Rodman, a fan favorite, is still seeking her first goal of the 2025 season. Last weekend, she came tantalizingly close, netting a clinical finish just minutes after coming off the bench, only for it to be disallowed for offside. Known for her electrifying pace, creativity, and relentless drive, Rodman remains a constant threat on the field. Fans and opponents alike will be watching closely for some signature "Trin Spins" and home-field heroics as she aims to join the Golden Boot race.​

Whether you're in the stands at Audi Field or watching from home, it's going to be a must-watch game from start to finish.​ Don’t miss the action this Friday at 8:00 PM ET on Prime Video!
