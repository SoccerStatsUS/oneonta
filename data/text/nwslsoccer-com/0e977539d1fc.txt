The quarterfinal round of the 2024 NWSL Playoffs, presented by Google Pixel, kicking off the weekend of November 8-10, has officially been set.

The expanded eight-team quarterfinal round begins Friday, November 8, on Prime Video at 8 p.m. ET as the No. 1 seed Orlando Pride, who clinched the NWSL Shield, presented by CarMax,at the beginning of the month, hosts the No. 8 seed Chicago Red Stars at Inter&Co Stadium.

The fourth-seeded Kansas City Current hosts the No. 5 seed North Carolina Courage in the lone quarterfinal match on Saturday, November 9, at CPKC Stadium, kicking off at 12 p.m. ET. The match will air live on the CBS Television Network and stream on Paramount+.

The No. 2 seed Washington Spirit opens ABC’s doubleheader coverage on Sunday, November 10, with a 12:30 p.m. ET match against No. 7 Bay FC at Audi Field in Washington, D.C.

ABC’s Sunday doubleheader coverage continues at 3 p.m. ET as No. 3 NJ/NY Gotham FC, the defending NWSL Champion, rounds out quarterfinal round action at Red Bull Arena in Harrison, New Jersey against No. 6 Portland Thorns FC.

The 2024 NWSL Playoffs, presented by Google Pixel, will air across a variety of platforms for viewers tuning in outside the United States, including NWSL+. Further information regarding international distribution will be available in the coming weeks.
