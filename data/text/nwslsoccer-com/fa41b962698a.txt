It’s here! Check out the 2024 NWSL regular season schedule.The season kicks off at 1 p.m. ET on Saturday, March 16 with Kansas City Current vs. Portland Thorns FC live on ABC.

HOW TO WATCH THE NWSL THIS SEASON

In 2024, you can watch 121 NWSL matches with our partners:

Weekly Friday night matches on Prime Video.

Weekly Saturday night double-headers on Scripps-owned ION network.

Regular season matches on the CBS Television Network and Paramount+, with additional matches airing on CBS Sports Network.

ESPN will air matches across ABC, ESPN, ESPN2, ESPN Deportes and ESPN+.

INTRODUCING NWSL+

All remaining matches will be available for free on NWSL+, the league’s first-ever domestic direct to consumer streaming platform. Learn more about how to get streaming access to live match coverage, highlights, select replays and additional league and club content. You can use the NWSL+ app on AppleTV, Roku, FireTV, Android, or iOS. Details regarding international distribution will be announced in the coming weeks.

OPENING WEEK AND CPKC STADIUM KICKOFF

The Kansas City Current host Portland Thorns FC in the inaugural match at CPKC Stadium, the first purpose-built stadium for a women’s professional sports team. The regular season opener, set for a 1 p.m. ET kickoff on Saturday, March 16, will air live on ABC. Fans will get their first look at the NWSL’s newest expansion sides live on ION, as Utah Royals FC make their return to the league at home against the Chicago Red Stars and Bay FC debuts on the road against Southern California rival Angel City FC.
