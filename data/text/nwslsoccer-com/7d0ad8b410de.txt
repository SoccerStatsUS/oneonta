It had been six long weeks in NWSL action without the back-to-back Golden Boot winner and league MVP getting a goal. So when Temwa Chawinga did finally got her first goal of the 2026 season, she had to put the league on notice.

Chawinga didn't just score her first goal of the year; she scored her first three, earning her first NWSL hat trick. It's only right that the first regular season hat trick in Kansas City Current club history belongs to TC6.

The first goal came in the 22nd minute against the Chicago Stars with an assist from Croix Bethune. It wouldn't be until the opening minutes of the second half that Chawinga would break through again to double the Current's lead, but she'd waste no time to complete the trio. After scoring in the 47th minute, Chawinga got on the board for her third in the 49th minute of the match, thanks to a Michelle Cooper assist.

Patrick Mahomes, Chiefs quarterback and part owner of the Kansas City Current showed his approval, tipping his cap to Chawinga.

Chawinga's performance led the Current to a 3-0 win over the Chicago Stars, the club's first shutout performance of the season.
