The U-20 Women's World Cup has always been a can't-miss event for soccer fans eager to witness the rise of future stars. This year’s tournament is particularly thrilling, with a record number of 11 NWSL players taking part, underscoring the league's status as a prime destination for emerging talent.

As these promising players step onto the global stage to represent their countries, fans have the opportunity to see the future of the NWSL in action. We've got all the details you need, from which NWSL players are representing their countries to when and where to watch them!
