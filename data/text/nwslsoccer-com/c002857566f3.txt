The shortlist for the most prestigious award in soccer, the Ballon d'Or Féminin, was announced on September 8. The Ballon d'Or is awarded to the best player in the world for the previous season by "France Football" magazine, and four NWSL players made the cut.

Barbra Banda led the nominations for the NWSL for her 14 goals across performances for both the Orlando Pride and Zambia. It's Banda's third-straight nomination for the award. With her 14th goal of the season this weekend, Banda sets a new single-season scoring record for the club.

Kansas City's Temwa Chawinga and Gotham's Rose Lavelle both earned their second nominations. Chawinga is the back-to-back NWSL MVP and Golden Boot winner, as well as the most recent WAFCON Golden Boot winner for Malawi. Lavelle scored six goals despite missing the first part of the season and went on to score the game-winning goal for Gotham in the 2025 NWSL Championship.

KC Current goalkeeper Lorena earned the fourth nomination for her record-setting performance. The 2025 NWSL Goalkeeper of the Year put up 16 clean sheets, including nine-straight matches, and a record-setting 869 consecutive shutoutt minutes.

Lorena and Gotham's Ann-Katrin Berger were nominated for Women's Goalkeeper of the Year. Berger, who conceded just 24 goals and earned 23 clean sheets in the qualificaiton period, has been nominated both years of the award's existence.
