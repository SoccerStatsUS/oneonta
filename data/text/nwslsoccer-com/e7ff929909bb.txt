In sports world, schedule release is the one day a year a club's social media team can flex their creative muscles and no other club can say anything about it.

The 2026 NWSL season schedule dropped last week, and all 16 clubs had some fun with the news.

There were dogs, art museums, and lots of nostalgia. From mysterious strangers with familiar names to a 5-year-old renaming every club, the teams brought it this year. You can check them out below.
