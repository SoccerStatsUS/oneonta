Every NWSL player’s career started with stepping onto the pitch the first time, and one lucky fan got a glimpse of what a pro career could look like thanks to e.l.f. As part of the 2025 e.l.f. Glow For Glory competition, 13-year-old Minka Lawson won an exclusive day in the life of a pro.

Minka was one of the youth players from all over the country to submit videos of their soccer skills, fancy footwork, and athleticism. The five finalists were invited to the 2025 NWSL Championship to compete in a skills challenge in San Francisco.

Minka was crowned the inaugural winner and chose to spend her day in the life of a pro at the Chicago Stars home opener. With her parents, Minka visited Chicago, attended training, got soccer tips and tricks from her idol, and even got her own Stars locker.

After Minka walked out on the field side-by-side the Stars put on a show at Northwestern Medical Field at Martin Stadium, defeating the Kansas City Current, 2-1.

Meeting your soccer hero and the W? It doesn’t get much better than that.
