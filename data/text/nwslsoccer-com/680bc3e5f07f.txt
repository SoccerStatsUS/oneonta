NEW YORK (May 1, 2025) - The National Women’s Soccer League (NWSL) announced today that Gotham FC forward Esther González has been named the Player of the Month, presented by EA SPORTS, for the month of April by the NWSL Media Association and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent.

The NWSL’s current Golden Boot leader, González scored a whopping seven goals throughout the month of April, including three braces and two game-winning tallies. The forward has found the back of the net in each of Gotham FC’s last four contests.

With a league-best 3.47 expected goals, the Spanish international’s attacking prowess was instrumental in propelling Gotham FC to a 3-1-0 record for the month.

The NWSL Player of the Month, presented by EA SPORTS, is selected by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league consistently, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. Awards for the month of April are based on performances during NWSL regular season matches.
