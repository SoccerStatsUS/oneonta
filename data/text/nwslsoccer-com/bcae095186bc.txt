April 19: Houston Dash (A) – Challenge Cup Opener <br><u>What to Know</u> : <br>Finalists in the 2022 season, the Kansas City Current are undoubtedly one of the favorites to again reach the NWSL Championship after a savvy offseason and the return of some of the team’s most important players set it up for success again this year. <br>On the additions, it’s impossible to overstate how significant the signing of Brazilian star Debinha is, especially considering she is joined by another impressive duo of midfielders in Morgan Gautrat and Vanessa DiBernardo, who captained Chicago last year. In defense, Hanna Glas joins after an impressive career at Bayern Munich and at the international level and will bring even more solidity to a back line that sees the return of goalkeeper Adrianna ‘AD’ Franch and defenders Kate Del Fava, Alex Loera, Hailie Mace, and more. <br>The Current don’t have a weak spot either, considering the likes of forwards Michelle Cooper, who capped off a phenomenal career as a Duke Blue Devil by winning the nation’s Player of the Year award, and Mimmi Larsson, who won two league title with FC Rosengard in the Swedish league, bolster the club’s attack. Those two only add to a forward line that boasts Kristen Hamilton, CeCe Kizer, and Alexa Spaanstra, once again demonstrating the Current’s intentions to set the pace under the direction of head coach Matt Potter. <br>Though the club have had mixed results in preseason, losing to Orlando and drawing with Chicago before capping off their run-in with a win over San Diego, but there’s little reason to believe that come Saturday they won’t be rearing and ready to go. <br>
