“Football is family” took on a whole different meaning on Monday night.

Before the Detroit Lions beat the Baltimore Ravens under the Monday Night Football lights, the Reign’s Jordyn Bugg made an appearance in a fun, unexpected way – in her cousin’s warmup fit.

Detroit’s Terrion Arnold rocked a custom made shirt with Bugg’s face plastered all over with “Dreamathon” written on the back. He’s not just a super fan of the Reign and US Women’s National Team defender, he’s family.

Bugg shared the picture posted by the Lions to her Instagram story in approval. Both Bugg and Arnold are in their second pro seasons, just two defenders making football their family business.

We love to see an NWSL x NFL crossover.

With the Lions playing the Browns at 1 p.m. on Sunday, it can be a family double-header. You can watch Bugg and the Reign take on the North Carolina Courage on Sunday, Sept. 28 at 8 p.m. ET on NWSL+ and Paramount+.
