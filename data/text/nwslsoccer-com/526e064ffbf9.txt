NEW YORK (March 27, 2026) - Following a review by the NWSL Disciplinary Committee, the National Women’s Soccer League has issued an additional one-game suspension to Portland Thorns FC defender Reyna Reyes for an incident that occurred in the 57th minute of the club’s match against Seattle Reign FC on March 20.

Following an in-game review of the incident, Reyes received a red card resulting in an automatic one-match suspension that was served during Portland’s March 25 match against San Diego Wave FC.

After further review of the altercation, the Disciplinary Committee determined that Reyes’ conduct violated Section 12.4.11, “Major Game Misconduct,” of the League Operations Manual.

As a result, Reyes has been issued an additional one-match suspension, increasing her total suspension to two matches.

Reyes will serve her final suspension during the club’s March 28 match against the Kansas City Current.
