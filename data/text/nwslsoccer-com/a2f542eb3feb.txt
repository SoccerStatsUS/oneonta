NEW YORK (July 3, 2025) - The National Women’s Soccer League (NWSL) announced today that Portland Thorns FC forward Pietra Tordin has been named Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, for the month of June by the NWSL Media Association and the league’s on-air broadcast talent.

Tordin scored her first career goal in May and she kept the ball rolling in June. The rookie was a difference maker in Portland’s last two games of the month, both wins to keep their home unbeaten streak alive. Tordin scored the game-winner against the Chicago Stars, which sent the Thorns into the break on a high note.

Ally, a financial services company with the nation’s largest all-digital bank and a leading brand in women’s sports, is also a primary sponsor of the NWSL and the official retail banking partner of the NWSLPA.

The NWSL Rookie of the Month, presented by Ally, is selected by the NWSL Media Association, a collection of writers that cover the league consistently, and the NWSL’s on-air broadcast talent. Awards for the month of June are based off performances during regular season matches.
