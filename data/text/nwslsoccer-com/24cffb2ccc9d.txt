NEW YORK (April 2, 2026) \- Following a review by the NWSL Disciplinary Committee, the National Women’s Soccer League has issued a one-match suspension to Boston Legacy FC forward Ella Stevens for an incident that occurred in the 36th minute of the club’s match against Utah Royals FC on March 28.

Stevens initially received a yellow card for a challenge on a Royals defender during the match. Upon further review, the Disciplinary Committee determined that the incident rose to the level of a violation of Section 12.4.11, “Major Game Misconduct,” of the League Operations Manual.

As a result, Stevens will receive a one-match suspension, to be served during Boston’s April 3 match against San Diego Wave FC.
