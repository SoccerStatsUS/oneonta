There’s a lot of pressure for a new player on a new team to make a good impression in their debut. Add the fact that that debut is on your birthday, and well, no one would fault an average player for jitters.

But 2024 NWSL Rookie and Midfielder of the Year Croix Bethune is no average player.

In her club debut for the Kansas City Current, Bethune scored the opening goal, immediately ingratiating herself to the soldout crowd at CPKC Stadium. And did we mention it was her 25th birthday?

It was nothing but net for your favorite Pisces’s favorite Pisces.

In KC birthday goal tradition, Bethune blew out the candles on an imaginary cake for the celly, just like her teammate Temwa Chawinga did last season.

Have yourself a day, Croix!
