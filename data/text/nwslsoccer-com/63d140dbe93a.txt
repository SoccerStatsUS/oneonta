It’s set to be an action-packed week in the NWSL, with 6 matches on the schedule, a close Race to the Shield race and players approaching several milestones. In addition to several teams looking to clinch playoff spots, there a quite a few individual milestones to watch for this week.

Wednesday highlights

Portland clinched a playoff berth for the 8th time in club history (all but one NWSL season that has featured playoffs). This is the second straight season that the Thorns have clinched a playoff spot first.

Haley Bugeja ORL made her NWSL debut Wednesday, becoming the 4th youngest player to play in NWSL at age 18 years, 4.5 months (the three younger players are Olivia Moultrie POR, Jaedyn Shaw SD and Ellie Carpenter POR). A total of 11 players have made their NWSL debut as teenagers.

Megan Rapinoe RGN tied the league record for career regular-season PK goals when she converted the game-winner against Gotham Wednesday. She tied former Reign teammate Kim Little with 12 PKG. With the goal, she also set a new club record for consecutive games recording a goal or an assist (7 games). League record is 10 games (Lauren Holiday, 2013).

Debinha NC set a new personal best for goal in one season (9) with her brace at Orlando Wednesday. Her first goal was the fastest scored by the Courage in this regular season.

Bella Bixby POR earned her 8th clean sheet to lead the league.

Five clubs are tied for most home wins this season — Angel City, Chicago, OL Reign, Portland and San Diego. <br>Future notables * Next goal for Christine Sinclair POR will be her 60th in regular-season play, all with the same club. No one has as many goals with one NWSL club, and only one player has more career regular-season goals (Sam Kerr, 77).

Next appearance for Alyssa Naeher CHI will set a new record for all-time regular season appearances by a goalkeeper (142). She's currently tied with Nicole Barnhart WAS at 141. Her next clean sheet will tie her with AD Franch KC for second on the all-time list with 40 (Barnhart has 52).

Kailen Sheridan SD is 2 saves away from the milestone of 400 regular-season saves. She is currently 4th on the all-time list, 70 saves behind Nicole Barnhart WAS at third with 468.

Katie Lund LOU has recorded 102 saves in 20 games this season. She is 6 saves away from the single-season saves record of 108 set in 2018 by Sheridan (23 games played) and Aubrey Kingsbury WAS (21 games).

Alex Morgan SD is currently 3rd on the all-time regular-season shots on target list with 196. Only Lynn Williams KC (200) and Sam Kerr (227) have recorded more.

There are 3 players sitting on 9 goals this season (Debinha NC, Ebony Salmon HOU, Ashley Hatch WAS). If any of them get to 10, it will mark the first NWSL regular season where more than 4 players record 10 goals.
