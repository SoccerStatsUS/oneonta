- Liverpool are on high alert with midfielder Adam Wharton expected to leave Crystal Palace in one of the next two transfer windows, potentially as early as January, reports TEAMtalk. Liverpool have a long-standing admiration for the 22-year-old, even though they didn't make a move in this summer as they prioritised strengthening other areas of the pitch. Palace had stood firm against any advances during the winter window as they were determined not to let him leave, but it is now highly unlikely that they will retain the midfielder. Newcastle United are also watching the situation.

- Newcastle United have agreed a new multi-year contract with left back Lewis Hall to see off interest from Manchester United, as reported by TEAMtalk. The 22-year-old has already committed his future to the Magpies and an announcement is expected later this week. Newcastle had been especially determined to keep Hall in the summer having already seen the departures of Anthony Gordon, Bruno Guimarães and Sandro Tonali.

- Chelsea are making a fresh move to sign AFC Bournemouth midfielder Alex Scott and are in a strong position to complete a deal, according to TEAMtalk. The Blues want to sign a midfielder after Enzo Fernández was allowed to join Manchester City and a deal for Monaco's Lamine Camara collapsed, with the Senegal international still being an option. Scott would welcome the opportunity to step up, but the Cherries won't offload the 23-year-old cheaply, so any deal would require a large fee and add-ons.

- Bayer Leverkusen are on the verge of extending the contract of Christian Kofane, who has impressed Arsenal, as reported by Kicker. They have taken the decision early and the 20-year-old striker's new deal will run until the summer of 2031. Kofane is seen as a prime candidate for the Premier League due to his talent and profile, while he has already impressed the Gunners since the clubs faced each other in the Champions League round of 16 last term. Leverkusen believe Kofane could command a fee that exceeds €100 million.

- Inter Milan and Juventus are both looking at Borussia Dortmund forward Samuele Inácio, as reported by transfer journalist Nicolo Schira. Both Serie A clubs had shown an interest in the 18-year-old during the summer transfer window, and they are now continuing to monitor him. Inacio has been with Dortmund since signing for their youth ranks from Atalanta in 2024.
