Manchester United talent JJ Gabriel, 15, marked his UEFA Youth League debut with a hat trick against Sabah.

The attacker is tipped as a future star and made his first appearance for the first team off the bench in last month's friendly against Atlético Madrid.

Gabriel has yet to make Michael Carrick's squad for a competitive match but the teenager, who turns 16 next month, continues to knock on the door.

He sparkled for the Under-19s in Thursday's 5-0 win against Sabah in the Youth League in front of director of football Jason Wilcox, director of recruitment Christopher Vivell and first-team coach Travis Binnion.

Amir Ibragimov and Jacob Watson scored early goals in Leigh, where Gabriel added another in a dominant first half.

Gabriel scored two more late in the second half to claim the match ball against the Azerbaijani visitors, who did not manage a shot on a day that United mustered 44 attempts.
