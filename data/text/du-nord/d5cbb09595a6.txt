TEAM USA - EL EQUIPO DE TODOS

World Cup Qualifying

There were only 3 upsets in the whole world last week: USA (lost to Jamaica), Norway (lost to Iceland) and Uruguay (lost to Colombia).

I think that was one of the worst USA losses in modern history. Yes they have been humiliated by much better teams, and had some hard to take losses in the World Cup. But this Jamaica team was not good at all. They were amazing athletes with tons of speed and strength, not to mention determination and will, but they are a bad soccer team. That is what made it such a miserable loss for the USA.

The USA has also gone three qualifiers now without a convincing performance. They could not put away a nothing Antigua + Barbuda, they had Guatemala right where they wanted them on the road but got out foxed, and then were visibly inferior to Jamaica. That is 0 for 3 under Klinsmann in my book
