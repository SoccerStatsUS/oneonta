STARTERS

Inter Milan has fired head coach Gian Piero Gasperini after just 5 games in charge. 1 loss in the Italian Super Cup. 1 loss in the UEFA Champions League. 1 tie and 2 losses in Serie A. And no signs that the team was going anywhere but down in each of those games. Its not Gasperini's fault at all. Its all on owner Massimo Moratti who keeps hiring people who are ill suited to the job (see Rafael Benitez and Leonardo last season.)

-The next manager will be #19 in the 17 years under the Moratti. Ugh.

Claudio Ranieri is the most likely to be named as the replacement.
