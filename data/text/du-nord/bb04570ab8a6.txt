Montreal has broken their own record for attendance! Their first home game of the MLS era saw them draw 58,912 on Mar 17 against the Chicago Fire. And this past weekend on May 12 they overshot that number by nearly 2000 drawing 60,860 for the LA Galaxy. Unbelievable!

-Their other two home games were Toronto FC - 23,120 - Apr 7, and Portland Timbers - 19,223 - Apr 28.

-They play their final home game at their temporary home, Olympic Park Stadium, this weekend (May 19) against New York, and then move back to their permanent home Saputo Stadium that will reopen after retrofitting on June 16 against Seattle.

PICTURE OF THE DAY

Yesterday during Manchester City's championship tour of the city a fan handed a sign to Carlos Tevez which he held up with a sort of glee on his face.

What is the story behind that sign? Turns out that Ferguson was once asked if Man City would ever challenge United for supremacy in England, to which he replied, "Not in my lifetime."

Thus Tevez obviously assumed that Ferguson must have passed away.

Later Man City issued an apology for his actions.

-Hell, I bet even Fergie laughed at that one.
