UEFA CHAMPIONS LEAGUE

Quarterfinals

Real Madrid and Schalke join Manchester United and Barcelona in the final four.

Rob Hughes of the International Herald Tribune reviews Wednesday's final two Quarterfinal games.

Schalke seemed to be the critics pick as the easiest target coming into this round, but most of them failed to note that the team had spent a lot of money over the previous 3 seasons and had set their sights on winning the Bundesliga this season. The team fell apart under coach and architect Felix Magath and had spent all of 2010/11 in the bottom half of the German league. On Mar 16 the owners decided that they had had enough and Magath was replaced by the more than capable Ralf Ragnick. Schalke lost their first game under the new coach, but have not lost since, winning 4 in the Bundesliga and now 2 in the Champions League. Manchester United had better watch out, this is a very talented and motivated team they will face.

WHY NOT!

The name of Portland's new home is very hard to say, so loyal reader Luke told us the real pronunciation - Civic Stadium - the original name. Why does Luke want us to refer the stadium that way? Here's what he has to say:

The stadium name in Portland finally pushed me over. The corporate sponsor’s name is so inanely clumsy that I can't take it any more. Soccer fans, don’t let the corporate name of your stadium stick.
