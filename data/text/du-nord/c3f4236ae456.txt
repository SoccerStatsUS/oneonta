It's Day 12 of 2018.

MLS

Atlanta has set a new MLS record for transfer fee paid. They are signing 18 year old Ezequiel Barco from Independiente in Argentina for $15 Million. Wow.

Orlando City announced that they will NOT field a team in division 2 USL this season. The team, Orlando City B, will go into moth balls and be reevaluated at a later time.

Chicago Fire forward Niko Nemanja was named the Hungarian Best Player Of The Year award.

Chicago has also signed Dax McCarty to a new contract.

Kansas City has signed both Tim Melia and Ike Opara to contract extensions.
