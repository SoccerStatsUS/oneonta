CONCACAF Champions League:

-Real Salt Lake, Santos Laguna and America all advanced to the knockout stage last night. finalizing the list of the last 8 teams. ADDED: Herediano and Tigres face off tonight for Group B spot.

-They join the other group winners: DC United, Seattle Sounders and LA Galaxy from MLS, and Quererato and Tigres UANL from Liga MX.

-This is COULD BE the first time that all 4 teams from MLS and Liga MX have advanced.

-Yes, Vancouver was in the group stage and is from MLS too, but they got there via the Canadian Championship.

-The seeding will be set after this weeks group stage games are completed on Thursday.

-Seeding is based on facts, not opinions, with #1 going to the team with the most points and the best goal differential from the group stage.

-In the Quaterfinals it will be 1v8, 2v7, 3v6 and 4v5.

-Semifinals will be 1-8 Winner v 4-5 Winner and 2-7 Winner v 3-6 Winner.

-The home and away series will begin some time in February.
