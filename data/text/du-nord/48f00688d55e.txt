My Minnesota United Notes:

Game highlights [VIDEO]

Loons played one of their most complete games of the season.

Went down a goal early after a big mistake in the wall lead to a shot which Sammy Ndjock bobbled and the Eddies buried.

Christian Ramirez grabbed 2 goals and Stefano Pinho 1 goal.

Ramirez now has 50 goals for MNUFC in 93 games.

Good games also from Ibson, JC Banks, Kevin Venegas, Justin Davis, Brent Kallman and Damien Lowe.

First time the Montons had given up 3 goals in a game this season.

Goalkeeper Matt VanOekel did not play for EDM cuz of a hamstring injury.

The assists on all 3 Minnesota goals (top notch finishing too) were great:

Ibson passed to Banks to set up Ramirez

Venegas passed to Ibson to set up Ramirez

Ramirez passed to Speas to set up Pinho

Almost everything went exactly right for Minnesota United this weekend in the battle for the final playoff position:

Minnesota won

Miami + Tampa tied

Carolina lost

The only imperfect thing was OKC + Ft Lauderdale both also won

Minnesota and OKC are tied on points but Minnesota has a +3 in goal differential.

There are 2 weeks left in the season.

Miami + Tampa play 3 times, everyone else in the race just twice.

MLS Week 33 Results:

Supporters Shield down to two - Dallas can tie and be champs.

The East is pretty much set, only seedling left to sort out.

DC and Montreal both clinched their playoff spots in the East.

Technically New England is still in the hunt a playoff spot, but they are 3 Points and a -12 Goal Differential behind Philadelphia. It would take a miracle.

In the West KC and Portland are tied on Points and Wins but KC is 1 better in Goal Differential.

Bradley Wright Phillips got 2 goals on Sunday bring his season total to 23. (To go along with 17 last season and 27 the season before that.)

BWP has 68 regular season and 6 post season goals.

By comparison Landon Donovan is the all time league leader with 145 regular season and 25 post season goals.

By the way, Landon Donovan started his first game for LA, and played 63 minutes.
