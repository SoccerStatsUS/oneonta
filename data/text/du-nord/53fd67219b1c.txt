Finally, the new FIFA rankings are out today. Italy is the new #1. How that happened, I don't know. They should have been #1 right after the World Cup, but weren't. And they haven't played all that great since then, leading one to wonder how they climbed to the top now. It's all so freakin' odd. Anyway, in news closer to home, the USA beats Denmark & Mexico to move up 3 spots to #28, while Mexico loses and still climbs 2 spots to #19. Even odder yet Denmark loses to the USA, and Honduras, while drawing with El Salvador then climbs 5 spots to #18. Its a joke right? Obviously. But really, who cares. Well, I guess we all do in some perverse egotistical way. The rankings that I have grown to like is the Elo system. The same outfit that ranks world Chess Masters. Let's check them out today: Denmark drops from 5 to 12, Mexico drops from 17 to 19, and the US climbs from 23 to 20. That's better. Brazil are still #1, with France #2 and Italy #3. Not so sure about that.
