Minnesota United laid an egg in Indy. They came with a very conservative approach that held out until a lazy pass from Lance Laing (Lazy Pass might be his new nickname) gave an opening to Indy and they got their goal. Yes MU had several chances to score and should have, but that counts the same in the standings as a red hot cup of jack squat.

Watching Christian Ramirez hit that soft PK and seeing Jon Busch save it so easily really summed up the whole game for Minnesota. Don't get cute, just blast the fucker!

Tiago Calvano (MU) and Lovell Palmer (IE) got into a kerfuffle as the game ended and were both ejected, meaning they are out for next weeks rematch.

Indy comes to Minnesota next Saturday where we hope to see the return of the high powered Loons attack.

Kei Kamara enacted revenge on Columbus Crew this weekend. Earlier this season the Crew scapegoated him for the teams troubles and sent the leagues top scorer to New England for virtually nothing. In Saturday's game Kamara got a goal and assisted on another as New England beat Columbus 3-1. He was involved in all 3 NER goals: drew multiple defenders away from teammates for first goal, had a cross punched in for an own goal, and scored while the CC defense took a nap. Also had a shot go off the crossbar, and should have drawn an obvious PK too but the ref said "Noooooo."
