The Mexican InterLiga tournament kicks off today in Houston at Robertson Stadium. All 8 teams play over the next two days in a pair of double headers.

-You can see all the games on Fox Sports en Espanol.

-The tournament is split up into 2 groups of 4 teams who will play a round robin with top two teams advancing.

-Games:

Wednesday - Group A:

Monterrey v UANL

Cruz Azul v Necaxa

Thursday - Group B:

Chiapas v Morelia

America v UAG

-The teams then move to Dallas's Pizza Hut Park for two more double headers on Sat & Sun, before moving to the final group stage games next Tue & Wed in LA at Home Depot Center.

-The top 2 teams from each group will play off at HDC for the title on Sun Jan 13.

-The winner gets a spot in the upcoming Copa Libertadores.
