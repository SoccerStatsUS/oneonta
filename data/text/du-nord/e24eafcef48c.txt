STORY OF THE DAY

The BBC with a story on a great and barely known South African player who we have honored before here at du Nord. The late great Ace Ntsoelengoe.

-I know him as a star on my childhood team the Minnesota Kicks. To me he was their great player. He also hit the great goal I have ever seen in person, a bombing line drive from about 40 yards out that stunned an entire Metropolitan Stadium in Bloomington MN!

I don't know if I have ever had a story that so many people sent me the link too. That is how much he is revered around here.
