Michael Lewis of Big Apple Soccer is reporting that the New York Red Bulls team is for sale, and that FC Barcelona is the potential buyer!

Wow! Wow! Wow!

Then again it's not much of a surprise, as Red Bull has shown no real clue how to run a team in MLS. Yes, the stadium building project is beyond parallel in the league, but the rest of their project has been miserable most of the time.

ADDITION: Coach Juan Carlos Osorio has stepped down from the team. Assistant Richie Williams will become interim coach yet again.
