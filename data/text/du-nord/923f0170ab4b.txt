2011-12 CONCACACHAMPIONS

Semifinals

Toronto was able to put up a good fight and hold Santos Laguna to a 1-1 tie. Not really enough to give them any advantage going to Torreon next week but at least it will be game on at kick off.

And not surprising to anyone - The Santos Laguna goal was scored by Herculez Gomez! Fantastic!

The biggest moment of the match happened as the whistle blew and both benches cleared and the teams went at it. Word has it that a Santos player hit a TFC player to set it off. A second Santos player got a red card in the incident after another had been sent off during the game. The end of the 2nd Leg could be a wild affair.

It still stuns me after all these years how many times we see Mexican teams involved in scrapes after tight or lost games. Amazing.

Defending champs Monterrey stuffed Pumas in the second match of the night.

Ryan Wolstadt of the Toronto Sun writes that TFC deserved to win the game, not just tie.

Duncan Fletcher of Sports Net calls TFC Jekyll & Hyde because their CCL form has been so much better than their MLS form, but I think its pretty obvious that the team has put all their eggs in this one basket knowing that bad results in a couple of MLS game will not make or break their season, where as you only get a couple CCL game to get it right.

-If I was a GM or head coach I would do things the exact same way that Toronto has.
