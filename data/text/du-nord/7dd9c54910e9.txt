-Sat May 28

NSC Minnesota Stars v - 7:30pm CT

Venue: National Sports Center - Blaine MN

Anyone bringing new people to the game can enter the contest to win prizes.

If you are planning to come to the game this Saturday its suggested that you bring a friend that has never been to a game before and then make sure to stop by the Dark Clouds Pre-Game Tailgate to win.

You can buy tickets right at the tailgate and save some money on the price, unless of course you are going to use your season tickets.

(The team is also unveiling their new alternate kit at this game.)

Here are some of the details for the contest:

-The tailgate area will have ballot boxes.

-Drawing for 5 scarves and 1 jersey will be done at the tailgate area at 7:05PM.

-Each new person will be entered to win a scarf.

-Each regular attendee is eligible to win the new alternate kit. They will enter their name 1 time for every 1 new person they bring (ex. bring 10 people, get 10 chances to win).

-The big winner will go on the field at half time to be presented their jersey in front of the crowd.
