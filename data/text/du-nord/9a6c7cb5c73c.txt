MLS TEAM & PLAYER NEWS

General News

The 2012 Expansion Draft will take place on Wed Nov 23. This will be Montreal's first shot at stocking their new roster with current MLS players. They will get 10 picks from the other 18 teams. Those other teams cannot lose more than 1 player, but can only protect 11 on their current roster. Players signed to Generation Adidas or academy contracts cannot be chosen by Montreal.
