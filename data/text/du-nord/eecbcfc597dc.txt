The draw was held today in Brazil.

And from the looks of things this is about as easy a path as the USA could hope for. No real threats until the Hexagon.

The USA enters in Round 3 (of 4) and has been drawn into Group A with Jamaica & the winners of what look to be the 2 weakest groups from the previous round (Rd 2) E & F.

-Rd 2 - Group E: Grenada, Guatemala, St Vincent & Grenadines, Belize. Group F: Haiti, Antigua & Barbuda, Curacao, US Virgin Islands

-Top two from Rd 3 go to Hexagon!

-Top three from Hex go to World Cup, and 4th place goes to home and away playoff with the winner from Oceania for another WC spot.
