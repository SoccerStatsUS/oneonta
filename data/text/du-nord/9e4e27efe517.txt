STAT OF THE DAY

MLS has turned to the same player rating index that FIFA uses to give us some indication of how much effect a player is truly having on a game.

Here is how the index works.

And here is the first ever ranking.

The top rated player is Thierry Henry.

The three lowest rated players that have been on the field for at least 100 minutes are Adin Brown, Zach Thornton and Nate Jaqua.

My biggest problem with a ratings system like this is that it does not reward the risk takers. It only rates them with the same degree of difficulty as everyone else. But there are those special players that may try to do something 3 times and fail, which deducts from their score, then nails it on the 4th try and its one of the plays that almost no one else even attempts.

I also find that it is not kind to goalkeepers most of the time.

MY THOUGHTS

For LA Juan Pablo Angel used a basketball move to help Landon Donovan score LA's goal. It was like he was blocking out Dan Califf under the basket getting in position for a rebound.

Donovan has scored in 5 consecutive games.

Angel is still not scoring for LA (I think he has one goal), but he is effective.

Carlos Ruiz in Philly on the other hand is anything but effective.

I really like the Keon Daniels plays for the Union, but then he gets subbed off at half time.

How huge of a pickup has Faryd Mondragon been? What a leader!

The Union went to 3 in the back around the 80th minute, and it paid off with a goal in the 84th. Great move by the coach.

Of course the goal was helped immensely by a stupid move from LA fullback Sean Franklin who laid out a weak backpass that Danny Mwanga easily scooped then rounded the keeper and fired home.

We saw another of those microscopic crowds in Dallas. Sad.

Brek Shea was back in the Dallas midfield and just tearing up and down the left wing almost at will.

This Richard Eckersley guy in the back for Toronto is a very reckless player, and then seconds after I write that he takes out Dallas's Brek Shea with a terrible flying tackle from behind. Not only did he leg connect with Shea's ribs but his forearm cracked him in the back of the head. Eckersley got a yellow. Well earned.

The TFC attack is ok, but there is still that extra thing missing.

TFC had a PK called against them late in the first half which the coach complained about after the game. I saw Adrian Cann with two hands on the shoulders of George John that spun him around and pulled him to the ground. Foul. End of story.

We see this kind of PK called all over the world on a regular basis. If you don't want the call to happen then don't put your hands on the guy! Easy solution.

A TFC assistant was tossed out of the game for arguing the call.

Daniel Hernandez converted the penal for FCD. It was his first goal since Aug 02!

TFC's Joao Plata at 5'2" is the shortest player ever in MLS. I thought Franco Niell was about 4'9"?

Marvin Chavez was all over the field for Dallas like an erratic squirrel. (I did not write erotic, but you thought it.) But he was not getting the job done.

Jackson on the other hand was amazing for Dallas! Wow! Defensively NOTHING got past him, he attacked with ferocity and his passing was great. Man of the Match. And maybe Player of the Week.

Both teams had guys booked for diving.

This was FCD keeper Kevin Hartman's 96th shut out. In honor of that lets listen to Question Mark and the Mysterians perform their hit song 96 Tears. Listen or download here.

Chris Wondolowski had a fantastic behind the leg goal for San Jose. See it here.

Vancouver midfielder Davide Chiumiento played like one of the best players in the league again last night.

Both teams had ample looks at goal in stoppage time but neither could execute and the both got 1 point.
