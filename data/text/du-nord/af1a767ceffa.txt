STARTERS

It was a rare double for me last night as both my Minnesota Stars won and the USA won.

-Minnesota ended a 9 game streak without a win, and clinched a playoff spot in a 4-0 rout of Puerto Rico. (2nd time this season the Stars beat the Islanders by that scoreline at home.)

-The USA dominated Jamaica but needed a 2nd half free kick to actually get the points. Many other chances in the 1st half were stopped by the Jamaican keeper Dwayne Miller who plays for Syrianska in Sweden.

Mexico clinched their spot on the CONCACAF Hexagonal for World Cup qualifying with their 4th win in as many games.

The raves were in for the crowd in Columbus. I can't wait to hear from my friends who were in attendance what they thought.

-And I can't wait to be attendance myself on Oct 16 in Kansas City where I know the crowd can be off the hook awesome.

Great freekick goal by Herculez Gomez won the game, in the 55th min, a little less than 30 yards from goal, straight down the middle of the field. Dempsey drew the foul. Herculez capitalized putting his shot the far right side where Jamaican keeper Dwayne Miller could only tip as it went into the back of the net. (Terrible goalkeeping on that play by the way.)

-Gomez also earned a Penalty Kick in the second half that the referee waved off.

Graham Zusi did well in his debut. How he didn't score we will never know. 3 great chances, 1 great save, 1 off the bar and 1 near miss.

Great to see Danny Williams finally play a game for the USA in his normal everyday position. He was very positive and productive. And should have scored a spectacular goal, only for a few inches of goal post.

Steve Cherundolo was also a big presence in the game Cherun and Doloing up and down the right flank all game long.
