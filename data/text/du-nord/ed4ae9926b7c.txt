CONCACAF has announced a new Champions League format! Now to sift through the rubble and see if its any good. By Jeff Carlisle of ESPNFC.

My Notes:

The Caribbean and Central American teams will playoff from August to October.

That winner will join teams from Liga MX (4), MLS (4) and champs from Canada, Caribbean Cup, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Honduras, Panama and El Salvador in a 2nd playoff from Feb to May.

The tournaments will most likely be set up bracket style, with each pairing featuring a home and away match.

But I am guessing that there will be a draw before each round to decide the adversaries.

This format does nothing to fix the MLS team's issue of being in preseason form when the key games kick off in February.
