STORY OF THE DAY

There is a storm brewing among supporters of the USA men's national team about the way they were treated last Saturday night at the Rose Bowl. All out hostility and violence is what a good many USA fans got in the stands from Mexican fans. We all know that if the tables were turned and American fans did this to Mexicans it would be an international incident. There should be no double standard, and US Soccer and CONCACAF need to stand up and make this right.

-This doesn't even get into the last time the USA played in Mexico City and how poorly we were treated there. Not cool.
