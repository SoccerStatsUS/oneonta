FIFASCANDALIZATIONINGS

A series of happy Tweets from Herr Blatter today on reforming FIFA:

@SeppBlatter

-Historic day for FIFA’s reform process; two-chamber Ethics Committee approved.

-One chamber investigates and the other chamber acts as a judge

-I proposed this in June 2011. It is also the main point made by Pieth’s Independent Committee

-New Audit & Compliance Committee also well received – another milestone.

-Delighted that the Executive Committee has agreed to back me on this crucial reform

-Particularly happy that the ExCo backed my proposal to have a female member

-Football is for men and women. Vital to have a female voice on the Committee

Those messages were all posted before the report came out so you knew nothing negative toward Blatter was on the way.

The Independent Governance Committee that was set up to suggest reforms for FIFA has filed their initial report. Read it and weep.

-One person on Twitter has already called it the shuffling of the deck chairs.

The document basically says FIFA is hiding things, and that an investigative committee should be formed, but does not call for any specific investigations, and says nothing about looking in to the bids for the 2018 and 22 World Cups. Very sad.
