STORY OF THE DAY

USA coach Bob Bradley was in Sweden this past weekend and watched Alejandro Bedoya play for Orebro, and then talked to the teams website and told them more in a few words then he ever tells the American media. Why is that?

About his World Cup roster plans (translated from English to Swedish and back again): "We have a clear frame of 16-17 players, and behind it there are many who fight for a place. Alejandro is in there. We will have a camp at home in May. There are around 30 players present, then the second phase of the camp to remain in South Africa when the squad will be reduced to the 23 players..."

See the full article here, mostly about Bedoya obviously, in Swedish.

QUOTE OF THE DAY

Jose Torres on why he has struggled to have a bigger impact with Team USA: "The game is a little faster. The position is played the same way, but the game is faster so you have to react faster. [Bradley] puts me in my place, but the soccer is much faster and you can't hold the ball as much. What he asks me to do, I just have to do it."
