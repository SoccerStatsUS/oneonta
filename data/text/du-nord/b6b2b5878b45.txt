STARTERS

I believe that soccer leagues around the world spending millions of dollars for electronic goal-line technology that will be used only on rare occasions is NOT a good use of money at all. That money can be spent on more players, and a fraction of it can be spent hiring more referees to patrol the area at each goal. A much much much much better use of money.

-And to make it pretty funny I wrote the preceding before the "goal" by Ukraine was not given. Yes it sure looked like a goal on TV replays, but the referee standing right on the line made his call that we all have to live with. Was it a goal or not? Would electronic goal-line technology have called it a goal? We will never no. But I still stand by my opinion. Yes refs get it wrong, but if we held players to the same perfection standards we held referees then there would be no players left to field a team.
