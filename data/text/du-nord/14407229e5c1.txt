USMNT - World Cup Qualifying

-Tue Sep 5

Honduras 1-1 USA - 4:30pm CT on beIN

Estadio Olimpico Metropolitano, San Pedro Sula

My USMNT Notes:

Whew...

Why did it seem like the USA spend so much of their possession just lumping the ball forward to no one. Was this the actual plan? I hope not. It was brutal.

When 3 of your 4 defenders have genuinely bad games your odds of winning are very very small.

So weird that Graham Zusi was one of the best USA players against Costa Rica last Friday and then last night was one of the worst. So weird.

The good part was that ALL THREE subs, Wood, Arriola and Cameron, had a real impact on the final score of the game. They brought energy, new attacking options and ultimately the tying goal from Wood.

Every game in Central America is a crazy, ugly affair no matter what year it is. Expecting something else is foolish.

Good game: Cameron, Arriola, Wood

Average game: Guzan, Besler, Acosta, Bradley, Dempsey, Morris, Nagbe, Pulisic

Bad game: Beasley, Gonzalez, Zusi

My final thought - Arena is going with experienced players, and I understand that, but even he has said that the World Cup is about young players, and I think he needs more of them in qualifying.

What is the formula for USMNT qualifying now? By Avi Creditor for Sports Illustrated.

What is going on with the USMNT defense, and how can it be remedied? By Scott French for Four Four Two.

Holger Czukay, bass player in the great German band Can, has passed away at age 79.

Their albums Tago Mago, Ege Bamyasi and Future Days are all essential listening.
