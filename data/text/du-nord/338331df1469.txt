My MLS Cup Notes:

I don't know how Seattle pulled this off.

Toronto sure didn't deserve to lose the championship game, but that they did. They dominated the game at several points, and limited Seattle's attack to almost nothing, yet Penalty Kicks killed them.

Both teams smothered the others creative players: Altidore, Giovinco, Morris and Lodeiro all had little impact on the game at all.

There was no real midfield play to speak of. A lot of the game was just one team roaring from one end to the other and then not being able to do anything, so the other team took the ball and raced back the other direction. Oof.

Seattle keeper Stefan Frei was the Man Of The Match. He made several key saves, but one stood out as the key play in the entire game:

The save of a lifetime. #MLSCup

A video posted by Major League Soccer (@mls) on Dec 11, 2016 at 8:37am PST

The giant Sounders centerback Roman Torres scored the championship winning Penalty Kick. His hair reminds me of Godzilla's spine and tail.

Michael Bradley had the worst Penalty Kick attempt of anyone. It was soft, slow and only slightly to the left of the keeper. He is going to have many sleepless nights after that one.

On the other hand Bradley controlled large parts of the game, breaking up Seattle and pinging long passes all over the field.

Toronto's defense was really good. How good? Seattle registered ZERO shots on goal.

How in the world did Toronto not win? I think a lot of people will be pondering that for a long time to come.
