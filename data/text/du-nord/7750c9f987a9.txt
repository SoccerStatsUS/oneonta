LOS LOONS

MN has signed 24 year old Cameroonian midfielder Frantz Pangop from Union Douala. Press release from MNUFC.

Very little is known about the player.

He was a sub for Cameroon in 2 World Cup qualifiers. (They had already been eliminated.) In game 5 of 6 he came on as sub in the 87th minute, and immediately scored a goal in the 88th!

He is not list on the current Doula roster, so its hard to know when he left there.
