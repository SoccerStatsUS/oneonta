FACT OF THE DAY

European courts have ruled that people in England can buy decoder cards from other countries around the EU to unscramble TV satellite feeds and watch football from everywhere. Wow.

-So how are leagues supposed to sell their viewing rights now? Simply put, they can't sell them country by country. You can assume the legal battles on this one have just begun.
