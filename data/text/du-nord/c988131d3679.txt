du Nord MLS Player Of The Week

Marcelo Gallardo - Midfielder - DC United

How easy was this call? Have you watched him the past couple of weeks. Wow. He finally has mastered this team and is not turning up the notches on their victims. He may have had 3 or 4 on Sunday if his finishing was a little better. But right now he seems to be everywhere in the attack. Do you need someone to relieve pressure? There he is. Do you need a quick give and go? There he is. Do you need a long ball over the top? There he is. He moves from side to side and goal box to midfield with a sleepy stealth. He goes out of the TV picture, and then turns up seconds later in EXACTLY the right spot. Amazing. Sure he clobbered Landon Donovan and should have been Red Carded, and a couple weeks back he semi-head butted Brandon Prideaux of Chicago and could have seen Red for that too. Even more so, I was unsure if he was ready to commit to this league early on. But with each passing week I ask myself, Christian who?
