SPAIN

du Nord recap: Real Madrid stumbled for the 4th time this season, drawing 1-1 at Almeria and are now 6 points behind Barcelona who cruised to another victory. 3rd place Villarreal won as well, and are a pretty good team overall, but they are 13 point out of the lead.

GERMANY

du Nord recap: Dortmund is a force of nature right now winning big again and running their lead to 12 points over #2 Hannover (who won), 13 points over #3 Mainz & #4 Leverkusen (who both lost), and 16 over Bayern Munich who blew a late lead and drew.

ITALY

du Nord recap: Top team Milan here held to a draw, but #2 Napoli failed to gain ground by only drawing as well, while #3 Lazio, #4 Roma and #5 Juve all won.

dN

Inter (currently in 6th) has a big chance to move the table up on Wed when they host Cesena. They are now (having won four in a row under new coach Leonardo) 9 points off the lead with two games in hand.

FRANCE

du Nord recap: #1 Lille continues to win, with #2,3,4 & 5 PSG, Rennes, Lyon and Marseille following suit. There is only a 3 point difference between the 5 teams.
