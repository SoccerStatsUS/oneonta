Back to Saturday's match: US v Argentina is expected to have over 65,000 on hand by kickoff. It snowed on Tuesday at the stadium, but it should be in good shape for gameday.

If you can't be there like Dear Leader, the match will be broadcast live on ESPN2 and Univision, and online at ESPN3.com. Fans can also follow the match on ussoccer.com's MatchTracker and on Twitter @ussoccer.

The US will be playing in their new, third kit, featuring Red jerseys including a slogan provided by the Twitter community. Steve Cherundolo (groin), Stuart Holden (knee) and Zak Whitbread (back) are all out of the match.
