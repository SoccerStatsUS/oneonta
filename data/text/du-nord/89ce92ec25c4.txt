du Nord MLS Player Of The Week

Joe Franchino - Los Angeles

I know some people are going to think I am an idiot for this one, and I admit I kinda think that myself, but without Joey watching their backs Donovan and Beckham could never have taken Chivas USA apart. Franchino played like a veteran possessed. He had one role and that was to interrupt the CUSA attack before it began, and then have the calm to make the right pass springing his teammates going the other way. And he did all this with out Nick Garcia style cheap shot tactics. Oddly he played it more like Michael Parkhurst and anticipated the Goats moves before they themselves did. I can't tell you how strange it feels to sitting here writing heaps of praise for a guy that I have used as an example on countless occasions to say: We will know MLS has truly arrived when guys with little skill like Joe Franchino can't get on a roster any more. For this week at least, I am dead wrong. It nearly tortures me to write all this but a big congrats to JF on a fresh start in LA, and one hell of a derby game.
