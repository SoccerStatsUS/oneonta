This story could be the biggest soccer news in America, maybe ever!

The Anschutz Corp. run by Phil Anschutz essentially bankrolled MLS during the very lean years, and is now set to sell off the entertainment portion of his company that contains his MLS stake.

What does this mean for MLS and American soccer?

The Hollywood Reporter broke the news on Tuesday night writing that Anschutz Corp is lining up to sell Anschutz Entertainment Group (AEG) which owns among other things the LA Galaxy and Home Depot Center.
