HOUSTON DYNAMO

So Houston gives up on it's top player Dwayne De Rosario! They sell him to Toronto (at an average price, not even big money) so he can go home and end his career there after a couple more seasons. OK, thats pretty cool of them. And they get a young center back with potential in Julius James. OK, but we have no idea if he is really any good or not. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~

The one thing is certain - it made Toronto better instantly.

~ ~ ~ ~ ~

But really, why oh why in the wide world of sports would you ship DDR off to Toronto for so little in return? Does Dom Kinnear know something we don't? Is DDR past his sell-by date? We all know he has lots of quality still in him. Or is this just the sacrifice you have to make sometimes to move forward. Get something for him while you can, and use the money to get even more. That is the only logic that makes sense to me.

My instincts tell me that this move to dump DDR will prove to be the right one this next season.

In Argentina they don't settle the winner with goal differential or any of that sissy stuff. They settle it on the field! So this 3 way tie leads us to the first ever 3 way playoff.

3 games will be played on Dec 17, 20 & 23, each team playing twice, with opponents to be determined for each date. Will they all be played at neutral sights?
