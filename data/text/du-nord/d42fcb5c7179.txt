Mexican Primera Clausura Playoffs

Torneo Clausura 2008 Liguilla is all set:

Top 2 from each of the 3 groups go through, then the next 4 best records regardless of group will playoff in the first round for 2 spots in the final 8. All games are home & away throughout the championship run.

-The 3 leaders are: Toluca, Santos Laguna & Guadalajara.

-2nd places belong to : Chiapas, Monterrey & Cruz Azul.

-And the next 4 are: San Luis, Necaxa, Pachuca & Atlas.
