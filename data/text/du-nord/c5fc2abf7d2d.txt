MY THOUGHTS

Wow did Houston keeper Tally Hall get burned badly for a goal or what? Colorado's Jaime Smith took a wicked free kick from about 40 yards out that blazed over Hall's head. You don't see a keeper misjudge a ball that badly very often. See it here.

Je-Vaugh Watson got his first start in Houston, and Spanish forward Koke came on late for his debut. Koke debuted last weekend. (Thanks to all who set me straight on that.)
