I love how he hid behind his role as guardian of fellow Caribbean nations only to bully them all into kowtowing to his every whim. Sure he helped them. Just enough to get them excited and stand in straight lines behind him. And for every dollar he acquired for them he took many more for himself. A classic dictator.

MY THOUGHTS

Some great goals again this past weekend:

Like this classic from New York's Thierry Henry. How many of these will be on his highlight reel?

Kevin Goldthwaite of Portland with an awesome behind the leg flick just like the one Chicharito had the same night. Again, awesome! (Or was it an own goal by Carlos Mendez?)

The Red Bulls go half the length of the field on three quick passes then Austin Da Luz makes a great move to finish.

Then we have the defensive blunder of the week as Michael Lahoud of Chivas forgets to play the ball and Jackson steals it to win the game for Dallas.

Juninho of LA jacks another long distance bomb of a goal, this time beating the keeper to the back post. He has had 3 of these this season.

Fredy Montero hits a brilliant free kick to win the game for Seattle, and he makes it look effortless.

But my favorite of the week comes from Vancouver where Davide Chiumiento makes a fantastic weaving run from midfield then feeds Alan Rochat who hits a curly rockets to the far post!
