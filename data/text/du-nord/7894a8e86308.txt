Why do people say Wade Barrett has picked Houston Dynamo back up after Owen Coyle was fired? I don't see it. The numbers are barely any better. Barely. Very barely.

Season total 33 points from 33 games

Under Owen Coyle - 11 points from 12 games (0.92 ppg)

Under Wade Barrett - 22 points from 21 games (1.05 ppg)

Conclusion: The team is fractionally better, approx 2.5 points better for the whole season under Barrett, which in my book is nothing. They need a whole new coaching staff, pronto.
