MINNESOTA STARS FC

NASL

Still unbeaten! The Stars went on the road for game 5 of a 7 game road trip and got another scoreless draw. AND they used a team of 90% reserves for the second week in a row to get the job done. At this point all of these guys look ready to step on the first team at 1 seconds notice too. Fantastic. Yes a goal and a win would be even better but given the circumstances we are in no position to complain as fans. On top of that this weeks opponent San Antonio and last weeks Tampa Bay both played their full starting 11. In case anyone was still wondering this Stars team has the ability to have a great season.
