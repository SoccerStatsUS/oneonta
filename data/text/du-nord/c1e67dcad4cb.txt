Does this one break new ground? Here is the semi-regular "I can fix the MLS playoffs" article. By Brian Straus of Sports Illustrated.

I prefer a single leg bracket with higher seeds hosting each game. IE: The top 4 (2 in East + 2 in West) get a buy in Rd 1 while teams 3-6 in East + West playoff for 4 spots again with the higher seed hosting. Then with 8 teams left we get E1 hosting the lowest E seed left etc. This rewards regular season records.
