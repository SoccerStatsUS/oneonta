QUOTE OF THE DAY

“I am done with Europe. I was fortunate enough to win everything on the national team and club level. I’m happy with that and I’m happy here to help my team win something. And I’ve been saying since I arrived here that it’s been more than amazing for me. We played in Seattle before 48,000 people. There are some amazing players in this league who maybe have a future playing in Europe. I’ve been surprised by the level of physicality and understanding of the players in the league.”

-Thierry Henry on his past, present and future.
