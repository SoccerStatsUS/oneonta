From the Puget Sound Business Journal: A group of Los Angeles-based investors that includes a French soccer star, a real estate developer and a business appraiser wants to bring a Major League Soccer team to Seattle to start play in 2010. The group -- Atletico Seattle Management Co. LLC -- also wants to build a 25,000-seat soccer stadium somewhere in a suburban city near Seattle. The stadium, which Atletico Seattle estimates would cost about $135 million, would be privately financed.

Celtic defender Mark Wilson talks about the teams recent preseason training in America: "Going to America definitely helped us because, against Parma on Sunday, I felt I could have run all day. When we were in Denver and Chicago, I couldn't run the length of the pitch. I was thinking at the time, it's crazy playing in these conditions. But now you can certainly see the benefits of working in that heat and at altitude. Training the night before the first game (at high altitude) against the MLS All-Stars left us all saying, 'Oh my God' . Then, when we played in Chicago on the Sunday, it was difficult because I hate playing in the heat. But, now we are back home, I feel great for it."
