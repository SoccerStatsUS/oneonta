My notes from the game:

-The team clearly made halftime adjustments and came out to win. They were good in the first half, but they were very good in the second half.

-The USA are in desperate need of a real left back. I am not confident in Heath Pearce or Jonathan Bornstein. Any thoughts?

-John Thorrington made a great run into the box and was hacked down but no penalty was given. Very poor call on the referees part.

-What minute did you have in the Pablo Mastroeni gets a Yellow Card pool?

-And note to John Harkes - the name is pronounced "MASS-tro-en-ee" not "MASS-tree-on-ee". He is not Brett Farve.

-Pablo's Yellow means he is out for the first game of the Hex.

-Where was Charlie Davies? He was the one guy I really wanted to see last night. And why can't ESPN put a list of the available subs on the screen? Is it that hard? Oh, and why no replays of the 3 or 4 off-side calls that went against the USA? We saw none.

-Really disappointing to see such a small crowd from Denver.

-Great to see Freddy Adu get his first Senior team goal. It was a very nice free-kick, not perfect, but very nice. I think a really good goalkeeper could have gotten to it.

-The Guatemala keeper had a pretty decent game, and single handedly kept his team from being embarrassed. The only other guy that showed much of anything was Pando Ramierez and that wasn't much.

-What was Seth Green doing at the game?

du Nord 3 Stars Of The Game

3. Ricardo Clark - He seemed to have that confidence to roam the field more knowing Mastroeni was behind him. Showed some great tackling skill to break up Guatemala's attack before it even got started. And then added to the offense with some real moves and decent passing. Impressive.

2. Kenny Cooper - Everyone finally got what they wanted. And when he scored the people I was with did the Frankencooper! (Arms straight in front of you, legs stiff, then stagger forward and beller like Dr Frankenstein's mute monster!) His goal was really great.

1. Freddy Adu - He was not pushed off the ball like we have seen so many teams do. He made some great passes. He turned up in many different spots on the field, and best of all, he really turned it up a notch in the second half. I like to see players who can adjust and improve during a game. And then he nailed that free kick. Great job Freddy. I love then teenagers (no, not like that).
