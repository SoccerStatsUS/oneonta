WHY?

So I was reading today that Colorado is going to host Seattle in a preseason game on March 19 as a charity fundraiser, and they are going to call it the Charity Shield. Why? That is the name of an English preseason game that pits the previous seasons Premier League champions against the FA Cup winners. We know that Seattle won the equivalent Open Cup last year, but what has Colorado won? Ever? Their lack of originality is killing me.
