WHY NOT!

My Thoughts on the current state of the USA mens national team:

Bob Bradley has instilled a certain mentality of playing the game within "the group", as he likes to say, and If find that the word "managing" is a good description of his style. Not as in "I am the manager." but rather in "We can keep a good grasp on the situation."

Meaning that the team on the field does what it has to do to try and win. But nowhere in the plans is there room to attack, breakdown and dominate another team from beginning till end. (I am not referring to attacking with the ball, or breaking down a defense with a nice pass, but in the bigger picture.)

Intimidation, positive attitude, confidence - all gone out the window.

The idea is to keep the game contained right in front of you. Simple boxes, easy movements, team effort and an honest days work. Managing.

"I thought we managed most situations during the game well. Others not so well."

Its also ego-less football.

Manage-the-game-from-within football.

React to whats-going-on-around-you football.

Take-what-the-other-team-gives-you football.

Painful-to-be-a-fan football.

Its getting old.

Like the air the next morning inside a previously smoke filled bar.

And I am one of the people who defend Bob Bradley.
