AMERICANS ABROAD

Christian Pulisic - Dortmund rolled over Benfica 4-0 in Champions League with Pulisic starting and right in the middle of all the action! He got the assist on 2 goals and scored the game and series winner! What a night for the 18 year old.

Paul Arriola - scored a huge goal in the Rd of 16 in Copa MX as they beat Club America 1-0. In the Quarterfinals they will host Morelia.

Michael Orozco and Joe Corona both played the full 90 in this game as well.

Jose Villafana started and went 90 for Santos Laguna as they won 1-3 at Necaxa. They will take on Cruz Azul next.

Jonathan Bornstein played the whole game but Queretaro was knocked out in Penalties.

Geoff Cameron - again went the full 90 in the center of midfield as the went to Manchester City with Stoke and got a 0-0 result. Looks like he is back to 100%, if not 100% fitness.
