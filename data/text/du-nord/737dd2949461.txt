US OPEN CUP

Great fun was had last night at the National Sports Center as my Minnesota Thunder beat Chicago Fire PDL 4-0 in the rain. For whatever reason I love rain games, and our team tends to play great in the rain as well. After a simply rotten start to the season the team has begun to get it together and won 3 in a row (2 Open Cup and 1 First Division). They host the Kansas City Wizards on June 30th in the 3rd round. Our boys went to KC in 2005 for a Quarterfinal match and came home with a 1-3 triumph of epic proportions. I have no doubt we will do it again.
