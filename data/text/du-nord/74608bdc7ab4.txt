TAYLOR TWELLMAN RETIRES

Due lingering damage from concussions, one of the leagues most prolific goal scorers has been forced to retire way too early. He is 30 years old.

Watch the press conference of Twellman's announcement.

Three writers know him as good as anyone:

-Frank Dell'Apa of the Boston Globe

-Kyle McCarthy of the Boston Herald

-Greg Lalas of MLSsoccer.com

Twellman impressively scored 29 goals for the 1860 Munich reserves, 101 goals for the New England Revolution, 6 goals for the USA, and 6 goals for USA youth teams.

In late July 2009 du Nord did a Q & A with Twellman. Conducted by Paul Demko.

Trivia: Twellman was born right here in my city, Minneapolis, while his father was a member of our Minnesota Kicks of the old NASL.
