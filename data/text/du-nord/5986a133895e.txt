US SOCCER

34 stadiums across the USA have been asked to submit a bid to host games at the 2026 World Cup.

US Bank Stadium in Minneapolis is on the list, but has very little chance of succeeding because the footprint of the floor of the stadium is not regulation size for FIFA. They would need an exemption to qualify. They would also need to bring in real grass as the stadium has fake turf.

More on the story with a list of all potential host stadiums. By Tom Marshall for ESPNFC.
