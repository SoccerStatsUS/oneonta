du Nord 3 Stars Of The Game

3. Tim Howard - He only needed to make one crucial save and he did. Very late in the game a corner kick was deflected by Bocanegra toward the bottom corner of the net but Tim-may got there first and smothered any chance for a rebound and tying goal.

2. Oguchi Onyewu - Has this guy returned to top form again? He is actually better than ever. When was the last time he got a card? His menacing defending has been huge two games in a row, and his passing out of the back was flawless. What a monster.

1. Clint Dempsey - He did exactly what we need him to do. Get the all important goal. He did not have the best looking game, and was subbed out. He even had another great chance earlier in the game that he shot very softly. But again, he did the job and got his team the win.

~ ~ ~ ~ ~

To counter balance these performances the USA had 3 players that were simply awful.

-DaMarcus Beasley

-Michael Bradley

-Maurice Edu

All three seemed to forget how to simply pass the ball. They gave away possession when they didn't need too. They attempted difficult/impossible passes when many simple options were available. Their pace was slow to plodding. And in the case of Beasley, he missed an open net, and botched 2 other great chances at goal to mercifully end the game. I would love to see all 3 on the bench come Wednesday night. Eddie Lewis, Sacha Kljestan and Ricardo Clark would make fine substitutions.
