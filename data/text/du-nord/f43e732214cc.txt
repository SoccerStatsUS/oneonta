Kansas City won its 2nd trophy in as many nights as FC Kansas City won the NWSL championship on Thursday night beating Seattle Reign 1-0 on a goal by Amy Rodriguez, set up by one of my faves Heather O'Reilly. Huge congrats to that excellent team on winning their 2nd consecutive league title. This team is stacked with superstars: Rodriguez, O'Reilly, Becky Sauerbrunn, Lauren Holiday, Yael Averbuch, Amy LePeilbet and Nicole Barnhart. Yeah, good luck beating them!

By the way, that Olympic qualifying team won their first game last night, 3-1 over Canada. Jordan Morris looked like a man among boys out there, scoring twice. The team as a whole was competent without being all that impressive, but in control for most of the game and only on the back foot for about 10 minutes in the 2nd half right after Canada scored on a set piece that should see the US coaching squad bring out the Iron Skillets for some head bashing in the next training session.
