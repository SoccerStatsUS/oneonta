QUESTION OF THE DAY

Why is it that two of the MLS teams with some of the lowest average attendances this season, Dallas at 13,241 (4th lowest) and Columbus 10,846 (1st lowest), are both owned by the Hunt family?

We all know about their late father Lamar Hunt's love of the game, and we are told the sons have the same passion. So why are they near the bottom this year, and consistently near or at the bottom over the past several years?

The league average is up over 1500 per game, or 9.3%. Meanwhile Columbus is down 25.9%.

On the better side Dallas is up 22.4%, but they only started at 10,815 from last season.
