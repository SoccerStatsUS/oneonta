On Wednesday August 12th of this year, the USA will play one of their biggest matches in several years - a World Cup qualifier at loathed rival Mexico, and their daunting 120,000 person capacity Estadio Azteca in the District Federal.

Because of the importance of this match, and the bigger than ever quantity of USA fans that will be traveling to that game, Adam Spangler of This Is American Soccer spearheaded the idea to create a USA traveling fan's guide to Mexico City.

My FIF (friend in futbol) Andy Wattenhofer contributed a great deal of information collected from his 2 previous trips to qualifiers at Azteca, making this article indispensable. Adam and I added the little things that we know, and we all agreed to get this out to you the fellow fanatico as quickly and efficiently as possible.

Enjoy Azteca In August
