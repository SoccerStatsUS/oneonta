The English Premier League is supposedly going to get serious about punishing players for abusing referees this season. By the Press Association via the Guardian.

I will believe it when I see it in action on a regular basis.

It took the NBA 2 years to really crack down against referee abuse when they instituted a similar policy. In the case of the NBA it has really worked. You just don't see the insane histrionics that used to go on happening at all any more, and the game is much better for it.
