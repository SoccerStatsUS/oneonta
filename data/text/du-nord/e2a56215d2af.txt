LOS LOONS

MNUFC was very busy in MLS draft day. See all the details below in Los Loons section.

Picks in MLS Superdraft:

5th Pick - traded this pick away to Chicago. MNUFC gets $75k GAM, $100k TAM and goalkeeper Matt Lampson

7th pick - acquired from Montreal in exchange for $150k TAM, and MN picks Mason Toye

15th pick - acquired in prior trade with Chicago - Wyatt Omsberg

23rd pick - acquired from Toronto for $50k TAM and 2nd Rd #5 pick - Carter Manley

28th Pick - traded to Toronto

41st Pick - pick Xavier Gomez

Rounds 3 + 4 will take place on Sun Jan 21 via conference call.

Mason Toye - 19s year old, 6'3", 180lbs, forward from South Orange NJ. From Indiana U. Generation Adidas contract that does not count against salary cap. He is considered a future project.

Wyatt Omsberg - 22s year old, 6'4", 180lbs, centerback from Scarborough ME. Dartmouth College. Said to be strong with feet and in air. This looks a solid gamble.

Matt Lampson - goalkeeper acquired from Chicago Fire - 28 year old, 6'3", from Cleveland Tennessee. Drafted by Columbus Crew, and played for Pittsburgh Riverhounds and Charlotte Independence. ARGH! Not someone you want to see on the team. If I had hair it would be on fire. Let Colonel Kurtz tell us what he thinks.

Carter Manley - 21 years old, 5'11", 170lbs, right fullback. From Elkridge MD. Duke University. Manly trained with Minnesota last summer. Great recall by Megan Ryan from the Star Trib.

Xavier Gomez - 21 years old, 5'10", 150lbs, midfielder. From Chicago and attended Univ of Nebraska-Omaha.

Words on these picks, plus details on the moves made today. By Megan Ryan for the Star Tribune.
