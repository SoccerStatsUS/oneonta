STARTERS

Rarely have I ever had more fun or better memories of going to a soccer game than in the pouring rain. I know that sounds idiotic. But I have found it to be completely true. Last night my NSC Minnesota Stars hosted Carolina in a game that was delayed 90 minutes by lightning, then went ahead in a drenching rain, and we won 1-0. I was thoroughly soaked, and I was wearing a raincoat and had an umbrella. Soaked! Another factor that makes these games so great is that only the real lunatics show up. The casuals stay far away. That leaves all of us to just egg each other on. Which of course leads to sheer insanity. Bombing down rain. Jumping up and down. Singing your lungs out. And then celebrating like madmen when that goal goes in. Nothing like it really.
