Also a silent auction will be held to raise money for a charity. The auction is always very cool, as players, the team and others donate items. In previous years you could get your hands on: practice uniforms, warm-up jackets, shoes, hats, guest appearance on broadcasts, signed photos etc. All income from the auction will be given to Ethan Zohn's Grassroot Soccer.
