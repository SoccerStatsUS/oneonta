STARTERS - BOB BRADLEY IS OUT

Bob Bradley was fired today as head coach of the USA national team. The federation has stated that they will have another announcement tomorrow. Don't know if that will be about a new coach, or an interim, or etc etc etc.

We here at du Nord are big fans of coach Bradley and want to thank him for all his hard work, and wish him all the best in his next endeavor. Rock on Bob.

The heavy rumors tonight say Jurgen Klinsman will be named the new head coach.

BRAZIL 2014 WORLD CUP

The qualifying draw will take place on Sat Jul 30 at 1:00pm CT streamed live on FIFA.com:

-Asia, CONCACAF, Oceanian and CONMEBOL all have a 1/2 place - draw will be made to determine which confeds will face off. (two legged play-off)

-CONCACAF: drawing for 2nd Round - five 1st Round winners (Belize, Dominican Republic, US Virgin Islands, St. Lucia and Bahamas) join the 19 next highest-ranked teams (FIFA rankings from March 2011) in 6 groups of 4 teams. Round robin home and away tournament. 6 winners advancing to Round Three. Then a drawing for 3rd Round - Pot 1: USA, Mexico & Honduras. Pot 2 Jamaica, Costa Rica & Cuba. Pot 3: 6 group winners. Drawn into 3 groups of 4. Again round robin home and away. Top 6 will move on to the Final Round, the Hexagon, with top 3 going to World Cup and 4th place going to playoff for final WC spot.
