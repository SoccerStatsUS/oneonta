Kicking + Screening (K+S) Soccer Film Festival, the world's premier international film event dedicated solely to the beautiful game, has announced the lineup of films to be screened at the 3rd annual New York City festival, July 20–23, 2011, at the famous TriBeCa Cinemas.

The lineup includes 11 films, boasting one world premiere and seven US premieres. All 11 films will make their New York debut.

Selected from more than 55 submissions from 28 countries, the films include Argentina Futbol Club, an inside look at the passion of Argentina’s biggest rivalry, between Boca Juniors and River Plate; Rise & Shine: The Jay DeMerit Story, which tells the remarkable story of defender Jay DeMerit’s rise from Wisconsin’s youth leagues to the World Cup; Soka Afrika, an unsentimental exposé of two young African players’ struggles to succeed in Europe; and Match 64, an intimate, behind-the-scenes portrait of the 2010 FIFA World Cup final.

Guest Speakers: New York Red Bulls technical director Erik Soler and ESPN soccer analyst Alexi Lalas will headline the 2011 speakers’ panel series. Also taking part in the panel discussions will be directors, producers, and writers from the majority of K+S’s official film selections.
