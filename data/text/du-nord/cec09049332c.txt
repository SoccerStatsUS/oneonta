STORY OF THE DAY

FIFA has given the thumbs up to the use of goal line technology to assist referees.

The International Football Association Board meeting at FIFA headquarters today approved 3 new upgrades for the sport - first the approval of the use of goal line technology, second the addition of 2 more referees, and third the allowance of players to wear head coverings for religious conditions.

IFAB has a great article on how such a goal line technology system will work and what steps it must be put through before it becomes official.

FIFA.com has a Q + A with their supreme ruler Sepp Blatter, on the matter of goal line technology.
