My game notes from USA v Mexico:

-I really hope Mexican left winger Andres Guardado is OK. He and Spector cracked heads, yet Mexico left him on the field. After the game he collapsed and was removed from the field with his neck in a brace. It was really scary looking. From what I have been able to gather, he was taken to the hospital but later released to travel with the team to Venezuela.

-Carlos Bocanegra captained the team again.

-It was cool to see Ricardo Clark come in after half time and have such a great positive influence on the game.

-Right before LD buried his PK the camera got a great shot of Mexikeeper Oswaldo Sanchez blowing kisses to Landy. LD had no reaction for the Mexican trash talker. Take that Oswaldo!

-The goal by Donovan was his 34th international goal. Tying him for the all time USA lead with Eric Wynalda. You can expect him to break that record this fall.

-After scoring the PK Donovan ran toward the corner flag and did an exaggerated swipe of the forehead with his arm to say "Whew, did we need that one or what!"

-After Donovan missed that sitter v Canada on Thurs Beasley made fun of him, only for Beasley to miss an even easier sitter in this game that could have sealed it. LD & DMB were in alone on Sanchez, LD drew out the keeper and passed to DMB who put his shot off the cross bar from 6 yards. He could have walked it into the open net. Damn. DMB said the ball bounced and hit him on the shin.

-That shot of Feilhaber's to win was 1000 X sweeter coming against the Mexicans.

-Of course the Mexican team swarmed the referee after the game complaining of everything under the sun. It's never their fault when they lose, always someone else's.

-Rumor has it that Carlos Salcido walked over to Landon Donovan after the game and asked to exchange shirts. Bravo!

-I met the excellent baseball writer for the Minneapolis Star Tribune, La Velle E. Neal III (I always thought that was a pretty kick ass name too) at the Sweetwater. What a passionate soccer dude. I had no idea he was a fan of the beautiful game.

-As the medals and trophies were being handed out post game we got to see the honorary Jack Warner on the podium helping with the ceremony's. He was seen pulling the medals out of a big box and handing them to his greasy buddy Sepp Blatter to drape around the players necks. As Jack pulled the medals out he was seen doing the old, "One for you. One for me" routine. Nice.
