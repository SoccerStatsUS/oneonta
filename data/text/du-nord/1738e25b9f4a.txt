Soccer In America

Tom Dunmore at Pitch Invasion with a very interesting article on a situation brewing with tickets and supporters sections who want to travel. Of course this a great new growing aspect of MLS. But while Toronto is going to be sending hundreds upon hundreds of fans to some away games, they will only sell 100 tickets to away teams supporters at BMO Field (I know they sell out every home game well in advance, but believe me, if they had 500 corporate schmucks wanting seats for ass-kissing they would get it done). And Toyota Park has decided to only sell 100 tickets to traveling Toronto fans. This problem will only grow, we all know that. So what is MLS going to do about it? So far they have chosen to ignore it. The old pretend-its-not-there-and-hope-it-goes-away trick. Just great. Slap a picture of El Battalion on the front of your website for sure, but help with tickets. Ah, no thanks.
