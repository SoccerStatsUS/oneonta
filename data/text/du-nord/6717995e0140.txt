USA CONCACAF WORLD CUP QUALIFYING

Rd 4 Game 1 Group C

-Fri Nov 13

USA 6-1 St Vincent + The Grenadines - USA goals by Jozy Altidore(2), Bobby Wood, Fabian Johnson, Geoff Cameron + Gyasi Zardes.

-In the bigger picture this game, beyond the 3 points, tells us nothing about where the USA team is at right now. Tuesday will tell us a whole lot more.

-USA tried to make it difficult for themselves by giving up a goal to St V in just the 5th minute. Oalex Anderson of Seattle Sounders 2 singlehandedly burned the right side of the defense badly (I'm looking at you DeAndre Yedlin, Gyasi Zardes and Geoff Cameron.) T+T wingers and strikers must be licking their chops in anticipation of Tuesday night.

-4 of the USA goals came on set pieces.

-Jozy Altidore now has 31 goals for the USA, just 3 behind Eric Wynalda for third on the all time list.

-USA players with good solid performances: Wood, Altidore, Johnson, Bradley, Ream, Besler.

-Matt Miazga + Darlington Nagbe made their debuts and are now cap tied to the USA.

-Jermaine Jones pulled up lame with more than 5 minutes left to play but the USA had already made their 3 subs so they chose to leave him on the field. It looked to be a hamstring injury.

-The USA was only whistled for 2 fouls in the game.

-Attendance was 43,433.

Rd 4 Game 2 Group C

-Tue Nov 17

Trinidad + Tobago v USA - 5:25pm at Hasely Crawford Stadium in Port-Of-Spain, Trinidad on beIN Sports

-T+T went to Guatemala for their first game and dominated the home team. 2nd half goals by Khaleem Hyland and Kenwyne Jones paved the way.

-This will be a very tough game for the USA on Tuesday, and I do not feel confident about a positive outcome.

-Tim Howard is expected to start in goal.

-Will there be other lineup changes for the USA? Kyle McCarthy of Fox examines the situation.

-T+T still has revenge on its mind for Tuesday stemming from the Nov 19, 1989 Paul Caligiuri goal that sent the USA to the World Cup and T+T packing. Still. From the T+T Guardian.

-T+T wants their fans to makes their presence felt in the stands. From the T+T Newsday.
