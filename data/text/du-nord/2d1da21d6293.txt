du Nord MLS Player Of The Week

John Thorrington - Chicago Fire

This award is not just for his great effort with a goal and an assist this weekend, but also for the 2 goals last week, and his big goal in the final game of last season that knocked LA out of the playoffs. Not to mention his a rise and fall and comeback from prodigy with Manchester United to injury bust with multiple teams around Europe and back to new rising star in MLS. Could the USA national team still be in his future? If he can stay healthy, I say why not! Fantastic for Thorrington!
