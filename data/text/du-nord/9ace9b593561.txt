Rayo OKC is circling the toilet bowl! In the past 2 days Spanish ownership has severed relations with American business partners in the club and then cleaned out some of the coaches and staff.

Head coach Alen Marcina resigned and has been replaced by Vallecano assistant Gerard Nus.

The club management is now engaged is massive cost cutting.

The mother club in Madrid was relegated from La Liga at the end of last season and is in an overall cost cutting mode.

Rumors of player revolt swirled, but as of now that has not happened. I am sure they want to keep their jobs.

The Scissortail Podcast Twitter feed has had the best info on the story.

OKC plays at the Cosmos on Sunday.
