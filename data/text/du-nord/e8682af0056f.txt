THE LATEST WORD ON USA V HONDURAS VIEWING IN THE TWIN CITIES

So if you haven't heard yet, we have a real mess on our hands for viewing this World Cup qualifying game on Sat Oct 10. The company who owns the rights for broadcast has chosen not to sell the viewing rights and is going to attempt to do a Closed Circuit Pay-Per-View situation in select bars only! Yes, you have read that correctly. So far I nothing at all about where we will be able to see this one in our area. But I will be doing full research to find us all the best viewing spot. I will be in touch with my favorite place The Sweetwater in St Paul and hope they will be coming through for us. I should know a lot more by the middle of next week, and of course I will let all you fine people know as soon as I find out anything. So sit tight for now and then wait for the magic to happen.
