Episode 6 of the du Nord Futbol Show podcast is now posted!

We had a blast doing this show!

On this show we record audio verite style on the road as we travel to watch the US Open Cup Final in Kansas City, and stop on the way home to see the USA Women in the Olympic Goal Medal game. Plus we answer lots of listener questions.

We also announce a special contest to give away an autographed Clint Dempsey jersey from Modelo beer.
