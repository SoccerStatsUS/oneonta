FACT OF THE DAY

Houston has gone 9 league games without a win. We hear a lot about the losses of Stuart Holden and Ricardo Clark as a reason for the drop in quality for this team, but I take it one step further back and look at the loss of Dwayne DeRosario as a much bigger factor. They have also never solved their lack of forward goal scoring even though they have had a revolving door of candidates on the roster.
