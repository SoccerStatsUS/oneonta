TEAM USA AT THE 2011 WOMEN'S WORLD CUP

WWC Germany 2011 has moved to the Semifinals.

The USA pulled off an incredibly dramatic comeback to beat Brazil and advance to the Semifinals against France on Wed Jul 13.

Brazil gave the USA an own goal in the 2nd minute as the USA plundered the goal. Then the USA hung on until a controversial foul led to a red card and penalty kick for Brazil. The first PK taker had her shot saved by Hope Solo, but the referee deemed that the USA had encroached on the shot. On the retake Brazil tied the game. The 90 minutes ended tied and the game went into Added Extra Time which saw Brazil dominate until they got the go ahead goal. Then they stopped playing. The USA poured it on to little success until the last of 3 minutes of stoppage time when Abby Wambaugh scored on a header off a desperation cross. In the Penalty Kick Shootout Brazil looked very nervous and on the third kick Hope Solo made the save that won the game for the USA.
