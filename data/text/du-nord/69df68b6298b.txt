MLS PLAYOFFS

Here is the format for the 2011 playoffs:

Ten of the 18 teams will make it this season.

Sadly we are still stuck with a home and away series in one round, while the other three rounds are single elimination - BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Either go all 2 legged series or none, make up your mind.

The league lays it out like this.

Let me try to explain it like this:

Top 3 in each conference, plus the next best 4 (Wild Card) qualify.

Wild Card Round - single elim

(top 6 teams get a week off)

1WC v 4WC

2WC v 3WC

Quarterfinals - 2 Legs

1 Overall v Lowest Wild Card winner

2 Overall v Other Wild Card winner

E2 v E3

W2 v W3

Semifinals - single elim

1/LWC winner v 2/3 winner from same conf

2/OWC winner v 2/3 winner from same conf

Final - single elim
