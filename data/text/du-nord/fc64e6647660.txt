CHARLIE DAVIES

Overshadowing the final game for all US fans is the tragic car accident Charlie Davies was involved in early Tuesday morning on the GW Memorial Parkway in DC.

The vehicle was torn in half, Ashley J. Roberta, 22 killed, Davies underwent 6 hours of surgery, and the driver, a yet unnamed person, was life flighted and is probably also suffering from major injuries.

For all fans of the US National Team, this is a huge tragedy and I imagine that most of you reacted similarly to me. There we were, working away when an email popped into our inbox announcing the news.

Our hands went to our head. Our mouths fell open. Our stomachs sank.

I know all US fans are wishing a speedy recovery to Davies and that he can find away to continue.

Charlie can recover, but the Ashley Roberta's life has ended, and that is the true tragedy.
