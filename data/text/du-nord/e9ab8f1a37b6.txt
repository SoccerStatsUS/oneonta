In MLS regular season attendance this season, the 2 bottom teams were of course Kansas City & San Jose who play in stadiums that have a capacity under 11,000. But to me the real news comes in the fact that two of the next three worst attended teams - Dallas & Columbus - are both owned by the Hunt family. A family that at one time was headed by Lamar Hunt who has been a patron saint to soccer in America for decades. But honestly, what does this say about the current state of the Hunt Sports Group run by his two sons? Yes they have done great things in the past for the game we all love. Now though? I don't like what I am seeing.
