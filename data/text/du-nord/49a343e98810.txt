The Mexican InterLiga tournament kicked off yesterday in Houston at Robertson Stadium. All 8 teams play over the next two days in a pair of double headers.

-You can see all the games on Fox Sports en Espanol.

-The tournament is split up into 2 groups of 4 teams who will play a round robin with top two teams advancing.

-Games:

Last nights scores - Group A:

Monterrey 0-2 UANL - Tigres got goals from Sindey Balderas & Walter Gaitan. Both in the second half.

Cruz Azul 3-2 Necaxa - First half Necaxa goals by Kleber & Fabiano, and an early second half goal by Osvaldo Lucas made it 3-0 before Salvador Carmona & Luis Orozco got two back for the Cement Mixers.

-Bernardo Fallas of the Houston Chronicle with his views on the two games last night.

-The Monterrey coach Miguel Herrera was looking pretty bad on the sidelines with his face all bandaged up. Turns out he was in an afternoon car accident and broke his nose. Damn.

Thursday - Group B:

Chiapas v Morelia

America v UAG

-The teams then move to Dallas's Pizza Hut Park for two more double headers on Sat & Sun, before moving to the final group stage games next Tue & Wed in LA at Home Depot Center.

-The top 2 teams from each group will play off at HDC for the title on Sun Jan 13.

-The winner gets a spot in the upcoming Copa Libertadores.
