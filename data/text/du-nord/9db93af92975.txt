The Guardian with a story on an arrest warrant being issued in Brazil for the agent of Carlos Tevez on money laundering charges. His name is Kai Joorcbchian, the same man who tried to buy West Ham last year, and is at the heart of the current Tevez contract mess. The money laundering is said to done through Brazilian futebol club Corinthians. To make it all even weirder, this scandal also includes the exiled Russian oligarch Boris Berezovsky, who may be the money behind Joorabchian. Berezovsky was also involved last year with the former Soviet spy that was assassinated in London with enriched plutonium. I love international intrigue.
