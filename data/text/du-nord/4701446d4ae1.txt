Copa Libertadores Finals:

The home team Nacional of Colombia scored early and then hung on to win their second mega trophy, the Libertadores Cup!!! Huge congrats to them and the fans in Medellin.

23 year old Colombian forward Miguel Borja only joined the team AFTER the Copa America, so he only played 4 games, but he scored 5 goals (of 6), including the Cup winner. Wow.

Tim Vickery of ESPNFC with the victory story.

Nacional played 14 games in the tournament: 10 wins, 3 ties, and only 1 loss. 25 scored and 6 allowed. Impressive.
