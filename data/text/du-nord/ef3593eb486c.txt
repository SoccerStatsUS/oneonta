US Soccer News

I heard from Glenn Davis down in Houston that San Jose midfielder Arturo Alvarez is appealing to FIFA to have his alliance to US Soccer wiped out, so he can join El Salvador. The rule is that if you play for a country on a senior team you cannot change. And if you play on a junior team you must apply to change before you turn 21. Alvarez is 23, and played for the USA U20 team. He has not played for the full team. We shall see. Either way, his offensive skills are good enough that he should get his chance with someone.
