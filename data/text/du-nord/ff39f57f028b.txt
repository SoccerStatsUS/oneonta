QUESTION OF THE DAY

Yesterday I asked the simple question - Why did the New York Red Bulls give up on right winger Dane Richards? And one of the best people in all of Metroland answered that question for us - Dan Ryazansky of Metrofanatic:

Richards is a free agent after the season who wants to go to Europe. Le Toux is a free agent as well. So, basically you're making a trade for the rest of the year. The team only has TWO strikers, Titi and Cooper. Richards is definitely not a striker, although has played there with limited success. Meanwhile, Solli has been freed from his right-back-by-necessity position by the emerging Barklage. So Solli (when healthy) is free to play in the midfield, especially at his preferred right midfield position. So either Solli replaces Richards on the right and Le Toux enters the forward rotation (remember, Titi misses a lot of games for various reasons), or Le Toux plays on the right and Solli becomes a midfield jack-of-all-trades. It also fits with the Cahill signing: they can now roll many parts in midfield and up front, adjusting due to injuries or other factors.
