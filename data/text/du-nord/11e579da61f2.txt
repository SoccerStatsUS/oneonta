If you are in the Twin Cities and free on Thursday night (Apr 23), then I think you should head to the St Anthony Main Cinema to see the film Rudo Y Cursi as part of the Twin Cities International Film Festival.

Gael Garcia Bernal & Diego Luna, the co-stars of Y Tu Mama Tambien, are back together again in a comedy of two brothers trying to become professional soccer players.

This is the debut film from director Carlos Cauron, who will be in attendance. He is the brother of acclaimed director Alfonso Cuaron (Children of Men, Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban, and Y Tu Mama Tambien.)
