OH YEAH COLORADO!

Huge congratulations goes out to the 2010 MLS Cup winners the Colorado Rapids.

The team was welcomed back to Denver with a real crowd honoring them. The pictures are exciting to look at. Could this be the beginning of something new? The Denver Post gives us an article, a video, and a photo slideshow.

A few things struck me as I watched the video of the teams homecoming in Denver:

-Jeff Larentowicz finally got an MLS Cup after all the losses in New England.

-Pablo Mastroeni got his title after so many years on crappy teams.

-Brian Mullan joined a very tiny list by winning his 5th MLS Cup title. 1 with Los Angeles Galaxy, 1 with San Jose Earthquakes, 2 with Houston Dynamo, and now 1 with his hometown Colorado Rapids! I may be wrong, but I think Jeff Agoss is the only other MLS player to achieve this amazing feat.

-The team had several players that grew up in Denver which is pretty cool

-The roster was full of guys I have never or rarely ever even heard of. They must have the most anonymous roster of all the teams in the league. Weird.

-Its only 2 days later but both Julian Baudet and Anthony Wallace have already been moved on to new teams. (See more details on those moves and many others below.)

And finally I wanna give a big shout-out to 2 friends-in-football who work for the winning team, and live & breathe soccer with the same passion that you and I have - Zach and German, Huge congrats to you guys!
