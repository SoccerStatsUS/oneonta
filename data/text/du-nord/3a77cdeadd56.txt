My notes from the New England v Atlante game:

-It was very sad to watch the losing team melt down at the end of the game, for the second night in a row. I was amazed at how little self control Atlante had. They ended up with 5 players Red Carded, and easily could have had more.

-Jay Heaps got a post game Red Card for New England. I didn't see what he did, but it must have occurred during the post game melt down. So New England will be without 2 starting defenders for the Final. (Michael Parkhurst is at the Olympics)

-Shalrie Joseph was incredible last night. He seemed to fill every passing lane, then put a great move or two on a defender and make a great pass that we all have come to expect from him. An excellent performance.
