"Holy Shit!" I got no less than three emails since last night that used that exact phrase. If you did not see the insane game from Azul Stadium in Mexico City pitting Cruz Azul against Real Salt Lake then do yourself a huuuuuuge favor and track it down. It is MUST SEE TO BELIEVE viewing!

-Real Salt Lake delivered one of the all time great collapses in modern American soccer history.

-There were 4 goal scored after the 87th minute! FOUR! Three of those by Cruz Azul.

-After RSL subbed off Espindola and Morales you could just see the team start to spin slowly and then build momentum as they headed for that drain pipe. They just fell apart.

-Javier Orozco was an amazing machine scoring 4 goals and Chano Gimenez did to RSL what he has done to Houston so many times.

-I have yet to mention the massive downpour that turned the field into the Boundray Waters Canoe Area.

-We also haven't gotten to the 2 huge referee mistakes in the first 5 minutes that contributed to the final. First RSL scored in the first 60 seconds but had the goal wrongly wiped out by an offside call. Then CA came back with a goal in the 5th minute that started with a free kick taken while the ball was still rolling, a no no. 2 rotten calls that were big in the end.
