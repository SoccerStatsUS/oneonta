The Italian TIM Cup Final:

Inter 3-1 Roma - With 4-2 aggregate win Inter claim their second straight TIM cup! Esteban Cambiasso, Julio Cruz & Obafemi Martins scored for the winners, and Shabani Nonda got a late one for the losers. Maybe the biggest news from the match was not the score though. It was the fact that Roma's Francesco Totti came on as a sub in the 54th minute.
