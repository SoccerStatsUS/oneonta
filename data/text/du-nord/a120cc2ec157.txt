The first of two Group Stages in CONCACAF World Cup qualifying ended tonight.

CONCACAF World Cup Qualifying Schedule

Matches & Scores:

-Wed Nov 19

El Salvador 1-3 Costa Rica - ES goal by Rudy Corrales. CR goals by Roy Myrie(2) & Freddy Fernandez.

Honduras 1-0 Mexico - Hon goal by Ricardo Osorio(OG).

Jamaica 3-0 Canada - Jam goals by Luke Shelton, Marlon King(PK) & Omar Cummings.

Suriname 1-1 Haiti - Sur goal by Wensley Christoph. Hai goal by Leonel Saint-Preux.

Trinidad & Tobago 3-0 Cuba - TNT goals by Kenwyne Jones, Dwight Yorke & Keon Daniel.

USA 2-0 Guatemala - USA goals by Kenny Cooper & Freddy Adu.

Mexico still managed to get through to the next round even (they Vikingsed their way in) though they had a total meltdown in Honduras. They not only gave up an own goal to lose 1-0,but they had Gerardo Torado & Carlos Vela Red Carded late in the match. Could not happen to a nicer bunch of sore losers.

Freddy Adu scored his first ever senior international goal!
