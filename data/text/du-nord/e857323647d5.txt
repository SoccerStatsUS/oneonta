-Sun Sep 25

My MLS Notes:

New York Red Bulls, Toronto, New York City all clinched a spot in the post-season. They join Dallas. 4 of 12 spots are now taken.

DC got a big win and took 6th place away from New England in the playoff race.

Somehow Columbus is back in long-shot-contention for the final playoff position in the East.

Ale Bedoya got his first ever goal for Philly. And the Union got a big road point in Toronto.

Seattle came from a goal down to wipe LA off the field in the 2nd half. Not even LD could save LA.

Jordon Morris showed off excessive pace for 1 goal against LA and infinite poise for the 2nd.

It was LA's first home loss of the season.

Colorado is the only team in the league without a home loss.

2 unsung forwards had big weekends for their team: Ola Kamara for Columbus and Patrick Mullins for DC.

The best individual performance of the week came from a player that I bet you have never even heard of: Mauro Manotas. Which team? Houston. What did he do? Hat trick! Dynamo beat the Timbers.
