Harvard rightfully suspended the men's soccer team for the remainder of the 2016 season following an investigation into a "game" the team has played for years where they rate the sexual quality (a “scouting report”) of all new Harvard women's team players, and put those ratings into a report that the whole men's team then received in an email. From the Harvard Crimson.

Unintentionally showing exactly what is wrong with the whole program, coach Pieter Lehrer released a statement that read in part... "the team is beyond disappointed that our season has ended in this way."

Recognize that his statement did NOT say he was "beyond disappointed" with the actions of his players that led to this. Now that is something to be "beyond disappointed" about.

There is nothing on Harvard's Athletic Department website, Facebook or Twitter addressing any of this. Sad.
