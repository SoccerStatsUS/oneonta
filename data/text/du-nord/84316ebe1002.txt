STORY OF THE DAY

Good News First:

MLS has announced that they will keep the same schedule format for next season, with everyone playing each other home and way once. Meaning that with 2 new teams joining next season the total number of games will increase by 4 to 34.

Bad News Next:

There will be no breaks in games for the CONCACAF Gold Cup, or any other FIFA international dates.

Details Last:

The 2011 regular season will kick off on Sat Mar 19 and conclude on Sat Oct 22.
