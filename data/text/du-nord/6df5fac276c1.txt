In this episode we sit down to interview one of the greats in the current American soccer scene - Peter Wilt. He was the original general manager of our beloved Minnesota team in the early 90's, then became the founding general manager and president of the Chicago Fire Soccer Club. We talk to Wilt about the past, present and future. Dig in.
