The beautiful and powerful Chicano Park is San Diego is in line to become a National Historic Landmark. From the San Diego Union Tribune.

I visited this park 2 years ago and the giant murals blew me away.

Here are many picture people have taken in the park.

There was a park in the city that was mainly used by Latinos and in the 60s the city decided to build a freeway right through it.

The citizen took it upon themselves to not let the park die or divide them.

They used the giant pillars that supported the freeway as their mural and told their stories in vibrant colors.

It is a beautiful place to visit. I highly recommend you check it out.

A huge thanks to Sasha who first took me there.
