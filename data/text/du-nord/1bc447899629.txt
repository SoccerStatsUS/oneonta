Kansas City has sold striker Dom Dwyer to Orlando for approximately $1.6M, a record amount of money within the league. By Sam McDowell for the Kansas City Star.

Seems like an odd move despite the big $. How are SKC going to score? Do they have a seriously big name striker in the pipeline?

Only 3 teams have scored less goals than SKC this season.

And while the SKC defense has allowed the fewest goals this season, they are only 6th in goal differential. It should be much higher.

From what I can tell SKC fans are really bummed out about this move. Really down in the dumps.
