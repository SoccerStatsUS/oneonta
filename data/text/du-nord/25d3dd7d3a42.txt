HOUSTON DYNAMO

Last week on his radio show, The Houston Dynamo All Access Show, Glenn Davis played a pre-recorded statement from Dwayne DeRosario thanking the team and fans in Houston for his great time there, now that he has been traded to Toronto. Listen to or download here. And while DDR sounds very sincere, I was left wondering why he didn't mention anything about WHY he left the team. We have heard speculation that he simply wanted to go home to Toronto to play. Fair enough. But I would like to hear him say it. And obviously he does not have a new contract in place at TFC seeing as they still have not made any introduction of their prized catch, and the word money keeps coming up as the reason why.

-On a connected note - I think Houston is going to miss DDR a lot more than I initially thought. Over the past couple of weeks I have just recalled so many time when he carried that team all by himself to victory. He is not replaceable.
