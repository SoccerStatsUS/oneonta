My thoughts on the USA U17 team:

Yes, our U17 boys played well in beating Brazil on Saturday, then proved they are still young boys by losing badly to Turkey on Sunday. Inconsistency is the key at this age, and its usually tough to achieve at 15 or 16 years old, much less 13. Their were several players who made themselves proud: Jared Watts, Perry Kitchen, Earl Edwards, Carlos Martinez, Joseph-Claude Gyua, Stefan Jerome, Marlon Duran & Charles Renken all showed stretches of high quality. Over all I don't have a lot to say about this team, other than they look quite good for their age.
