French coach Raymond Domenech on today's Euro 08 qualifier between his team and Italy in Paris: "I really hope the French public will respect the Italians and not whistle them during their national anthem," he said. "The Italians deserve to be applauded, at the beginning at least. After that, if we jeer our opponents during the match, that doesn't bother me."
