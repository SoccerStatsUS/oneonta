My notes from the game:

-Despite a decent performance against Argentina, our team has still not shown where any goals are going to come from against Barbados. That makes it three scoreless games in a row. While the players found themselves in decent, but not great goal scoring positions in all three games, there was rarely a shot taken that truly tested any of the keepers they faced. That bothers me. The good part is that Landon Donovan found himself in some OK spots last night, but his 3 weeks of rust caught up with him. Hopefully next weekend he can be more deadly, especially playing in his home stadium in LA.

-Pablo Mastroeni was shown his second yellow card, and ejected in the 71st minute after throwing away a ball after a free kick was awarded to Argentina.

-In the 84th minute Argentina's Javier Mascherano was shown his 2nd Yellow Card and ejected as well. He was on the verge of having 4 or 5 Yellows given to him throughout the game, but you could tell from his expression that he felt genuinely bad about the foul on Landon Donovan that got him tossed. It was a bad one. Luckily Donovan was uninjured.

-How about Eddie Johnson pulling a Vinny Jones on Mascherano!!!

-The down pour looked awesome! I love rain games!

-How did Kun Aguero not got a Penalty Kick when Tim Howard brought him down in the box in the second half. Howard would have even been Red Carded by some ref's.

-Gago and Gabriel Heinze both lef the elbows fly for most of the game and could consider themselves lucky not to suffer a worse fate than one Yellow apiece.
