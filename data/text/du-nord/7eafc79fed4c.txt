The Road To World Cup 2010 - South Africa News

BuaNews reports that South Africa will be using a special Event Visa for all foreign travelers to the World Cup. These will be handled different from regular Visa's and have special station set up to deal with those people in a more timely fashion. Some of the very biggest airports in the world will have SA customs agents that will clear people as they board their planes TO South Africa. Nicely done.
