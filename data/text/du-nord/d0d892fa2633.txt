My Minnesota United Notes:

A very good solid team win over OKC. The margin on the scoreboard was nervously small, but the margin of quality play was enormous. With the same effort on another night the score could have been 4-0.

Game highlights [VIDEO]

Great team goal for MN with Lance Laing, and Jamie Watson passing to Christian Ramirez to finish.

Both Laing and Watson had their best games of the season.

Loons switching things up formation wise with a 4-1-4-1.

Another shut out for the defense and keeper Sammy Ndjock. If you can't score you must stop the other team for finishing

Since being humiliated at home by Miami on Aug 6 they have only given up 2 goals in 6 games.

Maybe the only negative from the game for Minn was that Ibson did not have a particularly good night, or rather he was quite invisible.
