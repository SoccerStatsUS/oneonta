MLS Cup Playoffs

Both of last years finalists are OUT. LA and New England both went on the road and down to defeat.

Seattle and DC used great 2nd half performances to win their home games and advance.

-And they get to stay at home and host the 1st leg of the Quarterfinals on Sunday.

Jermaine Jones is the first big individual story of the playoffs. After a non-call on a possible handball in the box against DC late in the game Jones went Berzerker on the referee, screaming, pushing and grabbing at him, earning a red card and without a doubt a nice hefty suspension to start next season.

-NOTE: Jones' contract expires at the end of this season, so will he even step on a MLS field ever again? Damn. What an idiot. But somehow I will miss him if he goes.

The second big individual story was the total ineptitude of LA goalkeeper Donovan Ricketts. He played like Grandpa Simpson, bumbling his way to huge errors on all 3 Seattle goals. Ooooooof! Ricketts had help with the 3 blunders from Omar Gonzalez, Steven Gerrard and AJ DeLaGarza respectively.

We got to see brilliant goals from Juan Agudelo, Chris Rolfe and Erik Friberg. Watch em.

This song goes out to the game winning goal scorer Erik Friberg!

-Three blistering guitars and a lot of sunburn! Thank you Oakland.

-Sadly just 3 months after this recording several of these fuckin' rednecks would die in a plane crash.

The best keeper in MLS Bill Hamid saved DC from getting drowned early. It could have gotten ugly if not for some big saves. His coach Ben Olsen said it best: "Some teams have Giovincos. We’ve got a Bill."
