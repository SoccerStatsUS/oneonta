Now this is a scoreline even a hardass cynic like me can be satisfied with: USA 8-0 Barbados. Before the game kicked off, I loudly pronounced at the Sweetwater that our boys must win by at least 8-0 or I would be upset with them. Mission accomplished. Now they go to the island for leg 2 knowing only a 9-0 loss (or some equivalent there of) will knock them out. So let's see Coach Bradley give some other guys a chance to shine this time. How about an entirely different starting 11. There are a lot of guys us fans would love to see in a USA shirt, and this is the time to do it.

Turkey! Gobble! Turkey! Gobble! Turkey! Gobble! Turkey! Gobble! Turkey! Gobble! Holy smokes! What a come back. Wow. I am still stunned 18 hours later. Down 0-2 in the 72nd minute to a team with quality like the Czechs! And then to win it before were even get to stoppage time? Simply incredible.

My notes from the game:

-I find it very frustrating that the USA plays at home against a very weak opponent and they still line up with 2 defensive midfielders. We should be in an all out attack formation for a game like this.

-I also hate it when Landon Donovan is played out on the wing. Half of his game is taken away when he does not a have a free role to move around in the attack and become unpredictable. I really hate this about Bradley’s coaching.

-Pablo Mastroeni fired in a long distance shot that Brian Ching deflected in for the 3rd goal, but a few minutes later Mastro pulled up with a gluteus maximus injury and had to be subbed out. Freddy Adu replaced him in the 26th minute.

-The referee was none other than His Royal Highness El Vampiro - Marco Antonio Rodriguez of Mexico. He is a fairly lousy ref, but he sure looks cool!

-Tim Howard did not play after hurting his back in training on Friday.

CONCACAF News

Honduras became the first team to advance to the first group stage with a 2-2 draw in Puerto Rico giving them a 6-2 aggregate win. In the first leg games the USA, Jamaica & Guatemala dismantled their opponents by 8, 7 and 6 to nothing scores, while Mexico got a PK in the 90+ minute mark to seal a 0-2 win over little Belize. But the big news is Bermuda beating Trinidad & Tobago 2-1 in Port Au Prince, and Grenada holding Costa Rica 2-2. All other games went according to plan.
