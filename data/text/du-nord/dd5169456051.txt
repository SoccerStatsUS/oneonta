USA U17 TEAM

The USA wins the 2011 Nike International Friendlies tournament title.

A decisive win over Brazil on Sunday gave the USA the crown.

So, just how good are these kids? Remember they are just 15 & 16.

The most amazing part to me is their awareness of where they are on the field combined with their cool heads when finishing. I don't know how they stay so calm and focused on the task. Most veteran pros can't do that.

Watch these three goals:

-Wesley Wade and Junior Flores combine amazingly for a Flores goal. Cool Calm and Collected.

-Rubio Rubin with one of the most deft touches you will ever see. Eyes in the back of his head and super foot skills to match.

-Flores turns provider for Wade as they turn the Brazilians inside out. Flores burns em and the Wade uses magic feet to score.
