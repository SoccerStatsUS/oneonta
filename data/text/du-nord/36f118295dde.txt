USA U20 News

They have announced the dates for the CONCACAF U20 World Cup Qualifiers.

March 6-15 in Trinidad & Tobago.

There will be 2 groups of 4 teams, and the best 4 will go on to the World Cup.

6 teams either got a buy into this tournament or have already qualified. Host Trinidad & Tobago is in. Buys went to USA, Canada & Mexico. Already qualifed are Costa Rica & El Salvador. That leaves 2 spots to be determined with the Caribbean Champion advancing, and the Caribbean 2nd place team taking on Central America 3rd place team Honduras for the 8th and final spot.
