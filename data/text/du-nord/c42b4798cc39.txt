COPA LIBERTADORES

Round Of 16

Recap du Nord: The variety of countries (7) represented in the Quarterfinals is extraordinary. We are so used to Brazil and Argentina dominating that this is a refreshing surprise indeed. Yes Santos has the biggest name, but you would have a hard time calling anyone the clear favorite now. Of the teams left Junior and Libertad had the best records in the group stage.

Last night in the last games of the Round of 16 Velez Sarsfield coasted to an easy win, Chiapas Jaguares of Mexico surprisingly moved forward and Cerro Porteno needed Penalties to win after 2 scoreless legs.
