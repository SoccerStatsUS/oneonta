A very interesting looking film will be shown during the upcoming Tribeca Film Festival in New York City. The documentary titled, The Power of The Game, looks at soccer around the world and its social implications. A fuller description is available at the Tribeca Film Fest site. The other interesting note about this film is the director - Michael Apted. He has done all kinds of film projects, starting in British TV, and doing the documentary series 7 Up, 14 Up, 21 Up etc. He has also directed feature films like Coal Miner's Daughter, Gorky Park, Gorillas in the Mist, Nell, The World Is Not Enough & a ton more. He also did the official FIFA World Cup 2006 film, and is directing Goal 3 at this moment. Dave Brett Wasser at Historic Soccer Videotapes sent us this gem.

Don't know if you heard this initial story, but a couple months back former San Jose midfielder Ramiro Corrales was given the heave-ho by the immigration department in Norway where he had been playing the past several seasons. The story goes that his team Ham-Kam never filed his work permit renewal papers, and after he left them for another team, Brann, no one picked up the ball. So his permit expired and the government got serious. Hence he was officially deported. The good news is that the problem was fixed this week and after several months of red tape he can return to his club and play again. Hooray Akevitt. From Yanks Abroad.

UEFA has given Valencia's David Navarro a 7 month ban for punching Inter's Nicolas Burdisso after their Champions League match last week. I am not sure how this all works, but I would guess its 7 months no matter which competition Valencia play in. My only question then becomes, do the months of June, July & August count toward the ban seeing as the club is offseason during those months?

-Other players from both teams were also given shorter and more specific suspensions. The following bans are from UEFA club competitions: Burdisso - Inter - 6 games, Maicon - Inter - 6 games, Carlos Marchena - Valencia - 4 games, Ivan Cordoba - Inter - 3 games, and Julio Cruz - Inter 2 games.
