-Four big questions I have after watching the 3 games are these?

1. When you only bring 2 forwards to a tournament and you are desperate for goals, why does 1 of the strikers not see the field for even a minute? Why was he even there?

2. How bad does Brek Shea have to play before he gets subbed off? He constantly looked like a guy playing for the scouts in the stands. All flash and no substance. And to make matters worse, his flash was very poor. The touch of a pick-axe swinger, and the passing skills to match. At least he put in the time on getting his hair just the way he wanted it.

3. It looked as though over-confidence played a part in this third game loss.

4. Do we see any of these players attracting contract offers based on this tournament?
