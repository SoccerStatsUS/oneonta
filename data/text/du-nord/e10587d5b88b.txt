CONCACAF CHAOS

Bill Archer of Big Soccer tells us about Anton Sealey and Fred Lunn, the two representatives from the Bahamas federation who first brought up the whole mess of the bribes - "Not All Bahamians Are For Sale".

And if you want to show your support of Mr Anton Sealy of the Bahamas, you can do so at this email address. I think we should all tell him we have his back: info@bahamasfa.com

For the next three days FIFA investigators will be in Miami conducting interviews.

The Jamaica Observer reports that some federation have said off the record that they plan to boycott these interviews, and will hold their own meetings somewhere else.

And let's remind ourselves who the ring leader of the investigation is - Chuck Blatter - CONCACAF secratary general and FIFA executive committee member.

It should also be noted that during a 2007 lawsuit brought by credit card company VISA against FIFA the judge in the proceedings said the following about Blazer:

-Mr. Blazer’s testimony was generally without credibility based on his attitude and demeanor and on his evasive answers on cross-examination.

-Based on his evasive answers and his attitude and demeanor, Mr. Blazer’s testimony as to the March 14, 2006 Marketing & TV AG Board meeting is rejected as fabricated.

Not exactly hero material. But charm, he possess in spades.
