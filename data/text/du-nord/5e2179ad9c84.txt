Business Journal is reporting that Brener International Group LLC (a real estate & investment company) and Golden Boy Promotions Inc. (champion boxer Oscar de la Hoya's company), have bought 50% of the Houston Dynamo.

No price was given, but the article says the Dynamo are valued at $40-50 million.

At this time, current owners AEG will continue to run the team, and will also push forward on building a stadium.

Bernardo Fallas of the Houston Chronicle now has more. And says the sale is still being worked out.
