THIS IS WHAT ITS ALL ABOUT

Jay DeMerit of the Vancouver Whitecaps writing for Goal.com gives us his diary from the All Star game.

"We arrive at PPL Park, and there is already a great buzz. We get into the locker room and our jerseys are all laid out and hanging in our lockers. Taking that in was a great moment."
