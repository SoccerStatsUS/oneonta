MINNESOTA STARS FC

My beloved team the Minnesota Stars went to Des Moines on Tuesday night for the US Open Cup and beat the Menace 0-2. It was a good solid win for the team and fun road trip for the supporters that made the drive. Our reward was a Third Round game hosting Real Salt Lake! But as we arrived home in the early morning hours we found out that our team had sold the hosting rights to RSL, and would be going on the road for the next round, thus robbing the fans of a glorious night of soccer in Minnesota. The reason given by Stars management was a totally understandable financial consideration. (In case you don't know the team is owned and operated by the league as the Stars have no ownership group, and the league needs 8 teams to meet minimum US Soccer requirements.) So I guess we should just shut up and be happy we have team, a team that nobody wants, but who won the NASL championship last season. The fans are in deep pain today knowing that we will not get to host a great match up against a high quality MLS opponent. And I am not exaggerating when I say I get angry when I think about the circumstances for too long. As a fan I honestly don't worry about the team’s financial state, that is for the management to figure out, I only care about great games. And this was certainly going to be one. In this case management doesn't believe they can sell enough tickets in one week to pay for the expense of hosting this game and has done what they feel is best. So be it. It doesn't mean I have to like it. I could write a lot more on this topic but I am going to drop it and do my best to move on.
