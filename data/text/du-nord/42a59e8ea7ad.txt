Columbus Crew has changed its tune 100% and will offer FULL refunds (until Nov 3) on 2018 Season Tickets.

They had previously announced that they were looking to move the team to Austin TX for the 2019 season and would not be giving refunds to people who were angry at having already purchased 2018 season tickets.

Good move.
