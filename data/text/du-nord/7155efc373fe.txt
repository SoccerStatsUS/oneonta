EW: We don't have a team in Minnesota.

TS: They do have a team in Minnesota!

EW: Do "We" (meaning MLS) have a team in Minnesota?

TS: Not yet, but Shevchenko might start a team there.

EW: (Sarcastically) Now we're gonna have a team in Minnesota cuz Shevchenko's Wife is...

TS: Manny Lagos is gonna start it up there! Remember Manny Lagos?

EW: Sure.

TS: Isn't he from Minnesota?
