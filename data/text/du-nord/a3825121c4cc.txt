USMNT U20s at the CONCACAF Championship and U20 World Cup qualifying

A very big and surprising 1-0 win for the USMNT over Mexico. First time they have beat El Tri in 31 years at this age level.

Brief game highlights [VIDEO]

And it wasn't a fluke. The USMNT comprehensively out played Mexico, and got stronger as the game wore on.

The USMNT high press was a thing of beauty. They kept Mexico a step off and slightly rattled.

Then the USA got their goal on a set piece by Erik Palmer-Brown from Sporting Kansas City.

Tyler Adams, a central midfielder from New York Red Bulls, was terrific all game long. He destroyed the Mexican midfield and started the attack. Great game.

Erik Williamson, from Univ Maryland, playing alongside Adams, was also very strong.

This leaves the USMNT on the verge of qualifying for the World Cup. What they need next will hinge on the outcome of El Sav v Mex on Wednesday.
