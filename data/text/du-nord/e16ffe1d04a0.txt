As much as I love Manchester United fans passion for their team, and dedication to demand the best, sometimes I wish they would shut up about the Glazer family who own the club. Don't get me wrong, I don't have a secret boner for old Malcolm, but these fans go off on everything before they even wait to see what will happen. A classic example is when the teams shirt sponsor Vodaphone decided not to renew their deal for 4 years and 40 million pounds. Critics went insane declaring the end of the team as we know it. Turns out Vodaphone did not renew their deal cuz the price was not 40 million. It was more. AIG paid 56 million for 4 years. I wish some of these people would shut up once in a while and wait to see what really happens instead of wild guessing (and acting like the guessing is based on something factual.) The BBC has a report on the current state of the club. Despite the fact that they have a huge debt to pay off (a brutally bad idea in my book), the future of the team still looks pretty rosy to me.
