Episode 3 of the du Nord Futbol Show podcast is now posted!

dN

On this show we discuss the re-introduction of the New York Cosmos, and what that means for the future of the NASL and our beloved Minnesota Star FC. We also talk about all the roster moves Vancouver has executed in the past few days, Michael Bradley's transfer to Roma, the summer friendly season, and many more MLS and USA related topics. We also tell more great rock n roll stories, of course.

dN

Your hosts Bruce McGuire and Wes Burdine hope you enjoy it, and look forward to your questions and comments.
