PRESS RELEASE:

CONNECT WITH THE STARS - CHAMPION MINNESOTA STARS FC SOCCER TEAM STARTS SEASON WITH SHINE ON EVENT

Thursday, April 5, 2012

Minneapolis, MN (March 28, 2012) - Join Minnesota Stars FC, the 2011 North American Soccer League Champions, as they start their third season with a reception featuring a screening of a behind-the-scenes documentary and a fashion show debuting their 2012 championship jersey. A very limited number of tickets are available to the public.

To kick off the 2012 season, Minnesota Stars FC will host a reception for potential sponsors and owners, as well as season ticketholders who purchased by October 1, at Brit's Pub in Minneapolis, Thursday April 5, where they will screen the first 2012 episode of the Shine On documentary about the team. Created by local filmmakers Brave New Media, Shine On chronicles the challenges faced by the local professional soccer team during the 2011 season, including their ongoing quest to find a local owner. The first two episodes of the Shine On series can be found at the Stars YouTube page.

After the screening, the Stars will debut their new 2012 Championship jerseys, designed by player-coach Kevin Freidland and sponsored by Admiral Soccer. Guests will have a chance to meet the players, learn more about the team, purchase jerseys, and purchase tickets to the Minnesota Stars FC home opener in the Metrodome on Saturday, April 7, at 7:30 p.m.

A very limited number of tickets are available to the public starting today for $10 and include appetizers. Tickets can be purchased here.
