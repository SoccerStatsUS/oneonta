dN

"the play-in concept for MLS teams stinks."

-Portland Timbers owner @MerrittPaulson

I could not agree more.

The whole tournament needs a house cleaning.

First off we can all agree that it should be a lot more popular. And that takes times money and effort. Real effort. Not just talk about making it more popular.

Then I think these ideas should be implemented immediately:

-All MLS teams in the USA should be entered in.

-All NASL teams in the USA should be entered in.

-All USL-Pro teams in the USA should be entered in.

-Play-in for entrance by PDL teams.

-Play-in for entrance by all amateur teams.

-There should be NO bidding for home field, it should be a blind draw.

And finally, lets get some more games beyond the last round or two on TV. And if so many teams are already streaming their games online why can't there be a hub to find all of them, why do we have to play Sherlock Holmes to figure out where to watch something.
