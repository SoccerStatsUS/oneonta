A couple of big surprises selections: Veteran goalkeeper David Yelldell of Duisburg in Germany's 2.Bundesliga get his first ever call from the USA, as does 20 year old wide midfielder Timothy Chandler of Bundesliga team Nurnberg. We also have centerback Zak Whitbread of Norwich in England's Championship division who has played on USA youth teams but never the senior team, and a return after a fair amount of time away from the team for Sacha Kljestan. Youngsters Mix Diskeruud, Tim Ream and Juan Agudelo also get the call.

I am curious to see where Chandler might play on the field with this team as they have him listed as a defender (I assume fullback), but with his club team he has been used as a wide attacking midfield/forward player.

Finally, both DeMerit and Agudelo have been struggling with slight injuries at their club teams. So it will be interesting to see if they play in MLS games this weekend.
