STARTERS

The Associated Press with an article on German goalkeeper Robert Enke who took his own life yesterday. He had been suffering badly from depression, and left behind a suicide note. The article has quotes from Enke's wife, his doctor, fellow players & coaches and the German chancellor. This is a horribly sad story.

-Not surprisingly Germany has canceled their friendly with Chile on Saturday.

-Depression is a brutal disease and is not to be taken lightly, no matter how stigmatized it is.
