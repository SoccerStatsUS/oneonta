FIFA is seeking to suspend CONCACAF exec Gordon Derrick for 4 years. He is currently the head of the Caribbean Football Union, a job previously held by Jack Warner, and he is also the General Secretary of the Antigua and Barbuda Football Association. He has been charged with "Conflicts of interest, offering and accepting gifts and other benefits, mismanagement of funds, abuse of position, and disloyalty."
