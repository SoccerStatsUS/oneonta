THATS WHAT FRIENDS ARE FOR

The New England Revolution management team invited supporters to a summit last night to discuss "inappropriate language" in the stands and who the team has dealt with it. Fans feel they have been mistreated by stadium security.

Plenty of reports on the meeting from the fans point of view:

-Andrew Donahue for The Bent Musket

-Brian for The Drug Is Football

-Nathan Gower posted unofficial minutes from the meeting on Big Soccer.

And then we get an article by Kyle McCarthy posted at MLSsoccer.com. (This is the most official article we have seen yet.)

One of the funniest things I read in all of this is the fans section is referred to as The Fort, while the people who occupy The Fort refer to the rest of the stadium as The Morgue! Genius.

Again the team has to this day still never used their own website, blog or Twitter feed to alert the majority of fans to any of this. They almost appear to be trying to keep it well hidden.

One forum the fans have taken to is the team's Facebook page, and boy oh boy are they letting the management have it. Like this simple note to the team president:

-"Dear Brian Biello: Please Resign. Signed, a soccer fan who cares about his fellow supporters. - Brian Barrish"

And now on the heals of all that we get this mysterious string of Twitters that started yesterday and have lead to this press release: Fort Active Supporters Trust Founded - Begins Initiative to Bring New England Revolution Back To Boston Under Fan Based Ownership

-Hmmmmm. I didn't know the team was ever in Boston and then left.
