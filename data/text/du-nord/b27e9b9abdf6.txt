STARTERS

Everyone needs to settle down a bit over the exclusion of Charlie Davies to the USA World Cup team.

So many in the American soccer world seem to be furious with coach Bob Bradley for not picking him. That anger is based on ignorance. Davies was very simply not given medical clearance by his club team Sochaux, and there is very little the USA can do about that. End of story.

If you want to be mad at someone, be mad at Davies for going out partying instead of being at the team hotel like he was supposed to be.

I am beyond sick and tired of the Bob Bradley bashing.

Now move on.
