RED BULL NEW YORK

Goalkeeper Jon Conway and centerback Jeff Parke have each been handed 10 game suspension by MLS for using a performance enhancing supplements known as "androstatriendione (ATD) and boldenone metabolites". They were also fined $11,500 and $5800 respectively. Both players claimed innocence, but ignorance is another word that could be used in this case. Word has it that the league conducted a very thorough investigation over 2 months time.
