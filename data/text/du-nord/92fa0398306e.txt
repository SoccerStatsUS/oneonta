USA WORLD CUP '98 SCANDAL

Well, its taken long enough but the story of the inside scandal with the USA 98 World Cup team has finally been told - and the tale is exactly what the speculators always thought it was.

Team Captain John Harkes had an affair with teammate Eric Wynalda's wife in the months leading up to the tournament, and coach Steve Sampson decided to remove Harkes from the team because of it. The rest is history as the team went on to a terrible performance in France. But, according to Sampson, this was not the first strike against Harkes, it was the last.

Monday night Wynalda told the basics of the story on his Fox Football Fone-in show. Which led to coach Sampson breaking his silence of all these years and talking to Ridge Mahoney of Soccer America, and then Wynalda (and more Sampson) talking to Ronald Blum of the Associated Press to fill out his side. Blum also got 2 sentences from Harkes, who has always denied the affair.
