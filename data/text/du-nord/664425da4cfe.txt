MINNESOTA UNITED

A very important run of 4 home games will be crucial to the rest of the season. By Megan Ryan for the Star Tribune.

Houston is DESPERATE for a road win. Like MN they have none this season. Tonight is huge for them too, and they have serious playoff aspirations.

But they are missing four key attackers, Erick Torres with Mexico, and Romell Quioto, Alberth Elis + Boniek Garcia who are with Honduras, at the Gold Cup.

MNUFC could/will be missing tonight:

At Gold Cup - Calvo, JVenegas, Taylor

Potentially injured - Schuller, KVenegas, Danladi, Kallman, Molino

Out injured - Burch, Anor, De Villardi
