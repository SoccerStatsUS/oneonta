But there is another side to all this 20 Years celebration!

Last week in DC a long time United supporter was banned from the stadium for 1 year after igniting a smoke flair in the tunnel outside the stadium when leading the most hardcore of supporters into the stands. I have no idea if he had been warned before or this topic was a serious issue with the team, but a 1 year ban is pretty significant. You won't be surprised to find out that many fans are quite upset by all of this.

Here is an open letter to the club that was posted on Facebook today by a member of the supporters group District Ultras, the group whose member was punished.

The biggest point I take from all of this is that week-in and week-out we see MLS and many of the teams using images of supporters shrouded in smoke in their promotion of the games - and here is a guy getting a 1 year ban from all games for that exact thing, and it wasn't even done in the stadium!

My favorite line from the whole letter is this one: "Can you embrace us for who we are? Because we embrace you, good times and bad." Oh yeah.

Here is an earlier story from the Black And Red United blog with some more info on what actually went down from the club and supporters perspectives. There are still a lot of unanswered question here though.
