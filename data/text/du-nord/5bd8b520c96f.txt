I was at a party in 1984. The stereo was blasting Footloose, U2, Springsteen, Huey Lewis or some such shit. My friend Nick, who was a huge fan of The Fall, marched across the room, stopped the music, pulled a cassette out of his pocket, jammed it into the stereo, hit play and turned it all the way up. It was Repetition by The Fall. Full blast. People were terrified. Before that day I thought The Fall were awful. After that day I knew they were brilliant.
