SAN JOSE EARTHQUAKES

The Equakes laid out a classic beatdown last weekend to a team (New England) that should have done that to them. It was an amazing match to watch (in HD on HDNet no less) if you were Pro-SJE or Anti-NER! It's taken very little time for that big goofy general manager John Doyle and coach Frank Yallop to build a very solid team. Most of us knew they did it right on defense from day 1, something the 3 previous expansion team, Chivas USA, Real Salt Lake & Toronto FC botched badly. But this recent transfer period saw the team go out and add serious offense to this team. Could Doyle be a front runner for team executive of the year? Absolutely. Again, amazing. The beatdown they put on New England, 4-0, could be chalked up to several players being out due to suspension, Olympics and injury, but those are simply excuses, and I don't rate excuses highly. And New England are good. Give it a good look - Ronnie O'Brien, Francisco Lima, Scott Sealy, Darren Huckleberry, and Ryan Johnson are really making things happen. Let's see em do it on the road now too. Does anyone think the playoffs are a possibility. I honestly don't, but on the other hand with what we have seen the past few weeks you certainly could not be surprised if it happened. (Thanks to Steven for inspiring these few words.)
