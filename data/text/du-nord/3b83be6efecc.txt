MLS/SUM BUSINESS

3 highlights from MLS commissioner Donald B. Garber's internet chat with fans at USA Today:

-After they announce team #20 (likely to begin play in 2013), they will next look at Atlanta, San Antonio, San Diego, Detroit and Miami. Sounds like a 2nd New York team as #20 is a done deal to me.

-With the addition of 2 more teams next year the league will keep the conference set up but move 2 teams from the West to East. That doesn't seem to make sense to me because that would put 10 in the East and only 8 in the West. Anyway, who are the potential teams that would move? Houston and Dallas? They make the most geographical sense.

-MLS owners are working on having a reserve league or some other plan to continue the growth of young player development.
