2011 CONCACAF GOLD CUP

The Elimidate Round Robin play is now complete in all three Groups of Copa Oro, and surprising to some, the United States made it to the Quarterfinal round! OMG! Seriously, many folks felt that Coach Bob Bradley's job was on the line if he didn't get a result last night in Kansas City against "so hip they aren't ranked by FIFA" (see pic above) - Guadalupe from the Windward islands. Fortunately, U.S. Forward Jozy Altidore likes Bob Bradley and scored a scorcher from 25 yards or so in the 9th minute. The U.S. held on for the victory, despite 725 on-goal chances from Clint Dempsey. Clint must not like Coach B too much. Clint was seemed devoid of much emotion last night, so maybe his belly was still full of Gates BBQ Chicken.
