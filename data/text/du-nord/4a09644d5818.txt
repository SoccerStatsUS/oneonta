My notes on last nights 2 games:

-Luciano Emilio did not have a good game for DC. His touch was bad, his shots weak, and his passing dreadful. But he never gave up and we all know what he can do.

-The DC back line looked pretty solid for a first game. The 2 Gonzalos in the middle are bruisers and Burch seems to picking up right where he left off.

-DC looked really out of shape though. They got so tired that they could not even jump on those last few free/corner kicks and it cost them a goal.

-New DC #10 Gallardo looks like he has some skill left in his old body. But you gotta hope he holds up all season long. (See Jaime Moreno who tweaked a hamstring 15 minutes in)

-That stadium in Jamaica looked very very small.

-Houston proves again that they have what it takes. Short on players? No problem. They didn't score, but they didn't give up a goal either.

-But Houston definitely needs more strikers. Badly. Chris Wondolowski is not gonna get it done.

-Speaking of goals - if Municipal could finish they could be a fantastic team. But they are like the Colorado Rapids in front of goal. High & Wide.

-Was it just me or did former LA midfielder Pando Ramirez of Municipal look like he wanted some revenge on MLS? He tried to kill a couple people with his wild tackles and cannon shots.

-I enjoyed watching both Harbour View and Municipal. They have some real skills and seem to have a lot of fun playing.

World Cup 2010 - South Africa News

The Confederation Cup is a warm-up tournament sponsored by FIFA that takes place the summer before each World Cup in the host country, ie. South Africa. They use it as a test run of sorts. But it has become more than that when you see the prize money being handed out - Winner: $3.75 Million, 2nd Place: $3.25 M, 3rd Place: $2.75 M, 4th Place: $2.25 M, and the other 4 teams get $1.4 M each. The tournament is set for June 14-28 09.
