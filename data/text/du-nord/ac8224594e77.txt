The great soccer blog Sideline Views is no more. It was one of the few blogs that I made sure I read EVERY DAY. Andrea Canales along with Luis Bueno had such great insight on the LA scene, and soccer in America and Mexico over all. I loved it. I am really bummed. But happy for them that other things are making this blog unmanageable. Andrea is now an editor at Goal.com. I know she will bring great stuff to them. Adios muchachos. See you soon, where ever you are.
