CONCACAF Champions League

A loyal reader named Jared sent in the list of which teams from the USA qualify for the 2009-10 CCL.

-Four spots are up for grabs:

USA1: MLS Cup Champion (Columbus or New York)

USA2: MLS Supporters Shield Winner (Columbus, but goes to runner up Houston)

USA3: MLS Cup Runner-up (Columbus or New York)

USA4: US Open Cup Champion (DC)

-With Columbus claiming spot #2 and then getting either #1 or #3, they are moved out of spot #2 and the team with the second best regular season record gets that spot - which is Houston.

-Columbus & New York get the automatic bids into the group stage, while DC & Houston will have to play a qualifying round.
