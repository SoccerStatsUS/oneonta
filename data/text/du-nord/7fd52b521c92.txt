The Glockenspiel in St Paul is closing its doors. Damn.

-I have some amazing memories watching Germany games in that place, but these two are my fondest:

1. Germany destroys Brazil 7-1 in Semifinals at World Cup 2014 - the place was stunned at first, then exploded like nothing I have ever seen before, and then partied for hours afterward! Pure joy!

2. Germany crushes England 4-1 in Round of 16 at World Cup 2010 - one guy in particular got more and more insane as each goal went in for Germany. At one point he was literally on his knees banging both hands on the seat of his chair as hard as he could, screaming "Ja! Ja! Ja! Ja! Ja! Ja!" Wow.
