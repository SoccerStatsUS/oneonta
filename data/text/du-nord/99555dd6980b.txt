MATCH FIXING IN ITALY

Verdicts were handed out today and Serie A club Atalanta (just promoted) has been docked 6 points to start the season

-Goalkeeper Marco Paoloni has been banned for 5 years

-Team captain Cristiano Doni has been given a 3 1/2 years suspension.

-Thomas Mandredini formerly of Atalanta has been given a 3 year suspension.

I must admit that Italian justice has always baffled me. If Atalanta used match fixing to get promoted then why would there punishment be anything less than instant relegation?

The biggest name in the scandal was former Lazio & Bologna great Beppe Signori who has been banned from all football for 5 years.

Many more teams, players and officials from lower divisions have been punished as well.
