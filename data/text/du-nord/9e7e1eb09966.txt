ENDERS

Some of the greatest music of all time:

Augustus Pablo

King Tubbys Meets Rockers Uptown

Listen to it at Spotify right now

I think its one of a small handful of the greatest albums of all time. I am blown away every time I listen to it. Its far from a conventional sounding album. Its almost all instrumental, in a style called Dub. Recorded and released in Jamaica in 1976.

The album cover has Augustus standing in front of a collage featuring Haile Selassie's picture over his left shoulder.

Selassie the Emperor of Ethiopia from 1930 to 1974 is also known as the Rastafarian messiah, whose most famous follower was Bob Marley.
