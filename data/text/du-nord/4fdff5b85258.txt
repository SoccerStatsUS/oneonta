Clarence Rambharat of the Trinidad Express takes a deeper look at Jack Warner's infrastructure in FIFA, CONCACAF and T&T government. He has built a fortress.

Simon Evans of Reuters says Caribbean Football Union members are unhappy with FIFA's investigation in bribery allegations against Jack Warner and Mohamed Bin Hammam.

An unnamed CFU official:

"The investigation is tainted and biased and clearly has a U.S. driven agenda. Two America complainants, an American investigator, an investigation and an interrogation being conducted on American soil while FIFA remains a Swiss registered entity and none of the persons under investigation being U.S. citizens."

Evans also reports that most of the CFU members have decided that they will not go to Miami for questioning on the allegations.
