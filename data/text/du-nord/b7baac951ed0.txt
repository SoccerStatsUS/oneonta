UEFA CHAMPIONS LEAGUE

Semifinals

Barcelona has advanced to the championship game for the 3rd time in 6 years. And they will be going for their 4th crown.

To me there was only one story of the Barca-Madrid series - Where in the world was Cristiano Ronaldo? 180 minutes in the Champions League Semifinals and we barely saw the highest paid highest transfer fee player (by a mile) in the world. I would be pissed off if I was a Real Madrid fan.

Rob Hughes of the New York Times with a review of Barca v Madrid.

It was kind of amazing to see Eric Abidal take the field for Barcelona only about 5 weeks since he had surgery to remove a tumor from his liver. Amazing.

And now we know they will face Manchester United again for the title. Will Man U show up this time? They sure didn't two years ago. One of these two teams will win their 4th European Championship.
