The draw for the group stage of UEFA Euro 2012 tournament, hosted by Poland and Ukraine, was held today and here are the groups:

GROUP A

1 Poland

2 Greece

3 Russia

4 Czech Republic

GROUP B

1 Netherlands

2 Denmark

3 Germany

4 Portugal

GROUP C

1 Spain

2 Italy

3 Ireland

4 Croatia

GROUP D

1 Ukraine

2 Sweden

3 France

4 England

The tournament kicks off on Fri Jun 8 at National Stadium Warsaw in Warsaw, Poland.

The final will be played on Sun Jul 1 at Olympic Stadium in Kyiv, Ukraine.
