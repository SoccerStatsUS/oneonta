PRESS RELEASE:

Kicking & Screening rolling into Houston for one-day event, July 25!

Texas may not be the first place that comes to mind when thinking about soccer, but it's a mistake to think that the state isn't mad for the beautiful game!

Which explains why the Kicking & Screening Soccer Film Festival is headed down there for a special one-day event in Houston. K&S/Houston is part of the festivities around the MLS All-Star Game, featuring legendary English club Manchester United.

The festival is organized in cooperation with two-time MLS Cup champions the Houston Dynamo. A portion of the proceeds will go to K&S's 2010 charity, Play 31, and Dynamo striker Brian Ching's charitable effort, The House that Ching Built.

The schedule is as follow, but for more information and press inquiries, please contact me at 914.671.0213 or via e-mail.

The Ref: 11:00 am

Sixty-Six: 12:30 pm

The Last Yugoslavian Football Team and Ana's Playground: 2:25 pm

Rudo y Cursi: 4:35 pm

Argentina Futbol Club: 6:55 pm

The Damned United: 9:00 pm

Tickets cost $10 each and are available now. Or get a full-day pass for $25. Tickets available here.

All films are screening at the Angelika Film Center, 500 Texas Street, Houston.
