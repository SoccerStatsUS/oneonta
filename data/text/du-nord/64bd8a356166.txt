Roberto Gotta

Guerin Sportivo & ESPN Soccernet correspondent

Who is your favorite national team?

None really. Back in the Eighties, too many players I disliked put me off for ever supporting Italy. Enjoyed being in Berlin four years ago because of the experience and I was happy for some of the players, though

If your team gets knocked out, who do you follow then?

I always want England to do well; the US, Germany and Mexico, too. So I will be pretty neutral on June 12.

Who is in the final and who is going to win?

Without looking at how the pairings might be, I believe Brazil are going to beat Spain in the final, but I also think reliable predictions can only be made after the group phase. Getting through to the knockout phase is no indicator of a side's chances of winning it all.

Who will be the surprise breakout players of the tournament?

Marek Hamsik; James Milner; Pablo Aguilar; Mesut Ozil; Alexis Sanchez

How will the USA do?

Reach quarterfinals if central defenders play reasonably well and someone supports Altidore up front

Which team do you love to see get beat?

I do not like to say this, but... Argentina

Kartik Krishnaiyer

NASL Director of Communications and Public Relations

Who is your favorite national team?

USA

If your team gets knocked out, who do you follow then?

Nobody in particular except for England's opposition. I'll just hope for some good, attractive football, and England's elimination.

Who is in the final and who is going to win?

Brazil vs Germany with Brazil winning.

Who will be the surprise breakout players of the tournament?

Gonzalo Higuan, Gio Dos Santos, Meszut Ozil, Karim Ziani and Humberto Suazo

How will the USA do?

Second Round

Which team do you love to see get beat?

England, England and England. I love reading the tabloids after an England defeat. I disdain the hubris of the English press and English fans who assume an American cannot appreciate the game the way they do. We are more varied in our appreciation of the game here in the United States- we watch more leagues, and different styles of play here. I believe we know more about football than the English do. I would not make that argument about the Germans or the Italians, but simply about the English. Similarly, I don't have the same dislike of any other nation that I have for England in this sport. I don't care for Mexico either, but found myself in love with El Tri when they played England in a recent friendly. That says alot.

Chris Nee

Editor of twofootedtackle.com.

Who is your favorite national team?

England, by genetics rather than choice and proud of it.

If your team gets knocked out, who do you follow then?

I really shouldn't say this given the draw, but USA. I follow American soccer very closely and have a real fondness for USMNT. After 12th June, of course.

Who is in the final and who is going to win?

I think Brazil and Spain will reach the final but Brazil will edge it, maybe after extra time.

Who will be the surprise breakout players of the tournament?

I've been bigging up Jong of North Korea but I think their group's too tough for him to shine. I expect Alexis Sanchez to do well, but hardly a surprise. Let's say Marin and Ozul of Germany, who should make an impression between the two of them, and Teko Modise, who will be key if the South Africans are to benefit from home advantage.

How will the USA do?

Out in the second round along with England.

Which team do you love to see get beat?

Tough question. I really hope England don't have to play Portugal because they've got the sign over us in a big way. Apart from that, I think Spain being beaten would make the whole thing more interesting. I don't have any animosity towards our traditional foes, Argentina and Germany, unless we're actually playing them.

Kyle McCarthy

Boston Herald, Goal.com

Who is your favorite national team?

I've always had a soft spot for San Marino.

If your team gets knocked out, who do you follow then?

I always keep an eye on Ireland and England. Trapattoni has done a very nice job with the Irish, though he can't even find a way to game plan for a handball on the line.

Who is in the final and who is going to win?

Brazil and Spain are the two prohibitive favorites and they're located on opposite sides of the draw, so both of those teams are solid bets to reach the final. Brazil is probably the better bet with its approach, though Spain offers more pleasing fare for the neutral.

Who will be the surprise breakout players of the tournament?

It's so tough to peg breakout players when so many players are familiar faces. If you're going to find one, look to unheralded teams that could possibly make a run to the second round or the quarterfinals.

How will the USA do?

Second round, but advancing through the group won't be nearly as easy as many people have suggested.

Which team do you love to see get beat?

Sheffield United and Sheffield Wednesday, but I'm not sure Wednesday would be eligible for this prestigious tournament since the Owls are now currently resting comfortably in the third tier of English soccer.
