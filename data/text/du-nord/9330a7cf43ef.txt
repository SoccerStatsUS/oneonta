du Nord Three Stars of the Game - USA v Colombia

3. Jaime Castrillon - nice strong header for the games only goal.

2. Brad Guzan - started the game looking shaky & could do nothing about saving the one goal, but he settled down and did an awesome job saving the penalty!

1. Robinson Zapata - did one of the most masterfully idiotic jobs of time wasting I have ever been privileged to witness. Have you ever seen someone get TWO yellows for time wasting? Holy moly. And after the second one he must have stayed on the field at least another 5 minutes and wasted all that time too. Magnificent performance. Did he make a save? I don't remember. One other thing - that's a pretty good name he has.

A couple of my quick notes from the game:

-Colombia got the games only goal in the 14th minute on a header by Jaime Castrillon. The USA defense was helpless to stop it.

-Ooooof. That was one butt ugly game, wasn't it? Let's just quickly forget about it and move on.

-Herculez Gomez was really awful. Heath Pearce looked only slightly better.

-I think we all would have liked to see more of Lee Nguyen & Charlie Davies.

-Colombia are not very good, and an even slightly better USA team would have beaten them and Paraguay. Damn.
