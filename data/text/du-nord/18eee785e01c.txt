Check this garbage out: Sulley Muntari is playing for Pescara in Serie A when a bunch of Cagliari fans start racially abusing him. He tries to appease them, even gives a kid his jersey. But it just gets worse. So he goes to the referee to ask him to help make it stop. Not only does the ref not oblige but he books Muntari for hassling him. Muntari then takes things into his own hands and just walks off the field. After the game the Italian federation gets involved and not only do they do NOTHING about the Cagliari fans but they give Muntari a 1 game suspension for walking off the field. Unreal. These people who did the heckling, the ref and the federation officials are ALL horrible people who should have no place in soccer.

Now go to the Pescara website. Across the top there is a banner that reads "NO TO RACISM - RESPECT". No further explanation. But beyond that I dare you to find anything about Muntari and how he was treated. NOTHING!

UPDATE: After 24 hours of the Italian federation getting bombarded for their idiotic suspension of Muntari, they have backtracked and removed the ban. Now they need to get to work on "fans" in the stadium.
