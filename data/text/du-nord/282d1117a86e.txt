My MNUFC Notes:

Game highlights [VIDEO]

A necessary win at home over a badly weakened opponent in Colorado.

Miguel Ibarra gets his first MLS goal in dramatic fashion.

First clean sheet in MLS for the Loons is a good milestone after where they were early in the season.

Three consecutive halves without giving up a goal is also very good.

Played the lineup that I have been begging for since Day 1. (If they had not looked good and lost I think I would have had to shut up.)

________Shuttleworth________

Thiessen Kallman Calvo Burch

________Cronin Ibson________

___Molino JVenegas Ibarra___

__________Ramirez___________

Lots of improvement needed all over the field if they want to build on this success.

Got lucky with score still 0-0 and Badji hit the crossbar from 6 yards out with a relatively empty net - was harder to miss than hit crossbar.

Both teams missed a couple other good chances to score and probably should have. I would say Colorado had clearer chances.

Brent Kallman left the game with a calf contusion that should be OK. But you never know.

Word is also going around that Kallman is dealing with a hip injury that was bothering him when he came off the field too.

A big part of Ibarra's game has always been his confidence and you could see what that goal did for him. His focus went up several notches immediately following the goal.

After almost 2 years not playing much its taking time for Ibarra to re-calibrate, but its coming. I promise.

The team is desperately thin all over the field, centerback is no exception.

The attendance of 17,491 is really really disappointing. The organization needs to spend some real money advertising this team.

Two home wins in a row. They have a chance to make it three next week.
