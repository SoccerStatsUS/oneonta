Liga MX Liguilla Apertura Finals

-Huge congrats to Tigres UANL on their championship.

-Complete and utter madness in the end. Tigres had a chance to finish this thing in the 1st Leg in which they dominated winning 3-0, but it could have been 4, 5 or 6 and they didn't do it.

-I had a feeling Pumas was still alive when the 2nd Leg started, and sure enough, Pumas got the 3 goals back and then both teams scored in Added Extra Time to force a Penalty Kick Shootout.

-The American they call Gringo, Jose Torres, plays for Tigres and came on as a 61st min sub. He didn't have a very smooth game though, giving away possession cheaply and finding himself in the middle of mistakes that lead to the Pumas comeback. He tightened it up some in the extra periods and helped relieve pressure from the back line. Its his 1st Mexican championship.

-How often does a team win a game 4-1 and still lose. Wow.

-You can read all the details of the match right here. By Tom Marshall of ESPNFC.

Pumas 4-1 Tigres (0-3) - series ends tied 4-4 and Tigres win 4-2 in Penalty Kick Shootout.
