The Gaffer

EPLTalk.com

Who is your favorite national team?

Wales

If your team gets knocked out, who do you follow then?

Since Wales didn't qualify, I'm now supporting England and the United States. So my allegiances will be split on June 12th, but I'm hoping for a US win to send a message to the world and mainstream America.

Who is in the final and who is going to win?

Brazil against Spain with the South Americans winning it in extra time.

Who will be the surprise breakout players of the tournament?

I'm really hoping that Herculez Gomez gets a chance to shine for the United States. He could be a breakout player. But the footballer I'm most looking forward to seeing break out is Jesus Navas for Spain. Again, if he gets a chance to play, he'll be able to amaze viewers with his dribbling skills and clinical finishes in front of goal.

How will the USA do?

I keep on going back and forth on them. Some days I wonder if they'll make it out of Group C. Other days, I think they'll go far. My problem with the US squad is that they're a Jekyll & Hyde side. When I think they'll do well, they play with no passion. When I think they'll get clobbered, they step up their game and completely stun me. I'm predicting they'll make it out of Group C but will exit in the round of 16.

Which team do you love to see get beat?

I'd love to see France get beat for the way they cheated their way into the World Cup. A thrashing from Mexico, Uruguay or South Africa would be lovely. Come on karma!

Richard Allen

Crave Cottage Newsround

Who is your favorite national team?

England, but part of me will be cheering on Ghana, Chile, the US and probably someone else I haven’t thought about yet. North Korea even: there’s so little mystery in modern football isn’t there?

If your team gets knocked out, who do you follow then?

See above, although these teams will probably be out too. Who knows….

Who is in the final and who is going to win?

Cameroon v Brazil, with Brazil to prevail.

Who will be the surprise breakout players of the tournament?

Part of the fun of the world cup is when people you didn’t know about surprise you. I remember a Russian fellow scoring five goals in a game in the nineties. That was terrific. So here’s to the unknown player who doesn’t know what a hero he’s about to become.

How will the USA do?

Surprisingly well. England’s weaknesses and the US strengths (and vice-versa) are intriguingly aligned, which could lead to a more open game than people are expecting. Then in the knockouts anything can happen, so a semi-final isn’t beyond the realms of possibility.

Which team do you love to see get beat?

Portugal are the only team that stand out this year. I don’t dislike Holland as a team, but Arjen Robben winds me up. So his removal somehow would be pleasing.

Greg Seltzer

Mouthy scribe - MLSsoccer.com/Soccer 365/No Short Corner

Who is your favorite national team?

USA, but I also live 'n die with the Dutch

If your team gets knocked out, who do you follow then?

See above.

Who is in the final and who is going to win?

Injuries have changed things and I need to re-do my brackets. That being said, right now, I can say I think either Brazil or the Netherlands will make the final from one half. Depending on a couple of injury situations, Spain is likely my other half pick. My sleeper choices are Paraguay, Serbia and Uruguay.

Who will be the surprise breakout players of the tournament?

Several guys could make names, Japan's Keisuke Honda, Paraguay's Lucas Barrios, Serbia's Milos Krasic, Brazil's Michel Bastos, Spain's Juan Mata. And of course, let's not forget, possibly someone like Edson Buddle, Herc Gomez or Stuart Holden.

How will the USA do?

Advancement from the group seems well within reach, even with the injuries. From there, the round of 16 match should be very tough. That's probably where it ends. A famous win there could see them meet up with Mexico to make the semis. I doubt they can win it, but they could put in a good scare.

Which team do you love to see get beat?

Mexico!!

Amos Magee

F-I-F

Director Soccer Development, Coach - Portland Timbers

Who is your favorite national team?

US nats are clearly my favorite. However, I will always support the French National team as I spent three weeks of the 1982 world cup in France. The French national team of Platini, Tigana, Tresor, Giresse etc. helped make me a soccer fan and I have loved and supported them ever since. I am still not over the loss to W Germany in the 82 semifinal. I was also at Parc des Princes when Bulgaria upset France and knocked them out of qualifying for the 1994 world cup. Being among all those crying French fans solidified them as my team. The pathos was appealing. Suffice to say 1998 was my favorite world cup since 1982.

If your team gets knocked out, who do you follow then?

See Above.

Who is in the final and who is going to win?

It is tough to pick against Spain or Brazil. But what fun would that be? I am picking the Dutch in a surprise. They beat Brazil in the final.

Who will be the surprise breakout players of the tournament?

I think that Wesley Sneijder will have a fantastic world cup. He was the engine that made Inter go in the attacking 3rd. I think Carlos Vela will have a great world cup. Fresh and fit with a lot to prove. This could be his coming out party. Youan Gourcuff will pull the strings for France and almost make French fans forget about Zidane. Almost. And I think Landon Donovan establishes himself as the first bonafide American superstar at this tourney.

How will the USA do?

I think the US gets into the quarter final. Loses in extratime.

Which team do you love to see get beat?

I root against the Germans. Refer above to 1982 and that cheat Schumacher. I will root against Argentina this tourney as I can't forgive Maradona for not taking Cambiasso and Zanetti. I'm also comfortable w him failing as well. I actually root for Mexico in the world cup as their success helps CONCACAF.
