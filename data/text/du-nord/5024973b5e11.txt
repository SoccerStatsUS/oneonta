COLORADO RAPIDS

Colorado had to make all three subs due to injury, and two were defenders. which is terrible news for the second leg because none of them will be ready to go on Wednesday.

-Fullback Kosuke Kimura suffered a broken left foot. He will be out for 4-6 weeks.

-Centerback Drew Moor sprained the AC joint in his right shoulder. He will be out 2-4 weeks.

-Forward Caleb Folan sprained his left ankle and left knee. He will be out 2 weeks.

And substitute centerback Tyrone Marshall who came in for Moor got a red card and will miss Wednesday's game as well.
