Come join in a very special Free Beer Movement night on July 10th with Minneapolis amateur club Stegman's Old Boys.

The purpose of the event is to raise money to make soccer accessible for Somali immigrant youths that gets them off the street, playing soccer and learning non-violent conflict resolution. They also stay in shape. It's a great program that helps Twin Cities kids and your attendance and support, via the raffle, a donation or just coming out to show that it matters to you, can make all the

difference.
