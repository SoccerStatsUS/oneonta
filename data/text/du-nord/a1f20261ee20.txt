ALL AROUND THE WORLD

Reuters reports that FIFA has lifted the age limit on the rule that allows players who have been capped by youth national teams to switch to another country. Right now you can't switch after you reach 21, the new rule has no age limit. BUT the old rule is still in place that once you are capped by the senior team then you are tied to that country.
