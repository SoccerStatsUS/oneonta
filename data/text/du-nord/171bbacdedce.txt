CONCACAF

The Trinidad Express reports that the T&T football federation has now missed their 2nd deadline to pay their players from the 2006 World Cup who successfully sue the fed for money owed them from the tournament.

The Express also writes that those players are still not getting anywhere with their requests for an investigation into the finances of the T&T football fed and former leader Jack Warner who made the initial deal with the players.

-Its is estimated that the fed earned $40 Million from the World Cup and players were supposed to get 50% of the profits. After the tournament ended and the accounting was done the 23 players were offered less than $6000 each or $138,000 total!

FIFA has abandon the plan to have teams based regionally, schedule shows them going all over the country for 3 group stage games. Brutal on any fan trying to follow their team.
