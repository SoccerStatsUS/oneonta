So, the Fed Ex guy walked into my office with a fairly heavy package for me.

Halliburton?!?! Was Dick Cheney finally sending his goons after me?

I opened the box and pulled out the goods. Then undid the tape and took off the bubble wrap.

There was a manila envelope on top of a heavy duty silver attache case!

The envelope was then opened, and a letter inside gave me instruction. I would get a combination sent to me at 10am on Thursday morning via email, because the briefcase was locked!

I checked out the combination on the lock and noticed it was pre-set at 666. Only someone with a Cheney sense of humor does that. Sadly it was not my code to open it.

The case was very nice, and engraved with the US Soccer logo and the Nike logo. But I would have to wait.

Finally the email arrived with the secret code to open the case, and what was inside was very exciting - the brand new white USA shirt especially designed for the World Cup! Oh yes.

Sitting on top of that was a flash drive.

And then underneath the white shirt, was none other than the blue 2nd kit that was introduced to us in London 2 months ago! Wow.

So there we have it, an amazing package from Nike to show off an amazing new shirt.

I know US Soccer is already selling them, and I would bet that your fave shop will have them soon if not already. Here we go!

PS. Hey Cheney, you didn't get me!
