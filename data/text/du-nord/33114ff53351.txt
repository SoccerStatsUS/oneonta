The big news from this weekend's international matches - friendly's, Euro & Africa qualifiers:

-An awful scene went down in Zambia on Saturday after the home side beat Congo 3-0 in an African Nations Cup qualifier. For some reason hundreds of fans tried to get IN to the stadium AFTER the match ended which led to a stampede that killed 12 people and injured 46 more. What the fuck? An investigation is under way. A local newspaper called the Zambia Post who claims to be "The Paper That Digs Deeper" only has a small article on the event, and its basically a comment from the coach saying how sad he is. That's it? The Times Of Zambia with a few gruesome details. The worst details says there was only one gate open in the whole place.

-Sweden took an amazing 0-3 half time lead at Denmark, in a Euro qualifiers. But the Danes roared back to tie it at 3's in the 75th minute. Then all hell broke loose 13 minutes later as the referee called a red card and penalty for Sweden when a Dane punched a Swede in the penalty box. Yes punched. Search YouTube, its there. After that a fan got on the field and tried to punch the referee. The match was then abandon as two more goons ran on the pitch, and will be investigated by UEFA. Sweden could be given a forfeit victory. Danish players did a great job of keeping the fan from hitting the referee and then wrestling the idiot away. One Danish newspaper called the guy, "a fool without self control." That is putting it mildly. Reuters has quotes from "the most hated man in Denmark." The dude says he drank at least 15 beers.

-Gabriel Badilla & Bryan Ruiz scored for Costa Rica as they beat Chile 2-0 in San Jose CR in a Gold Cup warm up match.

-Mexico destroyed Iran 4-0 in their first of two Gold Cup previews. Jared Borgetti, Jaime Lozano, Francisco Fonseca & Gerardo Torrado all scored.

-Pippo Inzaghi stayed smokin hot and scored 2 more, this time for Italy as they squeaked past the Faroe Islands 1-2 on the road.

-David Beckham returns for England and sets up a goal, but Brazil equalizes in stoppage time.

-Dwayne De Rosario scored for Canada in a 2-2 tie down in Venezuela.

-New coach Carlos Alberto Parreira led South Africa to a 4-0 African Nations Cup qualifier over Chad. Maybe they can rebuild this team to be respectable for the World Cup.

-The little known German striker with the Hispanic name, Mario Gomez, scored 2 for his country as they pummeled San Marino 6-0. The score was only 1-0 at half time.

-Argentina were held to a 1-1 draw v Switzerland. Carlos Tevez scored for the Argies.
