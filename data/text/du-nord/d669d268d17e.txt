Several big changes coming to the Laws of Football as approved this week by the International Football Association Board. By Bobby McMahon of Forbes.

No more "triple" punishment for "denying an obvious goal scoring opportunity" - Was automatic red card, now yellow unless violent.

No more stutter-step Penalty Kicks. Punishable by Yellow Card and loss of PK.

Keeper coming off the line during PK is punishable by Yellow Card.

Free kicks for offside will take place where the pass was made from, not where the player was offside.

And many more including tests of some limited video replay for referees.

No idea when these will be put in to motion.
