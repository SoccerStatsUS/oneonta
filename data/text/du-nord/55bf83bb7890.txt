AFRICA WORLD CUP QUALIFYING

The last round of qualifiers:

-Sat Nov 14

Group A

Togo v Gabon - Gabon (9 pts & +3 gd) win and have Cam lose or tie and win goal difference

Morocco v Cameroon - Cameroon (10 pts & +5 gd) win, Gab loss, or win and Gab tie, or lose and Gab tie and win goal difference

Group B

Mozambique v Tunisia - Tunisia (11 pts & +4 gd) is in with a win or tie and Nig lose

Kenya v Nigeria - Nigeria (9 pts & +4 gd) must win and have Tun lose or win, Tun tie, and win goal difference

Group C

Egypt v Algeria - both (Alg 13 pts & +7 gd/Egy 10 pts & +3 gd) Algeria wins, or Egypt wins and wins goal differential while Alg loses.

Goal Differential is the first tie breaker.

Ghana (Group D) & Core d'Ivoire (Group E) have already captured 2 of the 5 available spots.
