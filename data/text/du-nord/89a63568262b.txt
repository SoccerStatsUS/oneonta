Who is your favorite national team?

Chivas. Oh, NATIONAL -- I thought you meant nationalistic.

All due disclaimers of journalistic impartiality aside, your national team chooses you. And if you want the game to thrive in your country, it's hard to say you want your national team to lose.

If your team gets knocked out, who do you follow then?

The one with the best story. Any African team that reaches the second round is a great story.

Who is in the final and who is going to win?

Spain over Brazil

Who will be the surprise breakout players of the tournament?

Wow -- I have no idea. The surprises will be people no one has heard of. If I've heard of him, he's not a surprise.

That said, I'm going to say Kanu turns back the clock for Nigeria after getting a good rest through much of the EPL season. Jong Tae-Se, the Wayne Rooney of North Korea (or Japan, really), may sneak in a goal or two in the Group of Death.

How will the USA do?

50% chance of getting out of the group stage, 15% chance of getting to quarterfinals.

Which team do you love to see get beat?

Italy. Every time they lose, Brian McBride's face heals a little.
