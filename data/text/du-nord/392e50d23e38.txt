MLS

That was one hell of a season ending day on Sunday. Wow. Drama everywhere:

Toronto tied Atlanta and set a new record for most points in a season at 69.

San Jose grabbed the 6th and final spot in the West with a game winner in 2nd half stoppage time.

Dallas + RSL were also chasing SJE but their wins were not enough to jump over the Quakes.

Portland beat Vancouver and leaped to the top of the West and a #1 seed.

Vancouver's loss saw them fall from #1 to #3.

Portland also claimed the Cascadia Cup, over Vancouver and Seattle.

Minnesota set an ignominious record by allowing 70 goals, a new league record.

Clint Dempsey got a red card against Colorado and I assume will miss the first playoff game.

More people in the stands in Atlanta throwing cups of beer at a player on the field. Last time it hit a ref. This time it hit no one but Giovinco picked it up and had a taste. Another dumbass in the stands.

Another MLS attendance record was set in Atlanta just weeks after they had set a new top number - 71,874 were in attendance on Sunday.

Toronto is the favorite for MLS Cup. By far. By Jeff Carlisle for ESPNFC.

MINNESOTA UNITED

Results:

-Sun Oct 22

San Jose Earthquakes 3-2 Minnesota

The improvements that Minnesota made throughout the season. By Jeff Rueter for ESPNFC.

My MNUFC Notes:

Game highlights [VIDEO]

Loons fought hard but could not keep the Quakes from their destiny of making the playoffs for the 1st time in 6 years. San Jose had to win and they did what ever it took to make that happen.

Calvo made a big mistake whiffing on a clearance allowing the 1st SJE goal.

Calvo then made up for that error by clearing a sure goal off the line.

Calvo further upped his day by scoring the tying goal at 2-2 on a looping header off a corner. Great job redeeming himself.

Thiesson scored a sweeping long range left footer to tie the game at 1s.

Thiesson later fell asleep at the back post allowing SJE to take a 2-1 lead. He has done this too many times in 2017. His weakness.

Taylor got the start at left fullback over Burch and kept that side of the field quiet until he got hurt late. SJE swarmed Burch when he came on and then scored the game winner from that side of the field with Burch left out to dry by his teammates.

When SJE used their last sub on Marco Urena I was having flashbacks to USA v Costa Rica when Urena killed the Yanks. He did the same to MNUFC.

Chris Wondowlowski willed his team to the playoffs. Not just scoring their 2nd goal but showing tons of leadership and never say die spirit. He is also just 11 goals away from Landon Donovan's all time MLS record. Unless he has a career ending injury I don't see him missing out on being the new #1.

Good game: Calvo

OK game: Shuttleworth, Thiesson, Boxall, Taylor, Finlay, Ibson, Warner, Nicholson, JVenegas, Danladi

Bad game: Molino, Ramirez, Burch

That's all folks. See you next year for game analysis.
