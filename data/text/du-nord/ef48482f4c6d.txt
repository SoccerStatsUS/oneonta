Liga MX Liguilla Apertura:

-Two lower seeded teams advanced, but neither can really be seen as a upset.

Quarterfinals aggregate scores:

America(6) 5-3 Leon(3)

Tigres(5) 3-1 Chiapas(4)

Pumas(1) 1-1 Veracruz(8) - Pumas advances as higher seed

Toluca(2) 3-2 Puebla(7)

-William Yarborough played the full 90 for Leon in Leg 2, which was a 2-1 win. Miguel Ibarra, Ventura Alvarado, Joe Corona, Gabe Farfan and Jose Torres did not play in the 2nd Leg games.

Semifinals

-Dec 2+3 and 5+6

Pumas(1) v America(6)

Toluca(2) v Tigres(5)

-Final: Dec 10 + 13

-All rounds are 2 Legs.

-In Semifinals away goals break ties, no extra time is played, and if still tied they higher seed advances.

-In Final away goals DO NOT break ties, and the game can go to Extra Added Time and a Penalty Kick Shootout if tied.
