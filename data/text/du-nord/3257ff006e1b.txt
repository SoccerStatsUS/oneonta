My notes from the game:

-After last nights game I don't think there is any doubt left as to who is the best team in the league right now. New England is very impressive!

-Is there any doubt that Kheli Dube has vaulted to the top of the Rookie of the Year race? Nope.

-I like it when a referee gives an erroneous Penalty and the keeper saves it. Justice served. Houston forward Franco Caraccio was clearly fouled outside the box, but the ref gave a PK, and Matt Reis stuffed Stuart Holden.

-It was good to see Houston backup keeper Tony Caig has continued with his early season reputation and given up another horrible goal. We here at du Nord coined the term "Caiged" for just such blunders. See video of the 2nd New Eng goal for a perfect example.
