TEAM USA - EL EQUIPO DE TODOS

World Cup Qualifying

The USA has their toughest road game of this group stage as they travel to Guatemala for a late game on Tuesday.

The USA will wear their 2nd jersey - navy blue with white sleeves, black sash and white numbers.

The USA has two problems they must overcome to have success on the road: 1) Who plays at leftback with injuries to all 3 players who have held the starting position in the past 3 games, and 2) Can a head coach with so little experience in his job (at any level, but especially at the qualifying level where this will be his second game ever) have his team ready to compete in Central America, a notoriously ugly venture for any team.

Guatemala will be desperate to claim 3 points after losing their first game at Jamaica on Friday.

Guatemala will also bunker in deep and look to hit the USA on the counter attack with some very fast players.

But the USA has not lost to Guatemala since 1988 going 12-0-5 since then. And the USA holds a 6-0-4 record in all World Cup qualifying games between the two.

Expect another slow and soggy field in Guatemala as it has been raining there and the forecast is for more during the game.

The 2 Guatemalan players most Americans will be familiar with are Carlos Ruiz who once starred for the LA Galaxy, is genuinely hated by all opponents for his antics, and currently plays in the Mexican 2nd division, and Marco Pappa who is a starting midfielder for the Chicago Fire.

-Pappa recently appeared in a big time Pepsi commercial with world superstars!
