MLS TEAM & PLAYER NEWS

Punishment

I think MLS needs to crack down on players abuse of referees, and to that effect Brek Shea gets to feel the brunt of it with a 3 game suspension and a fine after kicking a ball at and hitting an assistant! How was he not instantly ejected for that move??

Portland centerback Futty Danso gets a 1 game suspension for clubbing Houston's Calen Carr in the head during a breakaway. And Carr wears protective headgear due to previous concussions. I think Danso got off light.
