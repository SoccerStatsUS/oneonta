SOUTH AFRICA 2010 WORLD CUP

From a reader named Greg comes some interesting stats from the tourney:

I was looking at FIFA's WC stats and thought "looking at total fouls by team doesn't tell you the whole story." And sure enough, if you adjust for the number of games played, including extra time, a certain regional rival to the south was the, um, most aggressive team at WC 2010, committing 20.8 fouls per game! Netherlands was #13 -- fouling 15% less than El Tri -- and the other semifinalists were well below the median. The US, too, was low -- only committing an adjusted 12.2 fouls / game.

You can see all the numbers here. I took FIFA's numbers and added 1/3 of a game for teams that went to extra time.
