I know its controversial, and some of you will despise me for writing this, but these are the reasons to become a US Soccer member:

"As a benefit of being a member of the US Soccer Supporters Club, we are pleased to extend an invitation from US Soccer President Sunil Gulati to attend the first-ever US Soccer Fan Forum.

This is an opportunity for you to ask Sunil questions about US Soccer and get his thoughts on any topic our most loyal fans want to discuss. Sunil is looking to have an open and engaging conversation, so be sure to come prepared with questions.

The US Soccer Fan Forum will be held on Jan 22 at the Home Depot Center in Carson CA, prior to the USA match against Chile. The event will start two to three hours before kickoff. The exact start time is TBD.

This unique opportunity is available to a limited number of US Soccer SC members who have purchased tickets to this match, so submit your RSVP today to reserve your spot. Each US Soccer SC member that is selected for this experience is welcome to bring one guest who must also be a ticket-holder for the match.

Go here for more info."

NOTE: US Soccer needs to do a LOT more of this in the future if they want this program to actually work, and the fact that program is nearly to its third year and this is the first event of this kind if very disappointing. But lets hope its a sign of real action to come and not just a one off.
