My notes from the games last night:

Revs v Tuzos

-Apparently Pachuca goal scoring machine Bruno Marioni had an injury and that is why we did not see him play last night.

-New England midfielder Pat Phelan was knocked out cold when he collided heads with teammate Sainey Nyassi going for a header. I looked really bad watching on TV as he was stretchered off the field in a neck brace, but he is going to be OK, suffering a concussion.

-Is Pachuca midfielder/forward Damian Alvarez good or what? I would take that guy on my team in a second.

-Old Sven Goren Erickson was in Gillette again last night watching his potential charges for Pachuca.

-Youngster Chris Tierney was thrown into the fire last night, getting the start at left fullback for the Rev's. He did a fine job. As did Amaechi Igwe starting at right fullback.

Goats USA v Guerreros

-When was the last time you were this impressed with the play of Carey Talley? Wow. The start of this season he really struggled in Salt Lake, but the past few weeks he has been fantastic.

-I was amazed at how young and inexperienced a team Chivas USA put on the field against Santos. But they held it together long enough for Chivas to sub in the veterans and snag the winner.

-Sacha Kljestan was great last night. He was everywhere, running, passing, defending. He lead that team with style. And his cross for Ante Razov to glance to the back post and score the games only goal was golden.

-Surprising to see Roberto Nurse in the first 11 for CUSA. They signed him Tuesday when the window opened, and he makes his debut a day later. Didn't show much, but he tried hard.

-Still trying to figure out how Santos didn't get at least one goal. They had what seemed like handfuls of chances.

-Jonathan Bornstein & Paulo Nagamura will miss the 3rd game due to Yellow Card accumulation.

US Soccer Youth Development Academy

The first ever U18 & U16 national finals for the new Development Academy will be take place starting tomorrow, Sat Jul 12, and ending on Sat Jul 19.

All matches will be played at the Home Depot Center complex in Carson, CA.

The tournament is set up in two groups per age level and a round-robin tournament will determine #1-#4 in each group. Then those teams will then play the corresponding team in the other group for 1st, 3rd, 5th & 7th place trophies.

The last days U18 games will all be broadcast live on ESPNU, with the Championship (1 v 1) being shown live on ESPN2 at 10pm central on Fri Jul 18.

All 4 U16 final day games will be shown Sat Jul 19 on ESPNU.
