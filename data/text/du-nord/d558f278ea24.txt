Ken the Keeper is a big fan of Fiorentina in Italy, and one of the Czech Republic's best & least known players is on Fiore, so I asked Ken to fill us in on Czech defender Tomas Ujfalusi. Here is what he gives us:

If you live in Italy no doubt Tomas Ujfalusi is one of the best known Czechs in the country after Pavel Nedved. The tall 28 year old came to Fiorentina from Hamburg in January of '04 as La Viola were making their ascent back to Serie A prominence, and he has started nearly every game since. Typically a center back, Ujfalusi has often been filling in at defensive wing as Fiore have been weak in those positions. With 28 caps and 2 goals for the Czech Republic, Ujfalusi has played a key role in the international rise of his country. Ujfalusi played every game of Euro 2004 when they beat the likes of Denmark, Germany and Holland, before going down to the Greeks in the semifinals.
