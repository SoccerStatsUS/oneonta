USA beats Poland 1-0 in Kaiserslautern Germany at the Fritz-Walder Stadion! Deuce Dempsey from Nacogdoches with the diving header goal in the 48th minute!

I found the first half to be a bit frustrating and at times nerve wracking. Poland had the better chances by far, but the USA held on by their finger tips. Our boys just seemed scattered and out of focus, especially in their defensive tactics. And I mean the whole teams defense, not the back 4 - although they were quite imperfect. At half time I had a quick chat with the excellent Spencer, who was out of school due to an "appointment", and I said, "I think the team will settle down and play much better in the second half." Which is exactly what they did. The passing was better, the positioning firmer, and the pace tempered. It worked. The team has enough talent on the field to strike at any time from any where. Eddie Lewis & Steve Cherundolo are such a potent force from left & right back, and they hit those crosses so gorgeously. Lewis put the long pass into the box that led to the goal. Taylor Twellman and Clint Dempsey just did their thing to make it happen. Twellman showed his fearlessness in going straight at the Polish keeper Boruc who cleared the ball off Twellman's head and it fell straight to the garbage hauler Dempsey who always seems to be sitting in that spot looking for trash to pick up. This is how these guys play. Add the speed of Donovan, Wolfe, Beasley etc flying down the field, and this whole thing looks exciting to me.

Here now are the perfunctory du Nord 3 stars of the game:

3 Oguchi Onyewu - a monster in the back who ground the Poles down to dust, or was that flakes

2 Clint Demspey - the game winner on a classic scrappy stick-yer-nose-in diving header, plus he worked very hard helping on defense

1 Kasey Keller - I know I will hear about this one, and that he only played the first half, but with out his great saves, the second half win would not have been possible
