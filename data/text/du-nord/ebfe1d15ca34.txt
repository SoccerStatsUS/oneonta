A very thin Minnesota United roster gets thinner: MLS disciplinary committee has retroactively suspended Minnesota's Sam Cronin for 1 game - Sat Jun 3 at SKC - for a pretty brutal foul on Orlando's PC. [VIDEO]

When the play happened Cronin was "aghast" that he got a yellow card. But the referee was standing right there looking directly at the play. On replay its an obvious red card.

Someone will inevitably try to argue that Cronin "got the ball first." He sure did, and then his momentum took the bottom of his cleat right in to PC's planted ankle. Lucky its not broken.

Three very good reflections on how MNUFC could get better as unveiled by the game against Orlando last Saturday. By Alex Schieferdecker for Fifty Five One.

USMNT

USMNT centerback John Brooks has been sold by his hometown team Hertha Berlin to Wolfsburg, who barely survived relegation, for a reported $22,000,000. A record fee for an American.

Clint Dempsey, the kid from the trailer in Nacogdoches is one goal away from tying Landon Donovan as the USMNT all-time leading goal scorer. By Matt Pentz for the New York Times.

Bobby Wood is anxious to get back on the field for the USA, and hopes his injuries are behind him. From Associated Press, via Salt Lake Tribune.

Tim Howard has been named the starter for next weeks WC qualifiers.
