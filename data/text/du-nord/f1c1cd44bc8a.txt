My pick for league MVP Mauro Diaz of Dallas had the best weekend of any player until Mike Magee came on at halftime for LA Galaxy in the last game of the day. (Ignacio Piatti of Montreal had a pretty great weekend too.)

-Here is what Magic Mike did in 45 minutes:

Set up the 1st goal with his corner kick

Scored the 2nd goal by chipping the keeper

Drew a foul in the box for the 3rd goal

Scored the 4th goal with a flashing header

-I would say Magee's return to LA was a success! A 4-1 win!
