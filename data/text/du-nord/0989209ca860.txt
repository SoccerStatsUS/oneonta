My notes on RSL-NYRB:

-While the event was very memorable, the match was not. Frantic is the one word I would use to describe it. Neither team settled down and make cool crisp passing a part of their game. Sure there were moments, but over all it was summed up with Juan Pietravallo getting his foot up high as Clint Mathis ducked his head low and the two forces meeting, leaving Cleetus with a busted open head (took 12 staples to close, using a stapler from someone in the teams sales departments cubical) and Pietravallo with a Red Card.

-You could hear that damn bag piper clearly throughout the whole game - I would have brained that guy after a little while.

-Was I the only one who thought Dema Kovalenko was going to hurt someone as soon as he got on the field?

-Dave van den Bergh has been fantastic this season for NY!

-It looked fairly chilly there last night, so instead of sitting politely with blankets on why didn't those people get up and jump around? They would have had a blast. Instead they had "fun".

-Both teams needed 3 points and got 1. It's better than 0.

-The stadium was not as unique looking as I had hoped. It was a cross between Home Depot Center and Toyota Park. But not being there, I don't know about the details.

-Macoumba Kandji got his first Red Bull start. He had a couple good moves, and couple good crosses. Everything else, eh. BUUUUUUUT, he is a forward, not a winger, so what do you expect.
