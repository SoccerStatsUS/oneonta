STORY OF THE DAY

After all the teeth gnashing 1 year ago in Turkey over alleged match fixing, the court cases have now been settled, and the answers are a resounding "Nothing Wrong Here!"

All 16 teams charged with match fixing were cleared on any wrong doing, and the few people convicted of any crimes, like Fenerbahce president Aziz Yildirim, were released for time served.

In other words, nothing will change, and its back to business as usual for whoever can afford to pay to win matches.
