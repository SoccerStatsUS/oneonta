My friend Andy sent me this email yesterday: I just asked Miguel, my Mexican friend, if Cuauhtemoc Blanco has a nickname, and hid reply was - "The joke is that Blanco does not have a neck and it looks like he has a little backpack ("mochilita") under his shirt. Something like a hunchback." So he is called "la Mochilita" for "the little backpack." That is glorious fun!
