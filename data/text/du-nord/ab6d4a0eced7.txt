Episode 16 of the du Nord Futbol Show podcast is here.

dN

In this episode we bring you another road trip special. Again we head to Kansas City, this time for World Cup Qualifying - USA v Guatemala - where the home side needs at least a tie to advance to the Hexagon round. We also talk about the future of Livestrong, and our beloved Minnesota Stars racing into their second consecutive NASL Final! To top it all off we recorded some incredible atmosphere from inside the stadium - it will make the hair on your arms stand up!

dN

Your hosts Bruce McGuire and Wes Burdine hope you enjoy it, and look forward to your questions and comments.
