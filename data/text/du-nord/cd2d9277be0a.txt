All hail the 2006/07 Champion Stuttgart!

Going into today, the last day of the German season, Stuttgart had a 2 point lead over second place Schalke. But Schalke's match result was made moot as Stuttgart was victorious over Energie Cottbus 2-1. Schalke did win to keep them in 2nd place, 2-1 over Arminia Bielefeld. Werder Bremen, who finished 3rd, won 0-2 at Wolfsburg. Stuttgart miraculously won 8 games in a row to end the season to pull off the title!
