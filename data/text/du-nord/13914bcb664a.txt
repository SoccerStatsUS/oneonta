Game Action:

Fulham and St Patrick's Athletic advance, while Valarenga and Rijeka are knocked out of the Europa League.

UEFA Europa League - 3rd qualifying round - Second Leg

-Thu Aug 6

Clint Dempsey - Fulham - played the full 90 in a 3-0 home win over Vetra and advance 6-0 on aggregate.

Ryan Guy - St Patrick's Athletic - went the full 90 and lost 2-3 at Krylya Sovetov Samara leaving series tied 3-3 on aggregate and St.PA win on the away goals tie breaker.

Eddie Johnson - Fulham - came on as a sub in the 82nd minute of a 3-0 home win over Vetra and advance 6-0 on aggregate.

Troy Perkins - Valerenga - went the distance and won 0-1 at PAOK and the series ends tied 2-2, but PAOK advance on the away goals tie breaker.

Johann Smith - Rijeka - started and played the first half as they lost 2-0 at Metalist Kharkiv and were knocked out of the tournament 4-1 on aggregate.
