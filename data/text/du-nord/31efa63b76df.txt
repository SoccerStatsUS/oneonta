My MLS Notes:

Vancouver was my pick to win MLS Cup this year, I know I know, and they are in a total tailspin. Actually the season has been out of whack since the start. 5 in a row now without a win and 3 losses in a row. Not to mention in 8th place with 9 games to play. The job of head coach Carl Robinson has to be in jeopardy.

In the Dallas v KC match all 4 goals were scored between minutes 66 and 76.

Nigel De Jong has become such a menace on the field that this week he crushed his own goal keeper, recklessly going after a ball and kneeing Brian Perk in the head, busting Perk wide open and probably concussing him. Get him outta here.

Huge road win for Philly, 0-4 at the Revs, with Bedoya getting his first start. Up, up and away?

New England is really struggling in the league, nearing Columbus and Chicago's level.

NYC scored on a PK two minutes into stoppage time and thought they had beat Columbus, but the Crew scored 2 minutes later to claw back a tie.

Bradley Wright Phillips scored 2 more goals for RBNY who are getting hot at the right time.

The Houston v Toronto game was delayed 24 hours due to lighting. It ended in a 1-1 tie.

Frank Lampard has scored 9 goals in 10 games. Remember earlier this summer when he was terrible?!?!
