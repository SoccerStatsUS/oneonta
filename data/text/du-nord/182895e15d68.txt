Nike and Bumpy Pitch have collaborated on a limited edition tee collection celebrating the rivalry between the United States and Mexico.

With the United States hosting the CONCACAF Gold Cup this month, it creates the perfect opportunity to explore the rich rivalry between these two nations.

The graphics are inspired by the drive to not only dominate your rival, but it pays homage to the fact that our greatest rivals make us better.

Both shirts are available starting Thu Jun 9 at Bumpy Pitch, and at select Niketown stores and soccer stores in the USA.
