It's Day 113 of 2016.

He sang like Smokie Robinson. He played guitar like Jimi Hendrix. He danced like James Brown. He wrote songs like no one else.

RIP Prince.

Last night was pretty overwhelming in Minneapolis. The love for Prince poured out of every inch of the city. It was hard to get to sleep at all. I eventually took a late night drive to see the lights. My emotions ran high, especially the celebration of his music.

If you want to see some of the visual highlights go scroll through my Twitter timeline from Thursday night and Friday morning.
