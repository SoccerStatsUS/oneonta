One of the greats who made Jamaican ska music famous and pushed it into the English mainstream (where I always heard it referred to as bluebeat) was Prince Buster - who passed away on Thursday. By Peter Mason of the Guardian.

The band Madness basically owe their entire career to Buster. They named their band after one his songs ("Madness"), their first hit was about him ("The Prince"), and one their biggest hits was a cover of one of his songs ("One Step Beyond".)

The Guardian lists 5 of Prince Buster's greatest songs.
