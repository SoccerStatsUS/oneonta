VIDEO HIGHLIGHTS

Sanna Nyassi had one of those games you dream about for Colorado, bagging three goals and had another gorgeous shot go off the post which a teammate converted into the other goal. He got the start at forward in place of Conor Casey, and his speed tore New York to shreds. The first goal he scored was off a lightning fast give and go with Omar Cummings that takes your breath away it happen so fast. The his blast off the post for Wells Thompson to score. The third goal was a blazing-weaving run through the Red Bulls defense and hammered finish. And to finish off the hattrick - a little shuffle step past a defender, a burst of sonic speed and a cool low touch past the keeper. The game of a lifetime for one of the Nyassi twins!
