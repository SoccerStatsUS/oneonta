Freddy Adu is all but gone. To Portugal. The deal is nearly done and he is already at Benfica, and I would assume he will start training with his new team right away. Benfica finished third in the league last year, but only 3 points away from winners Porto. The club is best known for legendary 60's & 70's striker Eusebio. This is exactly the type of club Freddy needed to go to, not some giant club where he would be lost in the shuffle.
