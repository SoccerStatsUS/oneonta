Kurt Cobain committed suicide 22 years ago today, in 1994. The last days of his life were not pretty. By Neil Strauss of Rolling Stone. (Article originally published Summer 1994)

I remember where I was when I heard the news on April 8th. (Little did we know he had already been dead 3 days by that time.) I was at home packing a bag when the phone rang with the bad news. I had been putting things together to take a band to perform on Radio K in Minneapolis. To say things got weird is an understatement. I don't remember if the band performed live-on-air or not. I also don't remember if any of us got anything done that day. It was shocking.
